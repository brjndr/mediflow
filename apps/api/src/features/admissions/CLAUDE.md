# Admissions (ADT) API

What was decided for this feature's API (BE-23, #156). Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. The beds themselves (wards, rooms, categories, blocking) are `features/wards`; read its `CLAUDE.md` too.

- **`features/admissions`** admits a patient, gives a bed and moves the patient to another, discharges, keeps the bed history and serves the bed board. Behind the `admissions` module flag; anything that names a bed also needs the `wards` flag (403 `feature_disabled`), an admission without a bed does not. Permissions: `admission:read`, `admission:create`, `admission:transfer`, `admission:discharge`, `admission:cancel`, `bed:update` (housekeeping). Every mutating route also needs `admission:read`, because the answer names the patient.
- **An admission has its own inpatient encounter** (`type = 'ipd'`), opened with it through `openInpatientEncounter` and closed at discharge through `closeInpatientEncounter` (both exported by `features/clinical`; this module never writes `encounters`). Discharge is refused while a note of the encounter is a draft (409 `unsigned_notes`).
- **State:** `admitted -> discharged` or `admitted -> cancelled`, both final (a trigger). A patient has at most one admission not discharged (a partial unique index, 409 `already_admitted`). The admission's `version` also counts a change of bed (the trigger lets an admitted row be touched), so a stale transfer is 409 `conflict`.
- **A bed holds one patient at a time** by two layers. In code, every change of a bed takes the ward's lock first (`lockWard`, the order the wards notes ask for), reads the bed again and moves it from the status it is in (`moveBed`); a transfer locks the old ward and the new one in id order, after the admission row, so two transfers never deadlock. Underneath, `bed_assignments` has exclusion constraints on the time each assignment lasts (`tstzrange(assigned_at, released_at)`): one open assignment per bed and per admission, so even code that forgot the lock cannot double-book (23P01 is read as 409 `bed_not_available`). Lock order everywhere: the admission, then the ward(s), then beds.
- **Bed status:** a patient given the bed sets it `occupied`; a patient leaving (transfer or discharge) sets it `cleaning`; `POST /beds/{bedId}/housekeeping` moves `available <-> cleaning` (`bed:update`, checked against the bed's `version`). Blocking and unblocking stay in the wards API, which now also announces on the board.
- **The assignment keeps the bed's category and its bed-day service** as they were when the patient was given the bed (`bed_category_id`, `service_id`), as the wards notes promise. Billing posts the day charges from `bed.assigned`/`bed.released` and these rows; no charge is posted here.
- **Events** (ids and codes only): `encounter.opened` (type `ipd`), `admission.created`, `bed.assigned` (entity: the bed; assignment, admission, patient, ward, category, service), `bed.released` (reason `transfer` or `discharge`), `encounter.closed` and `discharge.completed` (discharge type). Rolled back requests emit nothing.
- **Idempotency keys are required** on every POST and nothing of an answer is kept (`body: 'none'`: each names a patient); a repeat changes nothing and answers with the admission as it is now.
- **The board:** `GET /beds/board` lists beds in use by ward name and bed number (cursor on ward name, ward id, number, id), with the occupant of an occupied bed (name and MRN), and audits each occupied bed's admission as a read. `GET /beds/events` is a server-sent stream through `core/live` on the `beds` topic (`BEDS_TOPIC`, exported by wards): `{ wardId }` only, published in the transaction of every change, so the board reads again through the audited route. Nothing about a patient travels on the stream.
- **Counts (#734):** `GET /beds/counts?wardId=` answers the number of beds in use for each status (all four, 0 when none) in one query, with `admission:read` and the wards flag, counting the same beds as the board. No patient is read, so nothing is audited. The census and the dashboard capacity widget use it.
- **Scope:** `own` is the admissions the caller admitted as a doctor, `department` those of their department's; the board and the stream are not narrowed (a bed has no owner). Defaults are `all`.
- **Cancelling a mistaken admission (#573):** `POST /admissions/{id}/cancel` with a reason (plain text, 200 characters) and the version, for `admission:cancel` (admin, doctor, receptionist) with `admission:read`. The stay's encounter is cancelled through `cancelInpatientEncounter` (clinical): only while nothing is on its record (409 `has_records`, discharge instead) and no note is a draft (409 `unsigned_notes`). The bed it held is released with the reason `cancelled` and set `available` at once (nothing was used, so nothing to clean). The admission keeps `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by` and `cancel_reason`; the patient can be admitted again. Emits `encounter.cancelled`, `bed.released` (reason `cancelled`) and `admission.cancelled`; the reason is never in an event.
- **Why a patient was given a bed (#574):** `assign-bed` and `transfer` take an optional `reason` (a list: `clinical`, `step_down`, `isolation`, `patient_request`, `ward_closure`, `other`) and a `note` (plain text, 200 characters, only with a reason). They are kept on the **new** `bed_assignments` row (`move_reason`, `move_note`) and returned on its `BedStay` as `moveReason`/`moveNote`; never in an event. `BedStay.dayPriceMinor` is the standard list's price of the stay's bed-day service on the day it started (at the hospital's time zone), absent when there is none; the history is read through `staysOf`. `BedStay.billedDays`/`billedMinor` (#650) are the days the stay bills by the bed-day job's rule, each at the price of its own date; the bed history shows them in place of the guide amount.
- **Details at admission (#575):** `POST /admissions` takes optional `adviceNote` (1000), `attendantName`/`attendantRelation` (list)/`attendantPhone`, `depositMinor` and `consents` (a set from `admission`, `treatment`, `financial`, `privacy`), kept on the admission and returned on it; the admission guard trigger keeps them unchanged afterwards. A relation or phone without a name is 422 `attendant_name_required`. The deposit is only a record of the amount asked for: no payment or charge is posted (billing's). The advice, attendant and note keys are redacted in logs and never in an event.
- **The visit it came from (#678):** `POST /admissions` takes an optional `sourceEncounterId`, an OPD, daycare or emergency encounter of the same patient that is not cancelled (`admissionSource` in clinical; 422 `unknown_encounter`). Kept on `admissions.source_encounter_id` (composite FK with the patient), returned on the admission, never changed, never in an event. A reference only: nothing is copied from that encounter.
- **Bed reservations (#582):** `POST /beds/{bedId}/reservations` (`bed:reserve` with `admission:read`: admin, nurse, receptionist) holds a bed for a patient until `expiresAt` (in the future, within 14 days, else 422 `invalid_expiry`), with an optional plain note; `POST /bed-reservations/{id}/cancel` lets it go. A bed has one open reservation and a patient one (partial unique indexes on `bed_reservations`; 409 `bed_reserved`, `patient_has_reservation`); a blocked or archived bed, or a patient already admitted, is refused. It is a record beside the bed, not a bed status: the board shows it as `reservation` on the bed (the patient's name and MRN, so `admission:read`). `claimBed` (admit with a bed, assign-bed, transfer) refuses a bed held for another patient (409 `bed_reserved`) under the ward lock; giving the held-for patient any bed (`placeInBed`) fulfils all their reservations. A reservation is dead the moment `expires_at` passes (every reader compares with `now()`); the row is marked `expired` the next time that bed or patient is reserved, and there is no sweep job. Events `bed.reserved` and `bed.reservation_ended` (reason `cancelled` or `fulfilled`) carry ids only, never the note. Not here: reserving for a patient with a planned admission date, reminders, a list of reservations apart from the board.
- **Discharge plan and clearances (BE-25, #169, `discharge-plan.ts`, migration 0097):** `GET`/`PUT /admissions/{id}/discharge-plan` (advice: `admission:discharge`) and `PUT .../clearances/{kind}` (`discharge:clear`, with `admission:read`; admin, doctor, nurse) keep the advice and, for nursing, pharmacy and lab, whether each is cleared, by whom and when. A stay nobody wrote to answers three pending clearances at version 1; every write bumps `version`. Writes are refused once the patient is not admitted (409 `invalid_transition`). **`POST /admissions/{id}/discharge` is 409 `clearance_pending`** while a clearance its type needs is open (`REQUIRED_CLEARANCES`: routine and referred need all three, against advice and deceased nursing and pharmacy, absconded none); it is checked before the encounter is closed, so nothing changes. Advice and notes are plain text, audited as `discharge_plan`, never in an event. Pending lab work is marked by hand; it is not read from the lab orders.
- **Bill gate (#790):** the discharge body takes optional `billAcknowledged`. After the clearances, `app.dischargeGates.check` (core hook; billing registers the bill policy, see its notes) may refuse with 409 `bill_outstanding` or `bill_unconfirmed`; nothing is changed then. This module knows no gate by name.
- **Discharge summary (BE-25, #169, `discharge-summary.ts`):** `GET`/`PUT /admissions/{id}/discharge-summary`, `POST .../sign`, `.../addenda`, `.../print` with `dischargeSummary:read|create|sign` (and `admission:read`). Six plain-text sections kept as a draft under a `version` (409 `conflict` when behind; the first save sends none), signed once by `dischargeSummary:sign` (422 `summary_incomplete` without diagnoses and course); a signed row is immutable in the database (trigger) and a correction is an appended addendum (`discharge_summary_addenda`, insert only). The answer's `version` is the row's plus the number of addenda. A template (id and version) may be kept as a reference. Reads, saves, signing, addenda and prints are audited (`discharge_summary`); the text is never in an event or log. Not here: filling the sections from the stay's record, a summary of a cancelled admission (409).
- **Not here (proposed follow-ups):** admission orders before the admission.
- **The stay's encounter is not closed or cancelled from the clinical routes** (409 `managed_by_admission`): only discharge ends it, so a discharge can never find it already ended.
- **Decided: `admission:read` implies seeing the patient's name and MRN** on admissions and the board, as `queue:read` does for tokens; grant it with that in mind. The board is not narrowed by an `own` or `department` scope (a bed has no owner); use `all` for those who work it.
- **Nursing reads the stay through `stayInScope`** (`index.ts`, BE-24, #163): an admission within the scope the caller holds a nursing permission with, as the admission list does (`own` the admitting doctor, `department` theirs). Nursing never reads the admissions table itself.
