# Operations API

What was decided for this feature's API (BE-30, #200). Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. Web: the blood bank unit list (#196) and, in #586 part 1, screening, cross-match, compatible units, reactions and the trace on its Units screen (`apps/web/src/features/blood-bank/CLAUDE.md`); the other screens are in their own web folders.

- **One folder, four modules.** `features/operations` holds rosters, the blood bank, ambulances and bed cleaning tasks, each behind its own hospital switch: `roster`, `bloodbank`, `ambulance`, `housekeeping`. Migration 0079 (the tables have `tenant_id` defaulting to `app_tenant_id()`, row-level security as usual).
- **Permissions:** `roster:read|manage`, `bloodUnit:read|manage|issue`, `ambulance:read|manage|dispatch`, `cleaningTask:read|work|manage`. A move route that has several targets (blood unit, cleaning task) declares the lowest permission and checks the one the move needs in the handler (`requireGrant`, 403 `permission_denied`).
- **Roster:** a shift is at most 24 hours; one person has no two scheduled shifts that overlap (exclusion constraint, 409 `shift_overlap`). A shift is only cancelled (once, a trigger), never edited or deleted; cancelling frees the time. The person must be a member of the hospital.
- **Leave (#591, migration 0089):** `roster_leaves`, a request by the caller for a period (end in the future, at most 90 days): `requested -> approved | rejected | cancelled`, `approved -> cancelled`. Asked with `roster:requestLeave` (own leave only), decided with `roster:manage` by someone other than the owner (403 `own_request`, also a CHECK), withdrawn by the owner or a manager (`cancel` declares `roster:read` and checks the grant in the handler). One person has no two requested or approved leaves that overlap (409 `leave_overlap`). Triggers keep shifts and approved leave apart under one advisory lock per person: a shift into approved leave is 409 `on_leave`, approving over scheduled shifts is 409 `shifts_in_leave`. No reason column on purpose. Events `roster.leave_requested|approved|rejected|cancelled`.
- **Leave kinds, allowances and self approval (#667, migration 0111):** `roster_leaves.kind` (`annual|sick|casual|unpaid`, default annual) and `self_approved`. `roster_leave_allowances` (days a year for each kind, same for everyone; a kind with no row is unlimited) and `roster_settings.self_approval` (`never|sole_manager`), both set by `PUT /roster/leave-settings` (`roster:manage`, replaces all; read with `GET`, `roster:read`). A request over the year's allowance is 422 `allowance_exceeded`: days are `round(hours/24)`, at least 1, counted for requested and approved leaves of that kind that start in the same calendar year on the hospital's clock (a leave across New Year counts wholly in its start year); checked under a per-person advisory lock. `GET /roster/leave-balance?userId&year` gives used (approved) and pending days with the remainder; another person's needs `roster:manage`. Approving one's own leave is allowed only with `sole_manager` and when no other active member holds `roster:manage` (`role_permissions`); it sets `self_approved` (the CHECK `decided_by <> user_id` now allows it only then) and `roster.leave_approved` carries `selfApproved`. Rejecting one's own stays 403 `own_request`. Not here: carry-over, half days, accrual, a different leave year.
- **Templates, copied weeks and swaps (#592, migration 0110, `rosterplan.ts`):** `roster_shift_templates` per hospital: name (one live template of a name, case-insensitive, 409 `template_exists`), kind, `HH:MM` start and end on the hospital's clock (an end at or before the start runs into the next day; the hours differ, 422 `invalid_period`), optional department. Retired once (`active = false`, a trigger), never edited or deleted; routes `GET|POST /roster/templates`, `POST /roster/templates/{id}/retire`. The hours only fill a form: shifts keep instants. `POST /roster/copy-week` copies the scheduled shifts starting in the 7 days from `fromStart` into `weeks` (1 to 12) weeks from `toStart` (a whole number of days away, no copied week overlapping the source; 300 source shifts and 1000 copies at most, 422 `too_many_shifts`). Each copy runs under a savepoint: one that clashes (person on duty, 409 `shift_overlap` rule, or on approved leave) is skipped and listed, the rest are made, each with `roster.shift_scheduled`. `POST /roster/shifts/{id}/swap` hands a scheduled shift to `userId`, or exchanges it with `withShiftId` (+`withVersion`): the old shift(s) cancelled, new ones scheduled with `replaces_shift_id` pointing at the old, all in the request's transaction (a refusal undoes everything). All `roster:manage`. Not here: swaps requested by staff and approved by a manager, coverage rules.
- **Blood bank:** `collected -> testing -> available -> reserved -> issued -> transfused`; `discarded` (a reason required) from any state before transfusion; `expired` for an available or reserved unit past its date; `reserved -> available` releases. The table is in `BLOOD_TRANSITIONS` and again in the `blood_units_guard` trigger, so SQL cannot skip a step either; the trigger also refuses reserving or issuing a unit past its date. Every move is a row in the append-only `blood_unit_events`. Testing, clearing and discarding are `bloodUnit:manage` (the bank); reserving, issuing, transfusing and releasing are `bloodUnit:issue` (the ward). A patient is named by id only, never a name; reserving needs a patient the caller may read. A job (`bloodbank.expiry`, every 15 minutes, `expiry.ts`) moves each available or reserved unit past its date to `expired` for every hospital with the blood bank on, with a history row by the system actor (the all-zero id) and the usual event; a person may still record it (`not_expired` otherwise). Issuing may name a visit and a priced service (`encounterId`, `serviceId`, both or neither); they ride on `blood.unit_status_changed` and billing charges the service once per unit (`blood:<unitId>`), as for ambulance trips (#588). No migration: nothing is stored on the unit.
- **Donors, collections, screening, blood group and cross-match (#585 part A, migration 0126; `blooddonors.ts`, `bloodtesting.ts`, `bloodcompat.ts`):** a donor is not a patient: `blood_donors` keeps a name, birth date, sex, blood group and optional phone, nothing else, under `bloodDonor:read|manage` (the bank: admin and lab technician). Lists and `POST /blood/donors/search` (the term in the body, never logged) return `maskedName` and `maskedPhone` only; `GET /blood/donors/{id}` is the full record and an audited read. The rules are fixed in code, not set by a hospital: age 18 to 65 at registration and on the day of a collection (422 `donor_age`), at least 90 days since the last collection (422 `donation_too_soon`), no collection while deferred (422 `donor_deferred`). A deferral is temporary (`until`) or permanent, never shortened, a permanent one never lifted (trigger too). `POST /blood/collections` locks the donor row, inserts an insert-only `blood_collections` row and its units (status `collected`, the donor's group, `donor_id`, `collection_id`); `POST /blood-units` still registers a unit with no donor. **Screening** (`POST /blood-units/{id}/screening`, `bloodUnit:test`): HIV, hepatitis B and C, syphilis, malaria, once per unit, while it is in testing; the `outcome` is a generated column (`cleared` only if all are negative). An AFTER INSERT trigger sets `blood_units.screening_status`; the unit guard refuses any other writer (it allows the change only one trigger level down), refuses `testing -> available` unless the status is `cleared`, and a new unit must start `pending`. A reactive result, in the same transaction, discards the unit (history reason "Reactive screening") and defers its donor for good, and discards the donor's other units that are still `collected` or `testing` (reason "Donor had a reactive screening", `bloodhold.ts`); units already cleared (available, reserved, issued) are left for the bank to decide (window-period recall is a proposed follow-up). Events carry the outcome only, never which test. **Patient blood group** (`patient_blood_groups`, append-only; the latest row is current): `GET|POST /patients/{id}/blood-group` under `patient.bloodGroup:read|update`; a different group than the one on record needs a `reason` (422 `reason_required`), the same group again writes nothing. Not merged with a patient merge (see follow-ups). **Cross-match** (`POST /blood-units/{id}/crossmatch`, `bloodUnit:test`): an available unit against a patient with a group on record; `valid_until` is `performed_at + 72 hours` (set by a trigger); a `compatible` result for a pair that can never match (`isCompatible`: red cells and whole blood by the red cell rule, plasma, platelets and cryo by the plasma rule with Rh ignored) is 422 `abo_incompatible`, an `incompatible` result is recorded as is. Reserving needs the latest cross-match of that unit and patient to be compatible and unexpired: the move route answers 409 `crossmatch_required`, and the unit guard raises the same for SQL. `POST /blood/compatible-units` (`bloodUnit:read`, patient in the body) lists available units that fit, soonest expiry first, with the latest cross-match and whether it is still valid. `BloodUnit` gained `screeningStatus`. Events: `blood.donor_registered`, `blood.donor_deferred`, `blood.collection_recorded`, `blood.unit_screened`, `blood.crossmatch_recorded` (ids, the group and outcomes). The web blood bank screen (#196) does not know screening or cross-match yet: against this API its "Clear" and "Reserve" actions answer 409 until those screens exist.
- **Transfusion reactions and the trace (#585 part B, migration 0127; `bloodreactions.ts`, `bloodhold.ts`):** `transfusion_reactions` (insert-only) and `transfusion_reaction_updates` (insert-only), under `transfusionReaction:read|record` (admin, doctor, nurse, lab technician). `POST /transfusion-reactions` needs a unit that is `issued` or `transfused` (409 `unit_not_given`) and the patient the unit went to (422 `patient_mismatch`; the unit keeps `patient_id`), an optional visit of that patient, severity `mild|moderate|severe`, kind `febrile|allergic|hemolytic|trali|taco|other`, `transfusedAt`/`onsetAt` (onset not before, neither in the future). A severe or hemolytic reaction holds the other units of the same collection (`siblingUnitIds`): each still collected, testing, available, reserved or issued is discarded in the same transaction through the unit guard, history reason "Held after a transfusion reaction", with `blood.unit_status_changed`; the ids come back in `heldUnitIds` of that answer only. The recorder needs no `bloodUnit:manage` for this: the hold is part of recording. `POST /transfusion-reactions/{id}/updates` adds a note and may raise the severity (never lower or repeat it: 422 `severity_not_raised`, serialised by an advisory lock); raising to severe holds siblings the same way and emits `blood.reaction_recorded` again. `currentSeverity` is the highest of the first report and the updates. Event `blood.reaction_recorded` carries `unitId`, `patientId`, `severity` only (no kind, no text). **Trace:** `GET /blood-units/{id}/trace` (`transfusionReaction:read`): donor (the name only when the caller also holds `bloodDonor:read`), collection, screening outcome, every move, cross-matches, `recipientPatientId` (taken from the latest `issued` history row; an id, never a name) and reactions; `GET /blood/donors/{id}/trace` (`bloodDonor:read` and `transfusionReaction:read`) lists up to 200 units of the donor with recipient id, reaction count and worst severity. Both reads are audited (`blood_unit`, `blood_donor`). Not here: the web screens for reactions and the trace, notifying a recipient's doctor, regulatory reports (haemovigilance), recalling already-cleared units of a reactive donor.
- **Ambulances:** a trip goes `requested -> dispatched -> picked_up -> completed`, or `cancelled` before the pick-up. Dispatching names an available ambulance and sets it `on_trip`; ending or cancelling the trip frees it. A partial unique index gives an ambulance one live trip. `out_of_service` and back is `POST /ambulances/{id}/service`, only from `available`. A trip's patient is optional (an emergency with no record). Pick-up and destination are plain text, not looked at beyond length.
- **Charging a trip (#589, migration 0106):** a trip request may name an `encounterId` (a visit of the trip's patient, not cancelled: 422 `unknown_encounter`) and a `serviceId` (active: 422 `unknown_service`; needs the visit: 422 `encounter_required`). They are fixed at request (the guard trigger). `ambulance.trip_status_changed` carries both; billing (`posting.ts`) listens and, on `to: completed`, posts one charge of source `service`, `source_ref` `ambulance:<trip id>`, priced from the list in effect by `postCharge`, so a repeat posts nothing. A cancelled trip posts nothing. The price is never sent. Not here: choosing the service or visit after the request, a charge by distance.
- **Crew (#590, migration 0109):** `ambulance_trip_crew`, named in the `crew` of the dispatch move (`[{ userId, duty }]`, duty `driver|paramedic|attendant`, at most 8, optional). Written once with the dispatch and never changed (insert-only grants); the trip carries `crew` with names (users joined as the roster does), drivers first. Each must be an active member (422 `unknown_crew`), no one twice and at most one driver (422 `invalid_crew`, also a unique index), and no one already on a dispatched or picked-up trip (409 `crew_busy`); crew with any other move is 422 `crew_at_dispatch`. The field is `duty`, not `role`, because the web lint rule forbids comparing role names. Not here: changing the crew after dispatch, crew shifts or licences.
- **Cleaning tasks:** `open -> in_progress -> done` or `cancelled`. Finishing a task moves its bed from cleaning to available, under the ward's lock and announced on the beds stream (it needs the `wards` module). The subscriber to `bed.released` makes a `discharge` task for the bed when the hospital has `housekeeping` on (one live task per bed, a repeat event does nothing). A `routine` task is asked for with `POST /cleaning-tasks` for an available (set to cleaning) or cleaning bed. `bed:update` housekeeping in admissions still works on its own; finishing a task on a bed already marked ready by hand just completes the task.
- **Idempotency:** creates require an `Idempotency-Key`; moves accept one. Responses hold no patient name, so the roster, blood bank, ambulance fleet and cleaning keep the whole answer; a trip request keeps only its id.
- **Events (ids and codes):** `roster.shift_scheduled`, `roster.shift_cancelled`, `blood.unit_registered`, `blood.unit_status_changed`, `ambulance.trip_requested`, `ambulance.trip_status_changed`, `cleaning.task_created`, `cleaning.task_completed`. The outbox event name allows letters only before the first dot, which is why they are not `blood_unit.*`.
- **Not here (proposed follow-ups):** the web screens (#195 to #198) and tests (#199); the web screens for donors and the patient's blood group (#586 part 2), a list of reactions in the web app (`GET /transfusion-reactions` exists), the blood group following a patient merge; a roster that repeats (templates, weekly patterns) and swaps; ambulance crew, mileage, GPS; assigning a cleaning team and its shifts.
