# Pharmacy API

What was decided for this feature's API (#100, PH-01, with the dispensing part of BE-21, #107). Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. The web side's notes are in `apps/web/src/features/pharmacy/CLAUDE.md`.

- **`features/pharmacy`** is the queue of prescriptions to dispense and the dispenses of their medication lines. Behind the `pharmacy` module flag. Counter sales and the controlled drug register are #101, returns #104.
- **Permissions and defaults** (the "Pharmacy dispense and sales" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`): `dispense:read` (the queue, a prescription, the options of a line, printing a slip) and `dispense:create` (dispensing, completing a line), for the pharmacist and the admin. Every write also needs `dispense:read`, both at scope `all`, as its answer is the patient's record. The pharmacist also gained `allergy:read` (triage's defaults), for the warning on the screen. **Only the scope `all` reaches anything,** as in the laboratory and radiology: the pharmacy dispenses the whole hospital's prescriptions.
- **The pharmacy never writes another module's table.** It reads a medication order and moves it through `medicationOrder` and `moveMedicationOrder` from `features/clinical` (which audit the order and emit `order.completed`), checks an item through `dispensableDrug` and `substitutesOf` from `features/drugs`, and takes stock out through `usableBatches` and `stockOut` from `features/inventory`, which post to the ledger with the source and reason `dispense`. While the pharmacy is on, the clinical route `POST /orders/:id/status` refuses a medication order (409 `medication_order`). The queue and a prescription read prescriptions, orders, patients and staff names in this module's SQL, as radiology's worklist does.
- **A dispense** (`POST /pharmacy/dispenses`) is units of one medication line from 1 to 10 batches at one place, each batch once, at the order's version (409 `conflict`). **Lock order:** the order (`for update`), then inventory's (the place `for share`, the item's stock, each balance). Refused: an order completed or cancelled (409 `invalid_transition`), more than the prescribed quantity in all (409 `over_quantity`), more than 10,000 units at once (400), the prescribed medicine archived (409 `item_archived`), a place not in use (422 `unknown_location`), a batch not of the item given or given twice (422 `unknown_batch`), an expired batch at the hospital today (409 `batch_expired`), more than a batch holds at the place (409 `insufficient_stock`, from the balance's check, so two dispenses of the last units at once give one: tested). It moves the order on through each step it has not taken (placed, accepted, in progress), to completed when the prescribed quantity is reached; a line with no quantity stays in progress until `POST /pharmacy/orders/:id/complete` (in progress with something dispensed, at its version: a line moved in progress by the order route before the pharmacy was on is dispensed first). Emits `pharmacy.dispensed` (the dispense, order, prescription, encounter, patient, item given and prescribed, place, and whether substituted: ids only), and `stock.low` from `stockOut` when the hospital's stock crosses the item's reorder level, as an approved adjustment does.
- **A dispense is made from a reading of the line:** its version and `expectedDispensedQuantity`, the units given so far (409 `conflict` when either changed). The version alone is not enough: a line in progress keeps its version from one dispense to the next, as its state does not change (the trigger refuses `in_progress` to itself), so without the units two counters could both give a line with no quantity prescribed (tested).
- **Exactly once:** the Idempotency-Key is required and nothing of the answer is kept (`body: 'none'`, `request.idempotentId` the dispense): a repeat moves no stock and answers the dispense as it is now, audited as a read. The order's lock and version make a second dispense of the same reading a 409.
- **Generic substitution:** `drugId` names another medicine in use of the same generic name, form and strength (compared on the drug master's stored keys, any brand; 422 `not_a_substitute`), with `substitutionReason` (plain text, tidied, 1 to 200; 400 without it, and 400 with a reason for the prescribed item). The dispense keeps both items, and the check `dispenses_substitution` holds a reason exactly when they differ.
- **The database holds the rules too** (migration 0051): a dispense names its order with the order's encounter, patient and prescribed item (`dispenses_order`, a foreign key to the unique `orders_tenant_id_medication` the migration adds), and its prescription with the same encounter; it keeps the item given as it read (name, schedule, controlled), its quantity and who dispensed it. Each line names its batch with its item and the ledger entry that took its units out (unique; a foreign key to `stock_ledger (tenant_id, id, batch_id)`, a unique key the migration adds), so a line and its entry are of one hospital and one batch. Both tables are INSERT and SELECT only, a trigger refuses UPDATE and DELETE to the owner too, and TRUNCATE is refused to everyone. The ledger's `source` and `reason` checks gained `dispense`, which raised the contract to 0.9.0 (`StockLedgerEntry.reason` is now `StockLedgerReason`).
- **The queue** (`GET /pharmacy/queue`): `open`, prescriptions with a medication line placed, accepted or in progress, the oldest first; `done`, none open and some in progress or completed, the newest first; prescribed within `days` (1 to 90, default 7) at the hospital, of one `patientId` when asked. It counts each prescription's lines in a lateral over its orders and pages on `prescribed_at` and id (`prescriptions_tenant_prescribed`); `total` with the first page (a window count over the days asked). Its cost is the hospital's prescriptions in the window; measured before the pilot. Names the patient (name and MRN), as `dispense:read` says; every prescription listed is audited as read. A cursor names its state, so one of the other state is 400 `invalid_cursor`.
- **A prescription** (`GET /pharmacy/prescriptions/:id`): its medication lines in order with the units dispensed against each, and its dispenses oldest first with their batches; audited (the prescription, each line as an order, each dispense). A prescription with no medication line is 404 `prescription_not_found`.
- **Options** (`GET /pharmacy/orders/:id/options?locationId=`): the prescribed medicine (absent once archived) and its substitutes, each with its batches in date with stock at the place, nearest expiry first. Not audited beyond the order's id: it holds stock, not the patient.
- **Printing a slip** (`POST /pharmacy/dispenses/:id/print`) writes the audit entry `print` of the dispense and answers 204, a key per press, as prescriptions print (#89).
- **Audit:** `dispense` (`create`, `read`, `print`), `prescription` and `order` reads. **Not here:** charges (billing, M11, prices a dispense by subscribing to `pharmacy.dispensed`; no charge is posted until it exists), returns, counter sales, the controlled drug register, and a reason for completing a line short.
