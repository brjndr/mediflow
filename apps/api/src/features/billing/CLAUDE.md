# Billing API: module notes

What was decided for billing's API (#114). The root and `apps/api` `CLAUDE.md` rules apply; the web module's notes are `apps/web/src/features/billing/CLAUDE.md`.

## What exists

Behind the `billing` switch. Permissions: `charge:read|create`, `invoice:read|create|issue|print|adjust`, `payment:read|create|refund`, `creditNote:create`, `cashRegister:read|manage`, `taxRate:manage`, `discount:apply|approve|manage`, `package:manage`. Billing works the whole hospital's bills, so every route needs the scope `all`.

- **Charges** (`charges`): posted by `posting.ts` from events, or by hand through `POST /encounters/{id}/charges` (below); a route never writes a price. `order.completed` (a lab test's service, or the order's imaging or procedure service), `pharmacy.dispensed` (one charge a batch, at the batch's MRP) and `pharmacy.dispense_returned`. Unique by `source_ref` (`order:<id>`, `dispense:<line id>`), so a repeated event, or an order completed again after a reopening, posts nothing more. Emits `charge.posted`.
- **A service charged by hand (#115):** `POST /encounters/{id}/charges` takes a `serviceId` and a `quantity` (`charge:create` and `charge:read`, idempotency key required, audited `create` of `charge`). The server prices it exactly as the events do (`priceOf`, `postCharge` in `posting.ts`): the standard price list in effect today, never a client price. It is source `service`, `source_ref` `manual:<new id>`, pending, and emits `charge.posted` with the caller as actor. Unlike the events it refuses what the desk should not choose: 404 `service_not_found` (also another hospital's service), 409 `service_archived`, 409 `no_price`, 409 `encounter_cancelled`. A repeat of one key posts nothing more and answers with the charge as it is now. `charge:create` is the admin's and the receptionist's by default: the price comes from the list, so unlike a typed line (`invoice:adjust`) it skips nothing.
- **Pricing:** the standard price list's price in effect today at the hospital. A service archived since, or with no price in effect, is posted at its last price and flagged `service_archived`; with no price at all, at 0 and flagged `no_price`. Work done is never held up by the price list; the billing desk reads the flag. Decided for the question in #114's comment on archived services.
- **Returns of dispenses:** a charge still pending and on no draft whose units all came back is voided; any other is flagged `return_review` for the desk to credit. Computed from all that has been returned, so a repeat changes nothing.
- **Invoices:** drafts are made from pending charges and typed lines (`invoice:adjust`). The server works out every line and total (generated columns), refuses one above a safe integer (422 `amount_too_large`). A charge sits in one draft (the draft row-locks its charges in id order: 409 `charge_unavailable`). A change carries the `version` it started from (409 `version_conflict`).
- **No empty draft:** a create or change with no charge and no typed line is 422 `empty_invoice`. An invoice holding a charge flagged `return_review` is not issued (409 `charge_returned`) until the desk takes it off. `cashRegister:read` (every session, with its difference) is the admin's by default; a receptionist opens and settles their own drawer with `cashRegister:manage`.
- **Issuing:** the next number of the fiscal year (April to March, `INV-2026-27-000001`), from `invoice_counters`, a row locked in the issuing transaction, so numbers are gapless and a rolled-back issue gives its number back. Charges become `invoiced`. An invoice of 0 is issued as `paid`. Issued invoices are immutable in the database (a trigger): number, visit, patient, lines and total never change; only paid and credited sums, status and version move. Emits `invoice.issued`, `invoice.paid`.
- **Payments and refunds** (`payments`, append-only): each takes the invoice's row lock first, so racing payments never overshoot what is due (409 `overpayment`, `refund_exceeds`). Cash goes into the caller's open register session (409 `no_open_register`). A refund is its own row of kind `refund`; `paid_minor` is net. A paid invoice refunded becomes `issued` again.
- **Credit notes** (`credit_notes`, append-only, own number series `CN-…`): an amount against an issued invoice, up to what is left (409 `credit_exceeds`) and never leaving more paid than is owed (409 `refund_first`). Credited in full, the invoice is `void` and its charges go back to `pending` to be billed again (the one way an invoiced charge goes back: the trigger asks for a void invoice). Emits `invoice.credited`.
- **Cash register** (`cash_register_sessions`): one open a person (partial unique index). Cash payments and refunds recorded by the person go into it; closing keeps the cash counted, the expected amount the server worked out (opening plus cash taken less cash refunded) and so the difference. Only the opener closes it; a closed session is immutable and takes no more cash. Emits `register.closed`.
- **The invoice list and printing (#110):** `POST /invoices/search` (a body of `q`, `status`, `from`, `to`, `cursor`, `limit`; a POST so a term that can name a patient stays out of the address) answers a cursor page of invoices newest first, drafts included; every word of `q` must be in the number, the patient's name or the MRN, and `%` and `_` are literal. Audited as one `search` of `invoice`. `POST /invoices/{id}/print` (`invoice:read` and the new `invoice:print`, admin and receptionist by default) writes the audit entry `print` and nothing else, once per idempotency key; a draft or a void invoice is 409 `not_printable`. Both need the scope `all`.
- **Tax (#119, migration 0060):** the hospital's rate for each tax class (`exempt`, `reduced`, `standard`, `higher`, the classes services and drugs already carry) is a row of `tax_rates` in basis points (1200 is 12%); a class with no row takes the default 0, 5, 12 and 18% (`DEFAULT_TAX_BP` in `tax.ts`). `GET /billing/tax-rates` (`invoice:read`) answers all four; `PUT` (`taxRate:manage`, the admin's, key required, audited) sets the classes named, each once. **A charge keeps the class it was posted with** (`charges.tax_class`, copied from the service, never changed: the guard trigger), so a service reclassified later changes nothing already posted. **A dispensed medicine has no class**: it is charged at the batch's MRP, which holds its tax, so it is not taxed again. **A draft takes the rates in force when it is saved** (`replaceLines`): each line stores its class and rate (`tax_rate_bp`) and the database works its tax out (`tax_minor`, generated: `round(quantity × price × rate / 10,000)`, half up, per line, in numeric). `invoices.total_minor` is **what is owed, tax included**, and `tax_minor` the part of it that is tax, so payments, credit notes, refunds and the register need no change and the line's `totalMinor` stays before tax. Once issued the trigger keeps `tax_minor` with the total and the lines, so changing a rate never alters an issued bill. A typed line (`invoice:adjust`) takes an optional `taxClass`, none meaning no tax. Tax is not split into CGST, SGST and IGST: the patient is the buyer, so a screen shows the tax by rate.
- **Numbers (#119):** gapless per hospital and fiscal year, by the locked counter row (see Issuing); the test "gives concurrent issues distinct numbers with no gap" holds it. A number never restarts inside a year and starts again at 000001 on 1 April at the hospital's clock.
- **Idempotency:** every POST needs an `Idempotency-Key` except `PATCH /invoices/{id}`, which accepts one. They store ids only (`body: 'none'`), and a repeat answers with the record as it is now.

- **Discounts (#116, migration 0061):** a line (a charge's or a typed one's) and the whole bill may carry a discount, `{ kind: 'percent' | 'amount', value, reason }` (basis points or minor units; `discount:apply`, the admin's and the receptionist's by default). The reason is a fixed code (`staff`, `charity`, `senior_citizen`, `goodwill`, `correction`, `other`), never free text, so the audit log and events never carry a sentence about a patient. It comes off **before tax**: `invoice_lines.discount_minor` is generated by the database (`line_discount()`, half up), a bill discount is spread over the lines in proportion to what each costs after its own discount (`spread` in `discount.ts`, largest remainder, earlier line first on a tie, so the shares add up exactly) and stored as `bill_discount_minor`, and the line's `tax_minor` is worked out on what is left. A line's `totalMinor` stays quantity times price (before discount and tax); `invoices.total_minor` is the lines less discounts plus tax; `invoices.discount_minor` the discounts together. A discount above what it comes off is 422 `discount_exceeds`; a percentage above 100 is 400. **Content comes whole:** a save sends `chargeDiscounts` (by charge id), a `discount` on each typed line and a `billDiscount`; leaving them out takes the discounts away. Giving, changing or removing one needs `discount:apply`; saving a draft with the discounts it already has does not (403 `permission_denied` otherwise), as with typed lines.
- **Approval above the limit (#116):** `billing_settings.discount_limit_bp` (`GET`/`PUT /billing/discount-settings`; read with `invoice:read`, set with `discount:manage`, the admin's) is the share of the bill before tax that may be discounted without approval, 500 (5%) until set. Above it the draft's `discount_status` is `pending` and the invoice is not issued (409 `discount_needs_approval`; the database refuses it too, in `invoices_guard`). `POST /invoices/{id}/discount-approval` (`discount:approve`, the admin's; key required; body `{ version }`) approves it, **never by the person who gave or last changed it** (`discount_applied_by`; 403 `own_discount`), so a hospital with one admin has the receptionist give and the admin approve. The approval stands for the discount and the bill it was given on (`discount_approved_minor` and `discount_approved_gross_minor`): saving the draft again with nothing changed in the discounts, the same discount total and the same bill before discount keeps it `approved`; any change to the discounts or the bill is `pending` again (when still above the limit) and its maker is whoever changed it. The maker (`discount_applied_by`) is kept for as long as there is a discount, within the limit too, and a re-save with nothing changed by someone else keeps them, so lowering the limit later cannot make the maker the approver. Discounts are compared field by field (kind, value, reason), so the order of keys in a body decides nothing, and a discount that leaves with its charge counts as a change. **Known policy gap:** the admin holds `discount:manage` too, so one admin can raise the limit and then discount without a second person; the change of the limit is audited. Issued invoices keep every discount column (trigger, `invoices_guard`). A discount within the limit is `none`. A changed limit applies when a draft is next saved. Issued invoices keep their discounts for ever (trigger). Audited as `discount` (a change), `approve` and `update` of `billing_settings`; `invoice.issued` still carries ids only. Tests: `discounts.test.ts`.

- **Packages (#117, migration 0062, `package-routes.ts`):** a package is a service of category `package` with a **flat price of its own** (never the sum of its parts). `package_items` says which services it **includes** (covered by that price) and which it **excludes** (always billed on their own). `GET` (`charge:read`) and `PUT /services/{id}/package` (`package:manage`, the admin's; key required; the whole list replaced; 422 `invalid_package_item` for the package itself, another package, an archived or another hospital's service, 400 for one named twice; 409 `not_a_package`; audited as `package`). `POST /encounters/{id}/packages` (`charge:create`, key required) posts one pending charge of source `package` at the package's price in effect today (never sent by the client; same refusals as a service charged by hand, plus 409 `package_empty` and `package_applied`, the visit row-locked so two presses apply it once) and then **voids the visit's pending charges, on no draft, for included services**, flagged `package_covered` with `charges.package_charge_id` pointing at the package charge. **A charge posted afterwards** for an included service (an event or a hand charge; `coveringPackage` in `posting.ts`) is posted void the same way, so the ledger keeps what was done and the bill never counts it twice. An excluded service is billed as usual. **Known gaps, decided:** a charge already on a draft, or already invoiced, is not touched, so the package is billed on top of it until the desk takes it off the draft or credits the invoice (the response does not say so yet); a covered charge stays void if the package's invoice is later credited and its charge goes back to pending; no quantity limits ("3 sessions, then billed") and no price built up from the contents; the contents are read live, so changing them changes what covers charges posted afterwards on visits that already have the package (charges already covered stay covered). **Concurrency:** applying locks the visit for update and `postCharge` takes a share lock on it before asking whether a package covers the work, so a charge posted at the same moment is covered either way; two saves of one package queue on its service row; `charges_package_covered` ties the flag `package_covered` to its pointer. Tests: `packages.test.ts`.

## Not built (follow-ups)

- **Counter sales** (`pharmacy.sold`) have no encounter, so no charge is posted for them yet (#101 says billing charges them on its own). They need a charge without a visit, and an invoice for it.
- No bed days (R2) or consultation fees: a consultation is billed by a typed line or a charge until a service is linked to the visit.
- Insurance and split billing (R3).
- An invoice left as a draft is never deleted; a draft's charges stay held until it is changed.
