# Inventory API

What was decided for this feature's API (#102, PH-03; the rest of BE-21 is #107). Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. The web side's notes are in `apps/web/src/features/inventory/CLAUDE.md`.

- **`features/inventory`** is the places that keep stock, the batches of the drug and consumable master's items, their stock at each place, the append-only ledger, and adjustments. Behind the `inventory` module flag (new: a hospital may keep a store without the pharmacy module). No patient data anywhere in it.
- **Permissions and defaults** (the "Inventory and purchasing" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`): `stock:read` (store keeper, pharmacist, admin), `stock:adjust` and `stock:approve` (store keeper, admin), `stockLocation:manage` (admin). Every write also needs `stock:read`, as its answer is a record of stock.
- **Stock moves only through the ledger** (migration 0050). `stock_ledger`'s trigger moves the balance of its batch at its place in the same statement; `stock_balances` refuses any other write (`pg_trigger_depth()`), the owner's too, and is never deleted. So every change of stock is in the ledger and a balance is the sum of its entries. **Use `postEntry`** for any new kind of movement (a dispense, a goods receipt, a transfer), adding its `source` to the check.
- **Never below zero, never oversold.** A balance has `CHECK (on_hand >= 0)`; stock coming in upserts the balance row, stock going out updates it (an insert's own row is checked before its conflict is found, so a negative insert would be refused even with stock), and either holds the row locked until the transaction ends. A refusal is `stock_balances_not_negative` (raised by the trigger with that name when there is no row), answered 409 `insufficient_stock`. Two approvals of the last units: one 200, one 409 (tested).
- **The ledger is append-only:** INSERT and SELECT only, its trigger refuses UPDATE and DELETE to the owner as well, and the ledger, the balances and the batches refuse TRUNCATE to everyone (a statement trigger, as the audit log). The trigger sets an entry's `at`, so none is back-dated. An entry names its batch with its item (a foreign key to `stock_batches (tenant_id, id, drug_id)`), its place, its source (`adjustment` now) and source id, the reason, who made it and when, and the balance after it.
- **A batch** is an item's batch number (unique per item whatever the capitals), its expiry date (the last day it may be used; expired the day after at the hospital), and its maximum retail price and unit cost in minor units, both optional. Never changed or removed (INSERT and SELECT only). Made by the approval of an opening adjustment that describes it; when someone made one of that number meanwhile, the same expiry takes it and another is 409 `batch_exists`.
- **An adjustment is asked for by one person and decided by another** (409 `own_adjustment`, and the check `stock_adjustments_two_people`), at its version (409 `conflict`), once (409 `adjustment_decided`; a trigger refuses any change of a decided one and of what was asked). Asking checks the place is in use (`for share`, so it is not archived meanwhile), the item in use (`stockableDrug` from `features/drugs`), the batch is the item's, the direction fits the reason (opening in, damaged and expired out, the database checks it too), and that stock going out is there now (409 `insufficient_stock`; checked again on approval, with the lock). Opening stock may describe a new batch instead (409 `batch_exists` when the item has that number). Approving refuses an item archived since (409 `item_archived`) and a place archived since (409 `location_archived`), posts the entry and emits `stock.adjusted`; rejecting needs a note. **Lock order:** the adjustment, its place (`for share`), the item's stock (`lockItemStock`, an advisory lock per item), then the balance (the ledger's trigger).
- **`stock.low`** is emitted when an approval brings the hospital's stock of an item (every place) from above its reorder level to at or below it; never again while it stays low. The item's stock is held (`lockItemStock`) while it is read before and after, so two approvals of one item at different batches or places at once send it once (tested). A movement added later (a dispense) takes the same lock to keep this. **`stock.expiring`** needs a daily job and is BE-21's (#107). Events carry ids and the reason, never a quantity, a note or a name.
- **Lists** are `GET`s (nothing about a patient, nothing typed): `GET /inventory/stock` (the items in use with their stock, by generic name then brand, `alert` low, expiring or expired, `expiringWithinDays` 1 to 365, default 90, one item or one place; `total` on the first page), `GET /inventory/stock/alerts` (the three counts), `GET /inventory/ledger` (newest first, no `total`), `GET /inventory/adjustments` (by status, pending by default, newest asked first, `total`), `GET /inventory/locations`. An id in a query that is not a uuid matches nothing, as another hospital's. Each part of a cursor is checked for its kind (text, an instant to the microsecond, a uuid), so a forged one is 400 `invalid_cursor`, never a failed cast.
- **Cost of the stock list:** each item's sums are a lateral over its balances, and the alerts are predicates over them, so a page (and its count) costs in proportion to the hospital's items in use (a few thousand). Measured before the pilot; a summary table kept by the ledger's trigger is the change if it is slow.
- **Item names are read from `drugs`** in the lists' SQL, as radiology reads patients; checking an item for an adjustment goes through `stockableDrug`.
- **Idempotency:** every POST takes a key and keeps the whole answer (`body: 'full'`): it holds names of items, places and staff, no patient data. The PATCH of a place takes the version instead.
- **Audit:** `stock_location` (`create`, `update`, `archive`, `restore`) and `stock_adjustment` (`create`, then `update` on the decision). Reads are not audited: no patient data. A place with stock is not archived (409 `location_has_stock`).
- **Dispensing (#100) takes stock out through `stockOut`** (`stock-out.ts`, exported from `index.ts`), with the batches that may be given from `usableBatches` (in date at the hospital today, with stock at the place, nearest expiry first). `stockOut` checks the place is in use (`for share`, 422 `unknown_location`), each batch is the item's (422 `unknown_batch`) and not expired (409 `batch_expired`), takes the item's lock, posts a negative entry per batch (409 `insufficient_stock`) and emits `stock.low` as an approval does. The ledger's `source` and `reason` take `dispense` (migration 0051), so `StockLedgerEntry.reason` is `StockLedgerReason` (the adjustment reasons and `dispense`).
- **Not here:** transfers between places and indents (#105), goods receipts (#103), the controlled drug register (#101), charging (billing reacts to dispenses).
