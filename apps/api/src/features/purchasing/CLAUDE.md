# Purchasing API

What was decided for this feature's API (#103, PH-04, with all of BE-22, #108). Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. The web side's notes are in `apps/web/src/features/purchasing/CLAUDE.md`.

- **`features/purchasing`** is the hospital's suppliers, purchase orders, goods received against them and goods sent back to the supplier. Behind the `purchasing` module flag: a hospital may keep a store without buying through the app. No patient data anywhere in it.
- **Permissions and defaults** (the "Inventory and purchasing" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`), all for the store keeper and the admin: `supplier:read`, `supplier:manage`, `purchaseOrder:read` (orders, receipts and returns), `purchaseOrder:create` (draft, edit a draft, submit, cancel, close), `purchaseOrder:approve`, `goodsReceipt:create`, `purchaseReturn:create`. Every write also needs the read of what it answers with.
- **Suppliers** (migration 0053): a name unique per hospital whatever the capitals (409 `supplier_name_taken`), an optional GSTIN (`^[0-9]{2}[A-Z0-9]{13}$`, checked by the schema and the database), drug licence number, phone, email and address; edited at a version (null clears a field), archived and restored. One with an order that may still bring goods (draft, submitted, approved, partly received) is not archived (409 `supplier_has_open_orders`). `GET /purchasing/suppliers` takes `q` (a part of the name, tidied, matched as text): suppliers say nothing about a patient, so the list is a `GET`.
- **An order's states** are draft → submitted → approved → partly received → received, with cancelled (from draft, submitted or approved, nothing received) and closed (from partly received, the rest will not come), each with a note. The database's trigger refuses any other move, and any change of the supplier, place or note once submitted. Its lines are written only while it is a draft (a trigger, and `DELETE` granted for replacing them): `PATCH` sends all of them to replace them. Each item once per order.
- **Two people:** whoever drafted an order, last changed what it orders (`edited_by`, set by every change of a draft) or submitted it never approves it (409 `own_order`, and the check `purchase_orders_two_people`), so nobody approves what they wrote by having a colleague press Submit. A store with one store keeper needs the admin to approve. Approving refuses a supplier archived since (409 `supplier_archived`) and moves nothing in stock. Who submitted, approved or ended an order, and why, never change once recorded (the trigger).
- **Numbers** are `PO-`, `GRN-` and `PRN-` and six digits, per hospital, from `purchasing_counters` (one row per kind, taken with an upsert that holds it until the transaction ends).
- **A goods receipt** is against an approved or partly received order (409 `order_status`), at a place in use (the order's when not sent). Each line is an order line's item in a batch as printed on the pack: the batch is made through inventory's `stockReceive` when the item has no batch of that number, added to when it has one of the same expiry date (409 `batch_exists` for another), and refused when already expired at the hospital (409 `batch_expired`). Each batch once per receipt. **A unit never costs more than the order line's agreed price** (409 `cost_above_order`): the approval covered that price, so a dearer delivery needs a new order; a cheaper one is received at what it cost. An item archived since the order is 409 `item_archived`. The order is locked, so two receipts take their turn, and more than an order line still waits for is 409 `over_receipt` (tested: two receipts of the last units, one 201 and one 409). Each line is a ledger entry (source and reason `receipt`), named by the line with a foreign key, so a receipt and its stock cannot disagree. The order becomes partly received or received, and its version moves with every receipt, so a step read before one is refused (409 `conflict`). **Units sent back are not waited for again:** what an order line still waits for is what was ordered less what was received, whatever went back since, so replacements of returned goods come on a new order (a follow-up may count returns back in). Posted at once by one person: a receipt records what was delivered. A correction is a return or an adjustment; receipts and their lines are never changed (triggers, and `TRUNCATE` refused).
- **Costing method:** each batch carries its own cost, the unit cost of the receipt that made it (`stock_batches.cost_minor`); a later receipt into the same batch records its own cost on its line. Stock is tracked by batch, so a batch's value is its units at its cost: no average or FIFO cost is kept. Costs are before tax; GST on purchases is not recorded yet.
- **A return to the supplier** is of a goods receipt's lines, each once, no more than the line brought in less what went back already (409 `over_return`; the receipt is held with an advisory lock, as the app may not lock a row it may not update), from a place in use (the receipt's when not sent), with a reason (`expired`, `damaged`, `wrong_item`, `other` with a note). Units leave through inventory's `stockOut` with `expiredAllowed`, the source and reason `purchase_return`: expired units are the usual thing sent back. More than the batch has at the place is 409 `insufficient_stock`. It may emit `stock.low`.
- **Lock order:** the order (or the receipt, by an advisory lock taken once it is found), the place, then each item's stock in id order, as every movement of several items takes them (inventory's notes), so a receipt and a return of the same items never wait for each other.
- **Money:** amounts are minor units in `bigint`; totals come back as JavaScript numbers, exact below 2^53, far above any real order (a unit's cost is at most 10^11 minor units and a line at most a million units, so one line could pass it in theory: accepted).
- **Events** (ids only): `purchase.order_approved` (the order, its supplier and place), `stock.received` (the receipt, its order, supplier and place), `stock.returned` (the return, its receipt, supplier, place and reason).
- **Audit:** `supplier` (`create`, `update`, `archive`, `restore`), `purchase_order` (`create`, then `update` on each change or step, a receipt included), `goods_receipt` (`create`), `purchase_return` (`create`). Reads are not audited: no patient data.
- **Idempotency:** every POST that creates or moves something takes a key and keeps the whole answer (`body: 'full'`): names of items, suppliers, places and staff, batches and costs, no patient data. Archiving and restoring a supplier take none, as doing either twice changes nothing; a PATCH takes the version instead.
- **Lists** are `GET`s, newest first (suppliers by name), with `total` on the first page; an id in a query that is not a uuid matches nothing.
- **Not here:** tax on order lines, free goods and discounts, receiving without an order, printing an order, stock valuation reports, paying suppliers (accounting is out of scope).
