# Wards, rooms and beds API

What was decided for this feature's API. Moved out of `apps/api/CLAUDE.md` (#370); Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. The web side's notes are in `apps/web/src/features/wards/CLAUDE.md`.

- **`features/wards`** is the hospital's wards, the rooms of each and the beds of each room, and the bed categories that decide what a day in a bed is charged as. A bed carries its status and nothing about a patient. Behind the `wards` module flag, with `ward:read` for those who admit, nurse and round, and `ward:manage` for the admin.
- **Every change within a ward locks the ward first** (`lockWard`), so changes to its rooms and beds take their turn: a room name and a bed number are one in the ward, and what can be archived depends on the other rows. A room or a bed is looked up inside the ward named, never by its id alone.
- **Nothing of an archived ward is changed** (409 `ward_archived`); the ward itself can still be read, changed and restored. An archived room or bed is restored before it is changed (409 `room_archived`, `bed_archived`).
- **A bed is `available`, `occupied`, `cleaning` or `blocked`.** From this API it is only blocked (from available, with a reason from a list and an optional note) and put back in use. Occupied and being cleaned are set by admissions and housekeeping (R2), which must lock the ward first (`lockWard`) and then change the bed: the lock order is always the ward, then a category, then beds. A trigger refuses a patient in a bed of an archived ward or room whatever the code does. A change of status is one statement that names the status it starts from (`moveBed`), so of two people making it only one does.
- **What a patient is in is never archived:** a bed (409 `bed_occupied`, and a check constraint), a room (409 `room_has_occupied_beds`) and a ward (409 `ward_has_occupied_beds`, and the trigger). The beds are held while this is decided (`for update`), not only read, so a bed given a patient at the same moment waits or is waited for, and the refusal is always the route's 409, never the database's. A room is archived with its beds and comes back without them. Archiving never changes a bed's status, and a trigger refuses a change of status on an archived bed: a bed being cleaned or blocked is still that when it is restored.
- **The note of a block is free text, so it is treated as possibly sensitive:** it is in the ward's answer and nowhere else. Not in an audit entry, not in an event (the event carries the reason, which is from a list), not in a log.
- **Adding a room or a bed keeps nothing of its answer** (`idempotent: { body: 'none' }`): the answer is the whole ward, with the notes of its blocked beds. A repeat with the same key adds nothing and is answered with the ward as it is then. Adding a ward or a category is replayed with the whole answer, which holds no note.
- **A ward has at most 200 rooms and 1000 beds,** archived ones counted (409 `ward_room_limit`, `ward_bed_limit`): every change answers with the whole ward, and nothing of it is ever deleted.
- **Whoever changes a ward needs `ward:read` as well as `ward:manage`,** because the answer is the ward.
- **The tariff is on the category,** as a link to a bed-day service of the hospital's price catalog that is in use when the link is made; the one a category already has may have been archived since. The same holds for a ward's department and a bed's category. A category a bed in use has is not archived (409 `bed_category_in_use`); giving a bed a category holds the category (`for share`), so it is not archived at the same moment.
- **An admission will keep the category and the service of its bed** as they were when the patient was given it, so a change here applies to stays that start after it. That is the admission module's to store.
- **Rooms and beds are put in order in the route,** the way people count them ("Bed 2" before "Bed 10"): a ward has a bounded number. The list of wards is paged, so it is in the database's order of the lower-cased name.
- **The list of wards is a `GET` with `q` in the address:** a ward's name says nothing of a patient (Searches in the root `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Changes are audited** (`bed_category`, `ward`, `room`, `bed`, with `block` and `unblock` as actions of their own) and emit `master.changed`, an update with the names of the fields that changed. Each bed archived with its room has its own entry and event. Archiving a ward tells of the ward only: its rooms and beds stay as they are.
