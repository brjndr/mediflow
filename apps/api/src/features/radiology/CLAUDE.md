# Radiology API

What was decided for this feature's API (#96, RD-01; reports #97, RD-02; uploads, the rest of BE-20, are #99). Claude Code loads it when a file of this folder is read. The web side's notes are in `apps/web/src/features/radiology/CLAUDE.md`.

- **`features/radiology`** is the worklist of imaging orders and their studies: scheduled for a slot, then performed, then reported (#97). Behind the `radiology` module flag, which the tenant config already listed. Image uploads are not here yet (#99).
- **Permissions and defaults** (the "Imaging (perform, report)" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`): `imagingStudy:read`, `imagingStudy:schedule`, `imagingStudy:perform`, `imagingReport:write` (the draft) and `imagingReport:sign` (signing and addenda) for the radiologist and the admin; reading a report is `imagingStudy:read`, and every report route also needs it. The radiologist also gained `template:read` (#97), to fill a report from a template. The admin holds all, as in the clinical module and the laboratory (`grantsWithin`). A doctor sees the order's status in the consultation (`order:read`); the signed report there (the matrix's "view results") is a follow-up.
- **Only the scope `all` reaches anything,** as in the laboratory: radiology works the whole hospital's orders, so a narrower scope sees an empty worklist and the same 404 as another hospital's record.
- **The modality is the service's** (`services.modality`, migration 0048: nullable, only for the category imaging, set in the services catalog), copied onto the study when it is first scheduled, so a study keeps it when the catalog changes. An order not yet scheduled reads its service's as it is now; a service with none is `other`. The worklist filters on it.
- **The state machine:** to schedule (no study, or one unscheduled) -> scheduled -> performed. One study per order (`imaging_studies_one_per_order`), made by the first schedule; rescheduling and unscheduling change its time, at its version (409 `conflict`; the first schedule takes none). Performing needs it scheduled (409 `invalid_transition`). **What it does to the order:** the first schedule accepts an order that was only placed; performing moves it in progress; signing the report (#97) completes it (`moveImagingOrder` to completed, which emits `order.completed`). Unscheduling leaves the order accepted, as the order's own state machine never goes back. The doctor may cancel until it is performed (cancelling is from placed or accepted), and a cancelled or completed order leaves the worklist and refuses every change (409 `invalid_transition`).
- **Performing needs a schedule first,** as the issue's flow is ordered, scheduled, performed: a walk-in X-ray is scheduled for the slot under way, then performed. Performing straight from the order is the change if a hospital asks.
- **Performing early is allowed:** a study may be marked performed before its slot (the patient came early), and `performed_at` is when it was recorded. A performed study never changes, so one marked by mistake stays; a limit to slots that have begun is the change if a hospital asks.
- **A slot is a start time on a 15-minute step** (422 `invalid_slot`), from the start of the step under way to the end of the 90th day ahead at the hospital (422 `outside_window`; `scheduleWindowEnd` works the midnight out in SQL from the hospital's timezone, so the whole last day the form offers is open). The slot a study already has is never refused when sent again, even once it has begun, with no length and **no room or machine yet**: two studies may share a slot, and nothing refuses a machine booked twice. A list of rooms with an exclusion constraint is the follow-up.
- **Radiology never writes the orders table.** It reads an imaging order and moves it through `imagingOrder` and `moveImagingOrder` from `features/clinical`, which audit the order and share `moveOrderOf` with the laboratory. While radiology is on, the clinical route `POST /orders/:id/status` refuses an imaging order (409 `imaging_order`); with it off, that route moves them as before. An order moved in progress before radiology was switched on, with no performed study, is listed as performed.
- **Every change locks the order first, then its study**, so two first schedules at the same moment are a 200 and a 409 `conflict` (the second finds the study and sent no version), and the unique constraint is the backstop.
- **The database holds the rules too** (migration 0048): the study names its order, encounter, patient and service together (a foreign key to `orders (tenant_id, id, encounter_id, patient_id, service_id)`, a unique constraint the migration adds); a trigger keeps what a study is of and its modality, and refuses any change once performed; performed needs scheduled (a check); there is no DELETE grant.
- **The worklist** (`GET /radiology/worklist`) finds the open imaging orders through `orders_tenant_imaging_open`, then works out each one's stage and sort time in SQL and sorts and pages on those, so a page (and the count of the first) costs in proportion to all the hospital's open imaging orders, and no index serves the order or the cursor. That holds while they are a few hundred; signing a report (#97) completes its order, which leaves this query. A query per stage on its own index (`imaging_studies (tenant_id, scheduled_at)` for the scheduled stage) is the follow-up, measured before the pilot, as for the laboratory's worklist. To schedule by priority (stat, urgent, routine) then the oldest ordered first; scheduled by slot, soonest first; performed the longest waiting first. `total` on the first page. Holds the patient's name, MRN and date of birth, as `imagingStudy:read` says.
- **Idempotency keys are required** on every POST, and nothing of an answer is kept (`body: 'none'`): a repeat finds the study by its order and answers with it as it is now, audited as a read.
- **Audit:** `imaging_study` (`create` on the first schedule, `update`, `read`), with the study's id; the worklist audits `read` of each order it shows and of each study in it. The migration builds its unique constraint and index on `orders` in the transaction, which holds writes to `orders` while it runs: fine at today's sizes; a large table needs them `CONCURRENTLY` in a `-- migrate:no-transaction` file. `scheduled_by` and `performed_by` name the user from the session, with no foreign key, as the laboratory's columns. **Events** carry ids and the modality, never the slot, a name or an instruction: `imaging.scheduled` (each schedule and reschedule), `imaging.unscheduled` and `imaging.performed` (the study, order, encounter, patient, service and modality).
- **Reports (#97, migration 0049).** One per study (`imaging_reports_one_per_study`), naming its study, order, encounter and patient together (a foreign key to `imaging_studies (tenant_id, id, order_id, encounter_id, patient_id)`). Three rich text parts (technique, findings, impression; `core/http/rich-text.ts`, reduced on the way in and out), links (`[{ label, url }]`, at most 10, https with a host, no credentials and no query or fragment parameter named as a secret (`token`, `sig`, `key`, `auth`, `pass`, `secret`, `credential`, `session` in the name), 400 `invalid_link`, since a signed report keeps it for ever; the label tidied), and the template and version the findings were filled from (422 `template_not_found` unless it is an `imaging_report` template of the hospital at that version, not archived unless it is the one the draft already names; checked through `isTemplateVersion` from `features/templates`; sent together or not at all; the last filled from is kept). `PUT /radiology/orders/:id/report` saves the whole draft: the first save makes it (needs the study performed and the order in progress, 409 `invalid_transition`), later ones need its version (409 `conflict`); anyone with `imagingReport:write` edits it. `POST /radiology/orders/:id/report/sign` at the version needs findings and an impression (422 `report_incomplete`) and no placeholder in double braces in any part (422 `unfilled_placeholders`, `placeholdersIn` from `features/templates`), then completes the order and emits `imaging.reported`. Once signed, a save or a second sign is 409 `report_signed`; a trigger refuses any change to a signed report and to what any report is of, and there is no DELETE grant. **Addenda** (`POST /radiology/orders/:id/report/addenda`): to a signed report only (409 `report_not_signed`, and the trigger), text not empty and a reason from a list (`correction`, `additional_finding`, `clarification`, `comparison`, `other`), signed by the caller; append-only, the trigger refusing UPDATE and DELETE to the owner too, and no grant for either. Emits `imaging.addendum_added`. `GET /radiology/orders/:id/report` answers the report with its addenda, oldest first, or 404 `report_not_found`; **a draft only to whoever holds `imagingReport:write` or `imagingReport:sign` with the scope `all`** (read from the policy, as the route declares neither), 404 to anyone else, as an unverified lab result is withheld. The admin holds sign by default, as it holds every permission of the module. Every write locks the order, then the study, then the report; each needs an idempotency key and keeps nothing of its answer (`body: 'none'`): a repeat answers the report as it is now, audited as a read. Audit: `imaging_report` (`create`, `update` for each save, the sign and each addendum, `read`). Events carry ids only (report, study, order, encounter, patient, service, and the addendum's), never the text, a name or a link.
- **The reported stage** of the worklist is a query of its own (`REPORTED_ITEMS`): the signed reports, newest signed first, through `imaging_reports_tenant_signed` (`tenant_id, signed_at desc, order_id desc`), with its own rank in the cursor, so a cursor of another stage is 400 `invalid_cursor` there and the other way round. It has no `total`: the signed reports only grow, and counting them on every first page would cost in proportion to the whole history. Every work item now carries `patientSex` (to fill a template) and its report's status, and when signed, when and by whom.
- **Not here:** image uploads through signed URLs with a malware scan (#99), rooms and machines, a study's length, a day view of the schedule, and charging (billing reacts to `order.placed` and `order.completed`).
