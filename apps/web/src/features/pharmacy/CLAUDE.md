# Pharmacy: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**The prescription queue and dispensing (#100, web and API):** `pharmacy`, in the navigation after radiology, behind the `pharmacy` switch and `dispense:read`. Works at every width.

- **Queue** (`pharmacy`): a `DataGrid` of the prescriptions with a medication line still to dispense, the oldest first (**To dispense**, the default), or those with none left and some dispensed, the newest first (**Dispensed**), prescribed today or within the last 3, 7 (the default), 30 or 90 days at the hospital. Each names the patient (the MRN masked), how many medicines are left of how many, its progress in words (not started, partly dispensed, dispensed) and who prescribed it when. **Dispense** (or **View**) opens the prescription.
- **Prescription** (`pharmacy/prescriptions/:prescriptionId`): the patient (name and masked MRN), the prescriber and when, the patient's allergies (or why they cannot be read), the advice, a note when the doctor revised it, **Dispense from** (the places that keep stock, the first pharmacy counter chosen), its lines (medicine, how to take it, prescribed, dispensed so far, status in words), and what was dispensed (the item, a substitute's reason, the batches with their expiry, who, when and from where), each with **Print slip**.
- **Dispense** (`dispense:create`, on a line still open with something left) opens a panel in place: **Give** (the medicine as prescribed, or a generic substitute, each with its units here), the reason for a substitute, and a field per batch in date at the place, nearest expiry first, filled with what is left to give from the nearest expiry on. **Mark complete** (`dispense:create`) completes a line in progress when nothing more will be given.

## Decided

- **A module of its own, `pharmacy`,** with the switch `pharmacy` and its own permissions: `dispense:read` (the queue, a prescription, printing a slip) and `dispense:create` (dispensing and completing), for the pharmacist and the admin, the "Pharmacy dispense and sales" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`. The pharmacist also gained `allergy:read` (#100) for the warning. The mock's hospital has a `pharmacist` role and user (`USER_IDS.pharmacist`, `pharmacy@sample-hospital.test`).
- **Stock leaves through the inventory ledger only** (the API's `stockOut`), with the reason `dispense`; the inventory ledger shows it as "Dispensed". **The places are inventory's** (`usePlaces` from `@/features/inventory`, read only for `stock:read` with the inventory switch on): without them the screen says so and nothing can be dispensed. A pharmacy hospital keeps inventory on.
- **Nearest expiry first, never an expired batch:** the API offers only batches in date at the hospital today with stock at the place, nearest expiry first; the form fills them in that order with what is left to give. The pharmacist may change any batch's units. An expired batch is refused by the API too (409 `batch_expired`).
- **Full or partial:** a line with a prescribed quantity is completed when that much is given, and refuses more (409 `over_quantity`, and the form checks it on sending). A partial dispense leaves the line in progress; another dispense gives more, or **Mark complete** closes it with less. A line with no quantity prescribed stays in progress after each dispense until completed. Once in progress the doctor can no longer cancel the line (the order's state machine).
- **Exactly once:** every POST carries an idempotency key, one per body, so a repeated press or a retry after a lost answer is one dispense; a dispense is made from the line's version and the units given so far (`expectedDispensedQuantity`), and a second dispense made from the same reading is refused (409 `conflict`), so two counters cannot give the same line twice, also once it is in progress (where its version no longer changes).
- **Generic substitution** is another medicine in use of the same generic name, form and strength (any brand), with a reason that is recorded with the dispense, shown on it and printed on the slip.
- **The allergy warning warns, never refuses**, as prescribing does (#81): a recorded allergy whose substance word begins a word of the medicine's name (`matchesAllergy` from `@/features/clinical`) is said on the panel with an icon, and sending asks once ("Dispense with this allergy warning?", focus on its heading): "Dispense anyway" or "Review". So does a check that could not be made (no `allergy:read`, triage off, a failed read, still reading, or never asked): "Dispense without an allergy check?". Names only, no drug classes (#380).
- **A print is recorded before anything prints:** **Print slip** sends `POST /pharmacy/dispenses/:id/print` with a new key per press; the slip (`DispenseSlip`, the letterhead, the patient, the item and quantity, a substitute's reason, how to take it, the batches with their expiry, the advice, who and when) is drawn by `PrintSheet` only once that is answered, and dropped when the dialog closes.
- **Every address holds ids only.** The queue is a `GET` with a state and a number of days: nothing typed says anything about a patient. The API audits every prescription listed or read.
- **Focus.** The panel's heading takes focus when it opens (`usePanelFocus`), focus goes back to its button when it closes, or to the status line when that went away (a dispensed line's button is `aria-disabled`, still there). A refusal is an alert in the panel, which takes focus; the dispense lives in the panel above the form, so the refusal stays said when the form starts over. After `conflict`, `invalid_transition` or `order_not_found` nothing more can be sent from it. A refusal of stock (`insufficient_stock`, `batch_expired`) reads the batches again (their own query key, apart from the lists the dispense reads again) and the form starts over from them. While the allergy question is asked the fields are locked, so what is sent is what was asked about; its answer returns focus to Dispense. Only items with stock in date at the place are offered; with none, the panel says so (or that the medicine was archived with no substitute).

## Known gaps

- No counter sales or controlled drug register (#101), no returns (#104), no charge for a dispense yet (billing reacts to `pharmacy.dispensed`).
- The queue has no search by patient (the API takes a `patientId`; a picker is a follow-up).
- Allergy checks match names only, not drug classes (#380).
- The place chosen is not remembered between visits to the screen.
- Completing a line short of its prescribed quantity records no reason.

The inventory mock adds stock at the pharmacy counter for it: paracetamol in `PCM24A` (4 units, expiring sooner) and `PCM25B` (100), an expired `PCM23X` never offered, another brand `PMD25C` (ParaMed, 30), and cetirizine in `CTZ25A` (50). Tests read it with `mockDispenses`. The clinic has the pharmacy off.
