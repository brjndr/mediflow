# Billing: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**The invoice builder (#109), web only, against the mock.** The billing API (#114) implements these endpoints (`GET /encounters/{id}/charges`, `GET` and `POST /encounters/{id}/invoices`, `GET` and `PATCH /invoices/{id}`, `POST /invoices/{id}/issue`) and more (payments, refunds, credit notes, the cash register: no screen yet); its decisions are in `apps/api/src/features/billing/CLAUDE.md`. `mocks/handlers/billing.ts` still answers them for the default mock run; keep it matching the API (an invoice also carries `paidMinor` and `creditedMinor`, a charge may carry a `flag`).

Behind the `billing` switch, `charge:read`, `charge:create`, `invoice:read`, `invoice:create`, `invoice:issue`, `invoice:print`, `invoice:adjust`, `payment:read`, `payment:create`, `payment:refund` and `creditNote:create` (the mock's admin holds all of them). Works at every width.

- **Entry:** "Create invoice" on the consultation, a contribution to the new slot `encounter.detail.actions` (given `encounterId`, `patientId` and `status`), for `invoice:create` and `charge:read`; none on a cancelled visit. It opens `billing/new?encounterId=`: the address holds the visit's id and nothing about the patient.
- **The builder (`InvoiceBuilder`):** the visit's pending charges, each put on the bill or left off it by a row action (never a checkbox: the grid's actions stay focusable and say when they do not apply), lines typed in (what it is for, a quantity, the price of one) and the total, worked out on minor units and shown in the hospital's currency. A new bill holds every pending charge; a draft holds what it held. "Save draft" keeps it; "Issue invoice" (for `invoice:issue`) saves it and gives it its number.
- **A draft is `billing/invoices/:invoiceId`** (for `invoice:read`): the builder again, to change and issue. An issued invoice is read-only there: number, lines and total. A visit's unfinished drafts are offered on `billing/new` to continue.
- **The invoice list (#110)** is `billing/invoices` (navigation entry "Billing", for `invoice:read`): a `DataGrid` of the hospital's invoices, newest first, drafts included, the number first (a draft says "Draft"), then patient, date, total, paid and status. Found by number, patient name or MRN, and by status; one server page at a time (25, 50 or 100), with `total` on the first. The term is component state only and goes in the body of `POST /invoices/search`, never the address (the list is that search with an empty term). The server has no sort; the grid offers none.
- **Printing (#110)** is on an issued or paid invoice's page, for `invoice:print`: the invoice is read afresh, `POST /invoices/{id}/print` records the print (audit action `print`, a new idempotency key per press), and only then the print sheet shows and the browser's print dialog opens (`useInvoicePrinting`, as the laboratory's report). A refusal prints nothing (409 `not_printable` for a draft or a void invoice). The sheet has the letterhead, number, patient, lines, total, credited, paid and balance due, and who printed it. The page itself shows the paid and credited sums from the invoice. A PDF made on the server is not built: the browser prints.
- **Payments (#111)** are on the page of an invoice that is not a draft (`PaymentPanel`), against `GET` and `POST /invoices/{id}/payments`: the balance due, a form for `payment:create` (only while the invoice is `issued` with something due) and the list of payments and refunds for `payment:read`. The amount starts as everything due, so paying in full is one press and a part payment is typing less; the modes are cash, card, UPI, bank transfer and other, with an optional reference (plain text, 100 characters). The amount is turned into minor units on its digits (`toMinor`), refused when it is nothing, above the balance or has a comma; the server still decides (`overpayment`, `no_open_register`, `invoice_not_payable` have their own messages). A saved payment is announced in an always-present status line, and when nothing is due the form goes and focus moves to that line.
- **Credit notes (#112)** are on the page of an invoice that is not a draft (`CreditNotePanel`), against `GET` and `POST /invoices/{id}/credit-notes`. An issued invoice is never edited (no builder here, the API trigger and `invoice_not_draft`); a mistake is corrected by a credit note with a required reason (plain text, 200 characters). The list is for `invoice:read`, the form for `creditNote:create`, offered while the invoice is issued or paid and something is unpaid. The amount starts as everything unpaid and is limited to it, since the API refuses a credit that would leave more paid than owed (`refund_first`): once all is paid the panel says to refund first (the refund form is in Payments above it). Crediting everything makes the invoice `void` (a notice shows) and its charges pending again. Same shape as payments: one idempotency key per body, `aria-disabled` while sending, an always-present status line that takes focus when the form goes, and the reason never kept in the mutation cache (`gcTime: 0`). `credit_exceeds`, `refund_first` and `invoice_not_creditable` have their own messages.

- **Pending charges and bill assembly (#115):** the charge grid names where each charge came from (service, order, dispensed medicine, bed, procedure, typed in) and, in words, any flag the API put on it (`service_archived`, `no_price`, `return_review`), so the desk sees what needs a look before issuing. "Charge a service" (`AddChargePanel`, for `charge:create`) searches the catalog with `ServiceSearch` (every category; its `category` is now optional) and posts the service with a quantity ("How many") to `POST /encounters/{id}/charges`. Only the service and the number are sent: the server prices it from the price list in effect, so a bill is built from the charge ledger and typed lines only. The new charge appears among the pending ones and goes on the bill being built (`onAdded` adds its id to the choice, leaving earlier choices as they were). One idempotency key per body (`useAddCharge`, `gcTime: 0`), `aria-disabled` while sending, an always-present status line. `service_archived`, `no_price`, `service_not_found` and `encounter_cancelled` have their own messages. The panel needs `service:read` and the `services` switch to find anything (the search says so otherwise).

## Decided

- **A payment is sent once.** `useRecordPayment` keeps one `Idempotency-Key` per request body until a payment is recorded, so a double press or a retry after a lost answer records one payment; another amount or mode is another key. The button is `aria-disabled` while sending. Every payment answer re-reads the invoice, the payments and the lists. The sending (`useRecordPayment`, so its key) and the message live in `PaymentPanel`, above the form, which is made again whenever the balance changes: a refusal that changed the balance is still shown and a key still unanswered survives. Focus moves to the status line only once it holds the message. The panel shows for every issued invoice (void ones list what was paid); a refund reads as a negative amount.
- **Refunds (#444)** are in the payments panel (`RefundForm`), for `payment:refund` and `payment:read`, while the invoice is `issued` or `paid` and some payment still holds money. A refund is of one payment (`POST /payments/{id}/refunds`): the form picks it (the most recent first), the amount starts as all that payment still holds (its amount less the refunds whose `refundOf` is it, in `lib/refundable.ts`) and a reason (plain text, 200 characters) is required. A cash refund comes out of the caller's open register session (`no_open_register`); `refund_exceeds` re-reads the payments. One idempotency key per payment and body (`useRefundPayment`, `gcTime: 0` so the reason is not kept). The form is made again whenever what a payment holds changes, so after a part refund focus moves to the status line (`PaymentPanel`). After a paid invoice is refunded it is `issued` again, and a credit note can take the money refunded.
- **Shared by the three panels:** `lib/panel-helpers.ts` has `amountText`, `refusalText` (the API's refusal under `<prefix>.refused.<code>`, else the generic message) and `useFocusWhenFormGoes`. A fourth money form uses them rather than copying.
- **Not here:** the cash register screens (cash needs an open register session, opened elsewhere; until its screen exists the message says so). Proposed as a follow-up.

- **A slot of core, added for it:** `encounter.detail.actions` on the consultation, because the desk's way into billing is a visit. The clinical module renders it and knows nothing of billing. `useEncounter` is exported from the clinical `index.ts` for the builder's header.
- **An invoice is made of charges and typed lines only.** A module never writes an invoice; charges are posted by the API from events (#114). The typed line (`source: manual`) has a description and a price typed by hand, in the hospital's currency, turned into minor units on its digits (`toMinor`: "12,50" is refused). A line from the price catalog is a charge posted by hand (#115, above), never a typed line; discounts are #116 and tax #119.
- **A charge sits in at most one draft.** The server marks it (`invoiceId`) and refuses a second draft with `charge_unavailable`; the screen then reads the list again and keeps what was chosen. Issuing makes the charges `invoiced` and the invoice read-only forever; a version conflict (`version_conflict`) keeps the person's choices and sends the newer version on the next press.
- **Every POST carries an idempotency key,** one per body; a double press makes one draft. The first save of a new bill moves to the draft's own page with a fixed-word notice in the navigation state (`saved: true`), never the record.
- **Typed lines need `invoice:adjust`,** since they skip the price list and the charge ledger: without it the form is not offered (a draft that already has some keeps them). The server (#114) works out every line and the total itself, and the client's total is display only; it audits create, update and issue and emits `invoice.issued` through the outbox on issue (ids, never the lines). A cancelled visit has nothing to bill (409 `encounter_cancelled`).
- **Issuing a new bill is two calls** (save, then issue). The draft made by the first is kept: if the issue fails the screen goes to the draft's page, never making a second one. Whoever holds `invoice:issue` without `invoice:create` sees a draft read-only; issuing needs both until the API says otherwise.
- **The route also needs `encounter:read`** (the visit's name in its header). The patient's name and MRN are shown whole on an invoice, as on the consultation: a bill must name who it is for.
- **No appointment entry yet.** The issue says "from a visit or appointment"; an appointment has no encounter until it is started, and `GET /encounters` has no `appointmentId` filter, so the appointment's screen cannot find its visit. Billing an appointment goes through its consultation until that filter exists (proposed as a follow-up).

## Mock

`mocks/handlers/billing.ts` also answers `POST /encounters/{id}/charges`, pricing from the services mock (`mockServicePrice`). Three pending charges on the hospital's open consultation `enc_h_1` (a consultation 500.00, a blood count 350.00, two strips of paracetamol at 25.50) and one on the clinic's `enc_c_1` (150.00 AED); invoices numbered INV-0001 in order of issue. Tests read them with `mockCharges`, `mockInvoices`, `mockPayments` (a refund has `kind: refund` and `refundOf`) and `mockCreditNotes`; `setMockRegisterOpen(false)` makes a cash payment answer 409 `no_open_register`. An invoice's `paidMinor` is worked out from the payments.
