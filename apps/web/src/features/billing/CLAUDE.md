# Billing: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**The invoice builder (#109), web only, against the mock.** The billing API (#114) implements these endpoints (`GET /encounters/{id}/charges`, `GET` and `POST /encounters/{id}/invoices`, `GET` and `PATCH /invoices/{id}`, `POST /invoices/{id}/issue`) and more (payments, refunds, credit notes, the cash register); its decisions are in `apps/api/src/features/billing/CLAUDE.md`. `mocks/handlers/billing.ts` still answers them for the default mock run; keep it matching the API (an invoice also carries `paidMinor` and `creditedMinor`, a charge may carry a `flag`).

Behind the `billing` switch, `charge:read`, `charge:create`, `invoice:read`, `invoice:create`, `invoice:issue`, `invoice:print`, `invoice:adjust`, `payment:read`, `payment:create`, `payment:refund`, `creditNote:create`, `cashRegister:read`, `cashRegister:manage`, `taxRate:manage`, `discount:apply`, `discount:approve`, `discount:manage` and `package:manage` (the mock's admin holds all of them). Works at every width.

- **Entry:** "Create invoice" on the consultation, a contribution to the new slot `encounter.detail.actions` (given `encounterId`, `patientId` and `status`), for `invoice:create` and `charge:read`; none on a cancelled visit. It opens `billing/new?encounterId=`: the address holds the visit's id and nothing about the patient.
- **The builder (`InvoiceBuilder`):** the visit's pending charges, each put on the bill or left off it by a row action (never a checkbox: the grid's actions stay focusable and say when they do not apply), lines typed in (what it is for, a quantity, the price of one) and the total, worked out on minor units and shown in the hospital's currency. A new bill holds every pending charge; a draft holds what it held. "Save draft" keeps it; "Issue invoice" (for `invoice:issue`) saves it and gives it its number.
- **A draft is `billing/invoices/:invoiceId`** (for `invoice:read`): the builder again, to change and issue. An issued invoice is read-only there: number, lines and total. A visit's unfinished drafts are offered on `billing/new` to continue.
- **The invoice list (#110)** is `billing/invoices` (navigation entry "Billing", for `invoice:read`): a `DataGrid` of the hospital's invoices, newest first, drafts included, the number first (a draft says "Draft"), then patient, date, total, paid and status. Found by number, patient name or MRN, and by status; one server page at a time (25, 50 or 100), with `total` on the first. The term is component state only and goes in the body of `POST /invoices/search`, never the address (the list is that search with an empty term). The server has no sort; the grid offers none.
- **Printing (#110)** is on an issued or paid invoice's page, for `invoice:print`: the invoice is read afresh, `POST /invoices/{id}/print` records the print (audit action `print`, a new idempotency key per press), and only then the print sheet shows and the browser's print dialog opens (`useInvoicePrinting`, as the laboratory's report). A refusal prints nothing (409 `not_printable` for a draft or a void invoice). The sheet has the letterhead, number, patient, lines, total, credited, paid and balance due, and who printed it. The page itself shows the paid and credited sums from the invoice. A PDF made on the server is not built: the browser prints.
- **Payments (#111)** are on the page of an invoice that is not a draft (`PaymentPanel`), against `GET` and `POST /invoices/{id}/payments`: the balance due, a form for `payment:create` (only while the invoice is `issued` with something due) and the list of payments and refunds for `payment:read`. The amount starts as everything due, so paying in full is one press and a part payment is typing less; the modes are cash, card, UPI, bank transfer and other, with an optional reference (plain text, 100 characters). The amount is turned into minor units on its digits (`toMinor`), refused when it is nothing, above the balance or has a comma; the server still decides (`overpayment`, `no_open_register`, `invoice_not_payable` have their own messages). A saved payment is announced in an always-present status line, and when nothing is due the form goes and focus moves to that line.
- **Credit notes (#112)** are on the page of an invoice that is not a draft (`CreditNotePanel`), against `GET` and `POST /invoices/{id}/credit-notes`. An issued invoice is never edited (no builder here, the API trigger and `invoice_not_draft`); a mistake is corrected by a credit note with a required reason (plain text, 200 characters). The list is for `invoice:read`, the form for `creditNote:create`, offered while the invoice is issued or paid and something is unpaid. The amount starts as everything unpaid and is limited to it, since the API refuses a credit that would leave more paid than owed (`refund_first`): once all is paid the panel says to refund first (the refund form is in Payments above it). Crediting everything makes the invoice `void` (a notice shows) and its charges pending again. Same shape as payments: one idempotency key per body, `aria-disabled` while sending, an always-present status line that takes focus when the form goes, and the reason never kept in the mutation cache (`gcTime: 0`). `credit_exceeds`, `refund_first` and `invoice_not_creditable` have their own messages.

- **Pending charges and bill assembly (#115):** the charge grid names where each charge came from (service, order, dispensed medicine, bed, procedure, typed in) and, in words, any flag the API put on it (`service_archived`, `no_price`, `return_review`), so the desk sees what needs a look before issuing. "Charge a service" (`AddChargePanel`, for `charge:create`) searches the catalog with `ServiceSearch` (every category; its `category` is now optional) and posts the service with a quantity ("How many") to `POST /encounters/{id}/charges`. Only the service and the number are sent: the server prices it from the price list in effect, so a bill is built from the charge ledger and typed lines only. The new charge appears among the pending ones and goes on the bill being built (`onAdded` adds its id to the choice, leaving earlier choices as they were). One idempotency key per body (`useAddCharge`, `gcTime: 0`), `aria-disabled` while sending, an always-present status line. `service_archived`, `no_price`, `service_not_found` and `encounter_cancelled` have their own messages. The panel needs `service:read` and the `services` switch to find anything (the search says so otherwise).

- **Tax (#119):** the API works it out (see its notes); the screens show it. The builder estimates it before saving from the rates (`useTaxRates`, for `invoice:read`) and the class on each charge (`lib/tax.ts`, the server's sum in bigint), and shows "Before tax" and "Tax" above the total only when there is some. A saved invoice's page adds a per-line tax column and the same two rows; the print sheet shows the tax by rate ("Tax at 12%"). A typed line has a "Tax class" select (default none). `billing/tax-rates` (`taxRate:manage` and `invoice:read`, linked from the invoice list's header; there is still no settings screen to hold it) edits the four rates as percentages with up to two decimals, read on their digits (`percentToBp`; "12,5" is refused). Saving never alters an issued invoice. Numbers: unchanged (#114), gapless per fiscal year.

- **Discounts (#116):** the builder has a "Discounts" section (`DiscountPanel`, for `discount:apply`; without it the discounts a draft already has are listed and nothing can be changed): pick the whole bill or one line, a percentage (two decimals, read on its digits by `percentToBp`) or an amount (`toMinor`), and a reason from the fixed list; a second discount on the same target replaces the first, and the grid's "Remove discount" row action takes one off. The builder keeps the discounts in state (by charge id or typed line key, and the bill's) and sends them with the draft (`chargeDiscounts`, a `discount` on a typed line, `billDiscount`); nothing is saved until "Save draft". `lib/discount.ts` previews what the server will work out (bigint, the same half-up rounding and largest-remainder spread) so the totals show "Before discount", "Discount", "Before tax", "Tax" and the total; the server's figures win. The hospital's limit (`useDiscountSettings`, for `invoice:read`) decides the note "above the hospital's limit of 5%: someone other than you must approve it". A saved draft whose discount is pending shows "waits for approval", and **Approve discount** only to someone with `discount:approve` other than the person who gave it (`discountAppliedBy` against the session's user; otherwise it says someone else must); issuing a pending bill is refused (`discount_needs_approval`, and the page shows the draft as pending). An issued invoice's page and the print sheet show the discount column and the "Before discount"/"Discount" rows. `billing/discount-limit` (`discount:manage` and `invoice:read`, linked from the invoice list's header) sets the limit as a percentage. One idempotency key per body for the approval and the limit (`gcTime: 0`).

- **Packages (#117):** "Apply a package" (`ApplyPackagePanel`, under "Charge a service", for `charge:create` and `charge:read`) searches only packages with `ServiceSearch category="package"` and posts `{ serviceId }` to `POST /encounters/{id}/packages`; the price is the server's. The package charge goes on the bill being built; the pending charges it replaces are void and leave the grid, so the total drops by them, and a charge for an included service added later is accepted but never shown (it is void, flag `package_covered`, worded "Covered by a package" where a flag is shown). `billing/packages` (`package:manage`, `charge:read` and `service:read`, linked from the invoice list's header, not desktop-only like the tax screen) chooses a package and edits its contents: services added as **included** or **excluded** with two buttons (no select), removed one by one, and "Save contents" sends the whole list (one key per body, `gcTime: 0`, an always-present status line). The refusals `invalid_package_item`, `package_empty`, `package_applied`, `not_a_package` have their own messages. The contents editor lives in billing, not in the services module, so no slot or core change was needed; the services notes say the same ("what it contains belongs with billing").

- **The running bill of an inpatient stay (DS-03, #166), web only, on the mock.** `billing/stays/:admissionId` (`admission:read` and `charge:read`; reached by the admissions list's "Running bill" row action, which needs `charge:read` and the `billing` switch): `GET /admissions/{id}/bill` (planned, `stay-bill.ts`) gives the charge ledger of the stay's encounter added up by source (void charges left out), the bed days to date, the deposits, what was paid on invoices of the stay and the balance. The screen shows the server's figures and works out nothing, so it matches the ledger. Bed days are a line marked `accruing` (worked out from the bed history, not a ledger charge) until the API posts `bed` charges; the mock drops the accrued line once a `bed` charge exists. **Deposits** (`DepositPanel`): `GET`/`POST /admissions/{id}/deposits`, listed for `payment:read`, taken for `payment:create` while admitted (409 `stay_closed` after discharge), never edited or removed; same form kit and `createPaymentSchema` as payments. **Estimate against actual** (`EstimatePanel`): `PUT /admissions/{id}/bill-estimate` (`invoice:create`), said in words as over, under or on the estimate. **Interim bill:** a link to the invoice builder (`billing/new?encounterId=`) for `invoice:create`; no new endpoint. Not built, the API (#169) has nothing: the real endpoints and the `bed` charge posting, setting the deposits against the final invoice, refunding a deposit, and a permission of their own for deposits (they use `payment:*`). The mock is `mocks/handlers/stay-bill.ts` (reads `mockAdmissionOf` from the admissions mock and `mockCharges`); `addMockDeposit` seeds one.

## Decided

- **A payment is sent once.** `useRecordPayment` keeps one `Idempotency-Key` per request body until a payment is recorded, so a double press or a retry after a lost answer records one payment; another amount or mode is another key. The button is `aria-disabled` while sending. Every payment answer re-reads the invoice, the payments and the lists. The sending (`useRecordPayment`, so its key) and the message live in `PaymentPanel`, above the form, which is made again whenever the balance changes: a refusal that changed the balance is still shown and a key still unanswered survives. Focus moves to the status line only once it holds the message. The panel shows for every issued invoice (void ones list what was paid); a refund reads as a negative amount.
- **Refunds (#444)** are in the payments panel (`RefundForm`), for `payment:refund` and `payment:read`, while the invoice is `issued` or `paid` and some payment still holds money. A refund is of one payment (`POST /payments/{id}/refunds`): the form picks it (the most recent first), the amount starts as all that payment still holds (its amount less the refunds whose `refundOf` is it, in `lib/refundable.ts`) and a reason (plain text, 200 characters) is required. A cash refund comes out of the caller's open register session (`no_open_register`); `refund_exceeds` re-reads the payments. One idempotency key per payment and body (`useRefundPayment`, `gcTime: 0` so the reason is not kept). The form is made again whenever what a payment holds changes, so after a part refund focus moves to the status line (`PaymentPanel`). After a paid invoice is refunded it is `issued` again, and a credit note can take the money refunded.
- **Shared by the three panels:** `lib/panel-helpers.ts` has `amountText`, `refusalText` (the API's refusal under `<prefix>.refused.<code>`, else the generic message) and `useFocusWhenFormGoes`. A fourth money form uses them rather than copying.
- **The cash register (#118)** is two screens and a print, against `/cash-registers/sessions`. `billing/register` (`cashRegister:manage`, linked from the invoice list) is the caller's own drawer: with none open, a form for the opening cash (it may be nothing); with one open, what it took by payment mode, the working of the expected cash (opening plus cash taken less cash refunded, `lib/register.ts`) and a form to settle it with the cash counted and an optional note (plain text, 300 characters). The server works out the difference; the screen says it in words (short, over or none), never by colour or sign alone. A settled drawer stays on the screen with its figures and "Print settlement" (`SettlementPrint`: letterhead, cashier, times, the figures, the count, the difference, the note and two lines to sign; it holds money and a cashier's name, so no audit entry), and a new drawer can be opened. `billing/register/sessions` and `.../sessions/:sessionId` (`cashRegister:read`, the admin's) list every session of the hospital, newest first, by cursor page (25, 50 or 100), and show one with its figures and handover note. One idempotency key per body (`useOpenRegister`, `useCloseRegister`, `gcTime: 0` so the note is not kept), `aria-disabled` while sending, an always-present status line that takes focus when the form goes. `register_open`, `register_closed` and `session_not_found` have their own messages; the cash-payment and cash-refund refusals `no_open_register` now point to the register screen. No nav entry of its own: the register is reached from the invoice list's header, so billing stays one entry.
- **Only cash is in a session (known gap).** The API sets a payment's `session_id` only for cash, so a session's `totals` and its expected cash hold cash alone, whatever "by payment mode" suggests: card, UPI and bank payments are on the invoices and not in the drawer's figures. The screen shows whatever modes the API returns, so it follows if the API ever records the others. Proposed as a follow-up: a day-end collections report by mode from the payments themselves (reports module), or the API recording every payment's session.

- **A slot of core, added for it:** `encounter.detail.actions` on the consultation, because the desk's way into billing is a visit. The clinical module renders it and knows nothing of billing. `useEncounter` is exported from the clinical `index.ts` for the builder's header.
- **An invoice is made of charges and typed lines only.** A module never writes an invoice; charges are posted by the API from events (#114). The typed line (`source: manual`) has a description and a price typed by hand, in the hospital's currency, turned into minor units on its digits (`toMinor`: "12,50" is refused). A line from the price catalog is a charge posted by hand (#115, above), never a typed line; discounts are in the builder (above). Tax (#119) is worked out by the server from the class of each charge and typed line.
- **A charge sits in at most one draft.** The server marks it (`invoiceId`) and refuses a second draft with `charge_unavailable`; the screen then reads the list again and keeps what was chosen. Issuing makes the charges `invoiced` and the invoice read-only forever; a version conflict (`version_conflict`) keeps the person's choices and sends the newer version on the next press.
- **Every POST carries an idempotency key,** one per body; a double press makes one draft. The first save of a new bill moves to the draft's own page with a fixed-word notice in the navigation state (`saved: true`), never the record.
- **Typed lines need `invoice:adjust`,** since they skip the price list and the charge ledger: without it the form is not offered (a draft that already has some keeps them). The server (#114) works out every line and the total itself, and the client's total is display only; it audits create, update and issue and emits `invoice.issued` through the outbox on issue (ids, never the lines). A cancelled visit has nothing to bill (409 `encounter_cancelled`).
- **Issuing a new bill is two calls** (save, then issue). The draft made by the first is kept: if the issue fails the screen goes to the draft's page, never making a second one. Whoever holds `invoice:issue` without `invoice:create` sees a draft read-only; issuing needs both until the API says otherwise.
- **Final bill and settlement (DS-04, #167), web only, on the mock.** `SettlementPanel` on the stay's page (`GET /admissions/{id}/settlement`, planned): the stay's invoices against the ledger (charges, not yet invoiced, invoiced, credited, paid, due, the deposit held and the excess), with "The invoices add up to the charges" shown when nothing is unbilled and invoiced equals charges. The server's figures are shown; nothing is worked out. The final invoice is made in the existing builder ("Make the final invoice", `invoice:create`); payments in any mode, refunds and credit notes stay on each invoice's page (row action "Open invoice"). **Deposits:** "Set the deposit against invoice N" (`payment:create`; `POST …/deposits/apply`, the lesser of what is held and what is due, recorded as a payment of method `other`, reference "Deposit", touching no register) and "Pay back the excess deposit" (`DepositRefundForm`, `payment:refund`; `POST …/deposits/refund`: amount up to the excess, mode, required reason; excess exists only when nothing is unbilled or draft). One key per body, `gcTime: 0`, an always-present status line. **Discharge gating by policy:** `billing/discharge-policy` (`invoice:adjust`, linked from the invoice list) sets `off`, `warn` (default in the mock) or `block` (`GET`/`PUT /billing/discharge-policy`). The admissions `DischargePanel` reads the stay's settlement through `useDischargeClearance` (exported from the billing `index.ts`; only with `charge:read` and the billing switch, otherwise the server decides alone): `block` disables the button and lists the reasons, `warn` needs a ticked confirmation, `off` ignores the bill. The gate is in the web app only: the discharge body (real API) carries nothing about it, and enforcing it server side, plus a permission of its own for the policy (it uses `invoice:adjust`), is #169. The mock's bed days stay "accruing" and are not in `unbilledMinor`, so they never hold a discharge. In the running bill, `depositsMinor` is net of refunds and `paidMinor` leaves out deposits set against an invoice, so the balance counts a deposit once.
- **The route also needs `encounter:read`** (the visit's name in its header). The patient's name and MRN are shown whole on an invoice, as on the consultation: a bill must name who it is for.
- **No appointment entry yet.** The issue says "from a visit or appointment"; an appointment has no encounter until it is started, and `GET /encounters` has no `appointmentId` filter, so the appointment's screen cannot find its visit. Billing an appointment goes through its consultation until that filter exists (proposed as a follow-up).

- **The role matrix is one test (#113),** `permissions.test.tsx`: each role's defaults as the API seeds them (`apps/api/src/features/billing/permissions.ts`, kept by hand in the test), and a custom role, on an issued invoice's page (payments list, recording, refund, credit notes, issuing one, printing; a hidden list is never read), the list, the builder and the consultation's entry. Admin all; the receptionist works the bill but has no refund, credit note or typed line; the doctor reads invoices and sees the balance but no payments list and cannot print; the nurse, pharmacist, lab technician, radiologist and store keeper have no way in. `e2e/billing.spec.ts` does the main task with the keyboard alone (invoice, payment, credit note, refund, list and print) and one permission-absent case against the mock's clinic receptionist.

## Mock

The mock also answers `GET`/`PUT /billing/discount-settings` and `POST /invoices/{id}/discount-approval`, and works discounts, their spread over the lines, tax on what is left, the limit and the approval rules as the API does (`priced`, `applyContent` in `mocks/handlers/billing.ts`). The mock's one admin gives a discount and cannot approve it: tests sign in as the hospital's doctor, granted `discount:approve`, for the second person (the receptionist belongs to the clinic).

`mocks/handlers/billing.ts` also answers `GET` and `PUT /billing/tax-rates` (defaults 0, 5, 12 and 18%, per hospital; an issued invoice keeps the rates it was issued at) and tax on lines as the API does. The seeded charges carry no tax class, so the sample totals are untaxed; a test gives one a class (`taxTheBloodCount` in `tax.test.tsx`). It also answers `POST /encounters/{id}/charges`, pricing from the services mock (`mockServicePrice`, which also gives the service's code and category), and the three package endpoints of #117 (contents kept per hospital and package; `setMockPackage` sets them for a test; the mock remembers which service a charge was posted for, so it can void the covered ones as the API does). Three pending charges on the hospital's open consultation `enc_h_1` (a consultation 500.00, a blood count 350.00, two strips of paracetamol at 25.50) and one on the clinic's `enc_c_1` (150.00 AED); invoices numbered INV-0001 in order of issue. Tests read them with `mockCharges`, `mockInvoices`, `mockPayments` (a refund has `kind: refund` and `refundOf`) and `mockCreditNotes`; `setMockRegisterOpen(false)` settles every open drawer and makes a cash payment answer 409 `no_open_register` until one is opened (by default a caller with no drawer gets one, with no cash in it, on their first cash payment, so the mock's payments keep working; the sessions are read with `mockRegisterSessions`). Cash payments and refunds carry the drawer's `sessionId` and are added to its figures, as the API does; other modes are not. An invoice's `paidMinor` is worked out from the payments.

## The mock posts charges from events (#538)

`postMockCharge` (`mocks/handlers/billing.ts`) is what the API's subscribers in `posting.ts` do: a verified lab order that completes is charged as its test's service (`order:<id>`), a dispense is charged per batch at the batch's MRP (`dispense:<id>:<batch>`). Once per source reference, and void when a package covers the service.

- **Letterhead details (#696):** the printed invoice header (`Letterhead`) shows the hospital's address, phone, registration number and GSTIN from the session's tenant; an admin sets them at `/hospital-profile` (`apps/web/src/features/hospital-profile/CLAUDE.md`).
