# Triage: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**Triage (web and API; the API is #79, notes in `apps/api/src/features/triage/CLAUDE.md`):** a nurse records a patient's vitals (blood pressure, pulse, temperature, oxygen saturation, weight, height), the chief complaint and the allergies before the consultation (#77). Behind the `triage` switch and `vitals:read`, `vitals:record`, `vitals:configure`, `allergy:read` and `allergy:update`. Works at every width, except the ranges screen (desktop only).

**Recording (#77):** `triage/:patientId`, behind `vitals:record` and `vitals:read`, opened from a token of the queue board (`?token=<id>`, through the `queue.row.actions` slot, on tokens waiting or being served) and from the patient's tab. It shows who the patient is (name and MRN, for `patient:read`), the allergies, whether vitals were already taken for that token, and the form. A value outside the hospital's range is marked "High" or "Low" beside its field as it is typed, BMI is shown from the weight and height, and the screen confirms in place with the reading and how many values are out of range. `?corrects=<id>` starts a correction from that reading.

**The patient's tab (#77):** "Vitals and allergies" in `patient.detail.tabs`: the allergies (edited in place, for `allergy:update`) and the readings, newest first, a `DataGrid` page at a time with the out-of-range values marked, corrections and the readings they correct labelled, and "Correct" on a reading for `vitals:record`. A role with only one of `vitals:read` and `allergy:read` sees that part.

**`TriageSummary`** (from `index.ts`): the allergies and the newest reading of a patient, shown on the consultation (#80, beside the notes). Renders nothing with the switch off, and each part only for its permission. A reading takes two columns by the width of its container, not the window's (`@container`), so it fits that side column.

**Printing (#398):** `TriagePrintout`, `useTriagePrintParts` and `readTriageForPrint` (from `index.ts`) give the clinical prints plain text for paper: allergies, and the newest reading with the chief complaint. Nothing is marked high or low on paper.

## Decided

- **A reading belongs to the patient, and to the queue token it was taken for.** No encounter exists before M7, and triage does not invent a visit: `queueTokenId` says which token. `encounterId` is set once, from empty to the encounter, by the triage module itself (no other module changes its rows) when it hears `encounter.opened` for the reading's token, idempotently; nothing else of a reading ever changes. A reading taken outside the queue (from the patient's tab) has no token: from M7 the API attaches it to the patient's open encounter, and nursing vitals (NR-01) reuse this record rather than add a second one.
- **Readings are append-only.** A correction is a new reading with `correctsId` and a reason (`correctionReason`, required); the original stays, marked "Corrected" in the history. A reading is corrected once (422 `already_corrected`): a later correction corrects the correction, so the history never forks. A correction takes the token and the encounter of the reading it corrects, on the server; the screen sends no token with it. A second reading for the same token is allowed (a re-check) and said on the form, not refused; any status of today's token of this patient is taken, as a re-check may come after the consultation. `vitals.recorded` is emitted for each, and `allergy.updated` for a change of allergies (never the allergies themselves), through the outbox (#79).
- **Fixed units, stored as numbers:** mmHg, beats a minute, °C and kg to one decimal, %, cm. The API refuses what a body cannot be (systolic 40 to 300, diastolic 20 to 200 and below the systolic, pulse 20 to 300, temperature 25 to 45, SpO₂ 50 to 100, weight 0.3 to 400, height 20 to 250); the form checks the same. Typed with a point: "37,5" is refused, not read as 375. A hospital that measures in °F or lb needs a setting, an additive change.
- **BMI is shown, never stored or sent,** and never marked high or low.
- **The ranges are the server's, per hospital and per patient (#77, #379).** `GET /patients/{id}/vital-ranges` gives the hospital's ranges for the patient's age band today (infant under 1, child 1 to 11, adolescent 12 to 17, adult 18 and over, by age in whole years at the hospital's date). The screen marks values being typed against them and never decides itself which band applies. A band a hospital has set none for uses the API's built-in ranges, which are a starting point for the hospital's clinicians to review, not a standard.
- **A saved reading carries its own marks (`flags`), decided by the server (#378).** The ranges are effective-dated: a change is new rows that apply from the moment of the save, and a reading is marked against the ranges in effect when it was recorded, for the patient's band that day. History, summary and the record screen show `flags`; only a value still being typed is marked against today's ranges (`useReadingLines` takes `flags` when the reading has them). Nothing the screens show for a past reading changes when the ranges do.
- **Editing the ranges (#378): `triage/ranges`, `vitals:configure` (admin) and `vitals:read`, desktop only (`DesktopOnly`), one nav item ("Triage").** One fieldset per band, a low and a high for every measure, a limit typed with a point and at most one decimal, both empty meaning the measure is not checked. Saving sends the whole table (`PUT /vitals/ranges`) with the `version` it was read at, as allergies are replaced whole: another admin's save first is 409, the screen reads the table again, keeps what was typed, says so, and a second save replaces theirs. A low limit not below its high one is refused on the form and by the API (422 `invalid_range`).
- **A mark is a word with an icon** ("High", "Low", an arrow), never colour alone, red-900 text on red-100 for contrast. As values are typed, one polite live region says what is out of range ("Systolic: High."); after saving, the heading takes focus and is described by the status line ("Vitals saved. 1 value is outside the normal range."). Blood pressure counts as one value, as it is one mark.
- **Allergies are a list on the patient with three states:** `unknown` (never asked, version 0), `none` ("No known allergies", asked) and `listed`. "Not asked yet" and "No known allergies" are never shown alike: one is a question still to ask. A substance, a reaction and a severity from a fixed list (severe, moderate, mild, not known). The substance is free text, or chosen from the allergen catalog and the hospital's own medicines (#380, below). At most 50.
- **Allergies are replaced whole, from the version the edit started from.** Someone else's save first is 409 `conflict`: the panel reads the list again at once, the form keeps what was typed and shows the list as saved now, and saving again replaces it from the newest version. If it cannot be read again, it says so and Save asks again; Save is never left waiting. Saving sends the whole list, trimmed, not the changes: a list of a few short rows has no fields to merge.
- **An allergy may name something the system understands (#380).** The substance field is a combobox (`AllergenField`): typing searches the server (`POST /allergens/search`, the term in the body; the catalog's drug classes, foods and others, and the generic names of the hospital's medicines, each once, by name), and choosing sets the name and records which one it was (`allergenId` or `drugId`). Typing over a choice, or typing what the list lacks, is free text again and is kept as typed: the form says which it is, in words, under the field ("Chosen from the list (Drug class)" or "Not chosen from the list"). A coded allergy comes back with its `kind` and `members` (the lower-case generic names it covers), which prescribing and the pharmacy match a medicine against (`allergyCovers` in `features/clinical`); the list shows the kind beside a class, food or other. The members are a snapshot taken when the allergy was saved, so changing the catalog never changes a recorded allergy. The search needs `allergy:read` and the `triage` switch; the list is at most 10 suggestions and nothing is asked until something is typed.
- **The allergies stay on the record screen across saving the vitals,** so an allergies edit in progress is not lost.
- **An answer is written to the cache only for the session it was asked in:** the same person at the same hospital. One that arrives after a sign-out, a time-out or a switch, even when someone else has signed in at the same hospital on a shared PC, is dropped. Recording a reading refreshes that patient's readings only.
- **Every reading carries an idempotency key,** one per body, as issuing a token: a double click or a retry records it once.
- **Nothing in an address but ids:** `triage/<patientId>?token=<id>` or `?corrects=<id>`, and the API's paths take the patient's id. The list of readings is a `GET`: nothing typed in it says anything about a patient.
- **The queue row shows a link, not whether vitals were taken.** A contribution to `queue.row.actions` is rendered for every row and reads no data of its own (a request per row would be up to 100 requests, and as many audit entries, per page). The form says when the token's vitals were already taken. A "vitals taken" mark on the board needs the queue's page to carry it, or one request for a page.
- **Permissions:** `vitals:read`, `vitals:record`, `allergy:read`, `allergy:update`. Default grants for the API's seed (#79) follow the "Triage and vitals" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`: nurse records, doctor and admin view (admin all four for setting up). The mock has no nurse: its admin and doctor hold all four, so the screens can be tried.

## Mock

`mocks/handlers/triage.ts` also holds a few entries of the shared allergen catalog (`alg_00001` Penicillins, sulfonamides, NSAIDs, peanuts, shellfish, latex) and answers `POST /allergens/search` with them and the hospital's own medicines from the drugs mock; a save that names an `allergenId` or `drugId` stores the snapshot as the API does, and keeps one already on the list as it was (`422 unknown_allergen` for an id it does not know). It keeps readings and allergies per hospital: at the hospital, two readings of `pth_00001` (the newer with a high blood pressure and pulse) and a severe penicillin allergy; at the clinic, one reading of `ptc_00001` (37.6 °C, above the clinic's limit of 37.5) and "No known allergies". Every other patient has no readings and allergies never asked. The ranges are kept per hospital with a version (`GET` and `PUT /vitals/ranges`; the clinic's adult temperature and oxygen limits differ), the band comes from `mockPatientDob`, and a reading's `flags` are stored with it when it is recorded, so a later edit leaves them. Tests change the ranges behind the screen with `editMockAdultPulse` and read them with `mockRangeTable`. It checks a token against the queue mock (`mockQueueToken`: this patient's, today), a correction against the patient's readings, versions and idempotency keys, and the limits of the API. Tests change allergies behind the screen with `editMockAllergies` and read what was recorded with `mockVitalsOf`.

## Known gaps

- The ranges are for four age bands by whole years; a newborn is not told apart from an infant, and a pregnancy or a condition changes no range. Resting values by sex, or a unit other than °C, would be additive.
- No "vitals taken" mark on the queue board (see Decided).
- No respiratory rate, pain score, blood glucose or head circumference: a field each, additive.
- Units are fixed (°C, kg, cm).
- An allergy to a combination product records its whole generic name, so it does not flag the single ingredient; choose the class (or add the ingredient) for that.
- The catalog is the API's shared starter set; a hospital cannot add its own entries yet, so what it lacks is typed as free text, which prescribing matches by words only. An allergy recorded before #380 stays free text until someone chooses it from the list.
