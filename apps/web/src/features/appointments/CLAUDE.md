# Appointments: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**Appointments (web, against the mock):** the list (#70): the appointments of a range of days at the hospital (today by default, at most 31 days), filtered by doctor and by status, a page at a time from the server, with the patient's name, the MRN masked, the doctor, the department and the status. A row opens the appointment's own screen (#73), and its patient's screen, by id, for someone who may read patients. Behind the `appointments` switch and `appointment:read`. Works at every width: the list becomes cards on a phone. Hosts the `appointment.row.badges` slot, given `context.appointmentId` only, in the status column, which cannot be hidden. A badge contribution is rendered once for every row, so it reads no data of its own: a query per row would be up to 100 requests, and as many audit entries, for one page. A feature that wants a badge needs the data for a whole page in one request first, as the `patient.detail.tabs` rule in the root `CLAUDE.md` keeps queries out of the contributed component. The calendar is #72, below.

**Calendar (web, against the mock, #72):** `appointments/calendar`, behind `appointment:read`, reached from the list by a List and Calendar switch on both screens. One doctor's day or week as a time grid, drawn with the shared `Calendar` (`shared/ui/calendar`, react-big-calendar, loaded in its own chunk). Every status is shown, each in its own style and said in words after the patient's name when it is not scheduled. An appointment opens its own screen, by id (#73). The week is offered at desktop width only; a phone or a tablet held upright gets the day.

**Booking (web, against the mock, #71):** `appointments/new`, behind `appointment:create` and reached from the list. The patient is found with `PatientSearch` from `@/features/patients`, the doctor chosen from `useDoctorOptions` (opening on the signed-in doctor's own profile), then a day (today to 90 days ahead) and one of the doctor's free times, and an optional reason. A refused time offers the next free ones. Works at every width. Hosts the `appointment.form.fields` slot. `GET /appointments`, `GET /appointments/free-slots` and `POST /appointments` are in `packages/contract/planned.openapi.json` until the API (#75).

**The appointment and its changes (web, against the mock, #73):** `appointments/:appointmentId`, behind `appointment:read`, opened from a row of the list and from the calendar. The record (patient, with a link to their screen for someone who may read patients, when, doctor, status, the reason for the visit and, when cancelled, why), and, for `appointment:update`, the changes its status and the clock allow: move (`RescheduleForm`, with the booking's `SlotPicker`), cancel (`CancelForm`), mark as completed, mark as a no-show. Works at every width. `GET /appointments/{id}` and `POST /appointments/{id}/reschedule`, `/cancel`, `/complete` and `/no-show` are in the planned contract until the API (#75).

## Decided

- **A doctor reads the whole hospital's appointments, and the list opens on their own (#70).** The default grant of `appointment:read` to doctors has no `own` scope: a doctor covers for a colleague, and sees who is waiting for whom. The list opens filtered to the signed-in doctor, found as the doctor profile whose `userId` is theirs (`useDoctorOptions` from `@/features/doctors`), and choosing "All doctors" is kept until the screen is left. Someone with no profile at the hospital opens on every doctor. It is a default of the screen, not a limit: a hospital that wants doctors to see only their own gives the role `appointment:read` with scope `own`, which the API (#75) applies to the data.
- **The list is a `GET`, with days, a doctor's id and a status in its address** (#70). Nothing in it is typed about a patient, so it is not a search (Searches in the root `CLAUDE.md`). Its answer names patients, so the API audits every page read (#75).
- **A row names its patient to anyone who may read appointments,** by name and MRN, without `patient:read`: an appointment means nothing without whom it is for. Opening the patient's screen still needs `patient:read` and the `patients` switch.
- **A booking is for today to 90 days ahead at the hospital (#71).** The day is checked on the screen as well as by the date input's limits, which a typed or pasted day gets past; outside them no times are asked for.
- **Days are calendar days at the hospital,** sent as `YYYY-MM-DD`; the server turns them into instants in the hospital's timezone. Today is today at the hospital (`todayIn`), not in the browser. The range is checked on the screen before anything is asked for.
- **The doctor filter offers up to 100 practising doctors** (`useDoctorOptions`). A hospital with more needs a search in its place. Without the `doctors` switch or `doctor:read` the filter is not offered.
- **The days are set when the screen opens.** Left open past midnight at the hospital, it still shows the day it opened on; "Today" brings it back.
- **No reason in a list row.** The reason for a visit is the record's, read when the appointment is opened (#73, where an appointment is opened to change it).
- **The server works out the free times (#71).** `GET /appointments/free-slots?doctorId&day` gives the slots of the doctor's weekly hours, cut into the profile's `consultationMinutes`, that no scheduled appointment holds and that have not started. Cancelled and no-show appointments free their time. The browser never decides that a time is free; it offers what the server lists, and an answer older than five seconds is asked for again when the window is focused or the doctor or day is chosen again. Leave and one-off changes to hours will narrow the same list.
- **Two bookings of one time cannot both succeed (#71).** The API decides with an exclusion constraint on the doctor's scheduled appointments (#75), and the loser gets 409 `slot_taken`. The form then keeps what was typed, asks for the free times again, names the next three after the time refused and marks them in the list (one set of buttons, not a second one in the message); with none left that day it asks for another day. A time that left the doctor's hours meanwhile (422 `outside_hours`) is handled the same way, with its own message. A refusal is shown only while the doctor and the day are still those it was about: changed while the request was on its way, the form shows the new list and nothing else. A chosen time that a refresh no longer lists is let go, so the form never sends a time it does not show. The 409 carries no list of times: the form reads them from the free slots, so the API client's error keeps carrying a code only.
- **A patient cannot be booked twice at overlapping times, with any doctor (#71):** 409 `patient_overlap`, shown under the form. The desk chooses another time.
- **Every booking carries an idempotency key,** one per request body as in patient registration: a double click or a retry returns the first booking, and another time or patient is a new key.
- **Fields of other features go in `extensions` (#71).** A contribution to `appointment.form.fields` is rendered inside the booking form, given `context.doctorId` and `context.day`, and names its fields `extensions.<featureId>.<field>` with the form kit. The booking sends `extensions` keyed by feature id (empty parts left out); the API (#75) checks each part against the schema its feature registers and refuses a part no feature registered (422 `unknown_extension`). A contribution reads no patient data: it is not given the patient.
- **Booking needs finding a patient and choosing a doctor.** Without `patient:read` or the `patients` switch, or without `doctor:read` or the `doctors` switch, the screen says what is missing instead of a form that cannot be finished. The free times need `appointment:create`, or `appointment:update` to move a booking (#73).
- **The calendar is one doctor's (#72),** as the issue asks; the list stays the view of the whole hospital. react-big-calendar can draw several doctors side by side in the day view (its free `resources` option), which a later issue may add; FullCalendar's equivalent is a paid plugin, and nothing paid goes into the project. It opens on the signed-in doctor's own profile, as the list does, and otherwise on the first doctor by name. Without the `doctors` switch or `doctor:read` it says so and asks for nothing.
- **The calendar asks for the days it shows and no more (#72):** `GET /appointments` with the day or the week, the doctor and `limit=100`, following the cursor to the end of that range, at most five pages. Past 500 in one range (far beyond one doctor's week) it says that not all are shown. The same doctor's previous range stays drawn, marked as loading, while the next one comes; another doctor's never does, since it would be drawn, and opened, under the new name. Its key is `calendarKey` (`[...appointmentKeys.all, 'calendar', range]`), not under `lists`: an update aimed at the list's pages (#73) never meets the calendar's data, and invalidating `all`, as booking does, refreshes both.
- **The week follows the hospital's locale (#72):** it starts on the first day of the week of the locale's region (or the likely region of a language alone), from a table of CLDR's Sunday-first and Saturday-first regions in `calendar/zoned`, Monday elsewhere. Not `Intl.Locale` week info, which Firefox lacks: two people at one hospital see the same week in any browser. A hospital that wants another first day needs it in the tenant config. A day heading of the week opens that day.
- **An appointment opens its own screen, from the list and from the calendar (#73),** for anyone who may read appointments; the patient is one link away from there. This replaced the calendar's first rule (#72), where a block opened the patient and was plain text without `patient:read`.
- **Only a scheduled appointment changes (#73).** Completed, cancelled and no-show are final (409 `invalid_transition`). It is moved any time while scheduled, cancelled until it ends (422 `too_late` after), and completed or marked a no-show once it starts (422 `too_early` before). The API enforces these in the transaction; `allowedChanges` in `schemas.ts` offers the same on the screen, recomputed every 30 seconds so "Mark as completed" appears at the start. Completing will also come from closing the OPD encounter (M7).
- **One permission, `appointment:update`, for all four changes (#73),** as the roles table gives the desk "create/edit". A hospital that wants cancelling apart needs a new permission, an additive change.
- **A reason from a list for moving and cancelling (#73),** with a note of plain text that is required for "Other". The cancellation's reason and note are on the record; a move's reason goes to the event and the audit log only. Completing and a no-show ask for no reason but for a confirmation, since neither can be undone.
- **Every change sends the `version` it was made from and an idempotency key** (one per body, as booking). Someone else's change first gives 409 `conflict`: the screen closes the form, says so and shows the record as it is now. No field-by-field merge as for a patient: a change of status has no typing to keep.
- **A change of status shows at once (#73),** on the record and in the list pages held in memory (`useChangeAppointment`), and goes back if refused. A move does not: it can be refused for a time taken meanwhile, and the form stays open for the next free times, as in booking. Whatever the answer, `appointmentKeys.all` is invalidated (list, calendar, free times), and nothing is written to the cache if the session ended meanwhile.
- **Moving asks the free times with `appointment:update` (#73):** `GET /appointments/free-slots` needs `appointment:create` or `appointment:update`. The appointment's own time is held by itself and is not offered; it never counts against its own move or the patient's overlap.
- **After booking, the screen confirms in place** (who, with whom, when), as patient registration does, with "Book another" keeping the doctor and the day. Nothing is passed to the list in the address or the history state.

## Mock

`mocks/handlers/appointments.ts` seeds appointments around today at each hospital, deterministic for a given day: six doctors with eight fifteen-minute appointments each morning at the hospital, two doctors with three each at the clinic, from three days ago to a week ahead. It keeps ids only and looks up names when it answers, as the API will join them. Free times come from the doctors mock's working hours (`mockDoctorHours`). A booking is decided with nothing awaited between the check and the insert, so two at once are decided one after the other, as the constraint decides them. It refuses any part of `extensions` unless a test calls `acceptMockAppointmentExtension(featureId)`. The changes of #73 follow the API's rules (final statuses, version, the clock, free slots, idempotency keys per appointment) and keep the reason and the cancellation on the stored appointment.
