# Insurers, TPAs and corporates: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**Masters for the insurance milestone (M21), web only (IN-01, #181):** the organisations a hospital bills instead of the patient (`insurer`, `tpa`, `corporate`), their plans and their rate agreements, at `/insurance`, `/insurance/new` and `/insurance/:payerId`. Behind the `insurance` switch and the `payer:read` and `payer:manage` permissions. Desktop-only (master data). The insurance API (BE-28, #187) now implements these endpoints (`/payers`, `/payers/{id}/plans`, `/payer-plans/{id}`, `/payers/{id}/rate-agreements`, `/rate-agreements/{id}`); the mock still does what it does. API notes: `apps/api/src/features/insurance/CLAUDE.md`.

Decided for now:

- **One kind of record, three kinds.** A TPA is a payer too (it administers claims for insurers); linking a TPA to the insurers it serves, and a patient's policy, are policy capture (IN-02, #182), not here. Contact details are the organisation's: the contact field says so, and nothing about a patient is ever stored or searched here, so the list search is a `GET` with `q` in the query.
- **A plan** holds the patient's share (`copayPercent`, whole percent), optional limits (`sumInsuredMinor` for a policy year, `roomRentLimitMinor` for a bed-day, minor units, absent means no limit), eligibility rules (`preAuthRequired`, `minAge`, `maxAge`, `waitingPeriodDays`) and covered services: `coverageMode` `all_except` (everything is covered but the listed services) or `only_listed`, with an optional limit per listed service. Services are chosen through `ServiceSearch` from `@/features/services`, searched on the server and kept by id; only the name is shown. A code is the hospital's own and cannot be changed. Terms are replaced as a whole by `PATCH` from the version the edit started from; a plan is archived, never deleted. Which rule applies to a given charge is worked out when a charge is made (IN-03), which keeps the cover it applied with the charge.
- **A rate agreement is effective-dated and never edited.** It has a first day, an optional last day, a discount on list prices in basis points and agreed rates for named services (an agreed rate replaces the list price of that service). Agreements of one payer never overlap (409 `agreement_overlap`, periods are closed ranges and no last day is open-ended) and cannot start in the past (422 `starts_in_past`). The only change is to end it: "End today" on a running agreement sets the last day to today on the hospital's clock. To change a price, end the agreement and add a new one from a later date, so a bill priced under old terms stays explainable.
- **Editing a payer** sends only the changed fields with the `version` the edit started from. A conflict keeps what was typed and takes what was saved in the other fields (`reset` with `keepDirtyValues`); unlike the patient and ward screens it does not show the other person's value under a field both changed. A field emptied is sent as `null`.
- Amounts are typed in the hospital's currency and read on their digits with `toMinor` (`@/features/services`); a discount is a percent with up to two decimals, read to basis points on its digits.
- **Not here yet:** the mock keeps no link between a payer and a price list (a rate agreement's discount and rates are data for billing to read later); payer-wise tariffs in the invoice builder, policy capture and pre-authorization (IN-02), split billing and co-pay on invoices (IN-03), claims (IN-04) and corporate credit (IN-05) are their own issues. Phone numbers and emails are only checked for shape.

## Policy capture and pre-authorization (IN-02, #182)

The endpoints (`/patients/{id}/policies`, `/patient-policies/{id}/end`, `/patients/{id}/pre-auths`, `/encounters/{id}/pre-auths`, `/pre-auths/{id}/decision` and `/cancel`) are implemented by the insurance API (#187); the mock mirrors them. Permissions `policy:read`, `policy:manage`, `preauth:read`, `preauth:manage`, behind the `insurance` switch.

- **The Insurance tab** (`patient.detail.tabs`, `InsuranceTab`): policies (organisation, plan, card number, holder and relation, dates; the server works out active, expired or ended) and pre-authorizations. Adding a policy also needs `payer:read` (it picks the organisation and plan; the first 25 organisations are offered, a server search is not built). A policy is ended, never edited or deleted; an ended or expired policy backs no new request. The lists are small arrays per patient, not paged.
- **A pre-authorization** is requested against an active policy: treatment text, amount asked for, and what is enclosed **by name**. The files themselves are uploaded under the patient's Documents tab; there is no link to a stored document yet. Answered once by `preauth:manage` (`approved` with an amount, or `rejected`), or withdrawn while waiting. Money is typed with `toMinor`.
- **Limits during admission billing:** `LimitPanel` is contributed to the new slot `invoice.builder.panels` (given `encounterId` and `patientId`, rendered by `billing/new`). It lists the visit's pre-authorizations from `GET /encounters/{id}/pre-auths`, where `billedMinor` is what the server worked out from the charge ledger (the mock holds a fixed figure). `LimitMeter` shows approved, billed and what is left, a bar and a word: ok below 80% (`NEAR_LIMIT_PERCENT`), near from 80%, reached at 100%; near and reached are announced (`status` and `alert`). It reads again when the window regains focus, not live as charges are added.
- **Not here yet:** policy fields on the patient registration form and on the admit form (the tab is where a policy is captured; both screens belong to other modules and would need a slot contribution), pre-authorization tied to an admission (it is tied to the visit, `encounterId`), enhancement requests when the limit is reached, eligibility checks against the plan's age and waiting period, and the TPA-to-insurer link.

## Split billing and co-pay (IN-03, #183)

`GET` and `POST /encounters/{id}/coverage-split` are implemented by the insurance API (#187); the mock mirrors them. `SplitPanel` is a second contribution to `invoice.builder.panels` (for `policy:read`; saving needs `policy:manage`; no new permission). `mocks/handlers/coverage-split.ts` does what the API will.

- **The server divides, the screen shows.** Per charge of the visit's ledger (void ones left out): the payer's agreed rate (an agreed rate for the service, else the agreement's discount, rounded half up), then covered or not (`all_except` / `only_listed`), the limit per listed service, the plan's co-pay percent on the eligible part and the sum insured, in that order. Whole minor units, so `payer + patient = rated` on every line and `ledger + rate adjustment = payer + patient` in total. Each line says why (`covered`, `copay`, `not_covered`, `service_limit`, `sum_insured`) in words.
- **Reconciliation is checked, not assumed.** `splitReconciles` (`split.ts`) re-adds the server's figures and the panel shows "The split adds up to the charges" or, if not, a danger badge saying it does not. It computes nothing for display.
- **No active policy, no panel** (the API answers 404). The newest active policy of the patient is used. The sum insured is applied to the visit alone, not to the policy year, and the pre-authorization limit is not part of the split (it is `LimitPanel`'s).
- **"Save the split"** keeps a copy (ledger, payer and patient totals); the next read says `stale` with a message when the ledger moved since.
- **Not here yet:** issuing separate bills for the patient's and the payer's share (the invoice builder bills the whole ledger; the payer invoice needs the claims work, IN-04, and an invoice `payerId`), non-payable items by hospital rule beyond the plan's list, room-rent proportionate deduction, and the sum insured across visits.

## Claims submission and tracking (IN-04, #184)

`GET /claims`, `GET /claims/aging`, `GET /claims/{id}`, `POST /claims/{id}/actions` and `POST /encounters/{id}/claims` are implemented by the insurance API (#187); the mock mirrors them. Permissions `claim:read`, `claim:manage` (prepare, send, answer, record the payer's decision and payments, withdraw) and `claim:writeoff`, behind the `insurance` switch. Routes `/claims`, `/claims/aging` and `/claims/:claimId` belong to this manifest; the only navigation entry is still "Insurers and TPAs", so they are reached from the "Prepare a claim" link, the list and the address (a nav entry per feature is one; a separate `claims` feature would give them their own).

- **A claim is prepared from a visit's split.** `SplitPanel` gets "Prepare a claim" (`claim:manage`, payer share above zero): the packet is the documents enclosed **by name**, as with pre-authorizations (the files are on the patient's Documents tab; no link to a stored document yet). The server takes the claimed amount (the payer's share of the split) and the policy; one claim in play per visit (409 `claim_exists`), nothing owed is 422.
- **The state machine is the server's, the screen offers `allowedActions`.** `draft` (submit, cancel), `submitted` (raise_query, approve, reject), `query` (reply, cancel), `approved` (settle), `rejected` and `settled` (write_off), and `written_off` and `cancelled` end. `settled` offers a write-off only while money is still outstanding. Any other move is 409 `invalid_transition`. The table is in `mocks/handlers/claims.ts` (`CLAIM_TRANSITIONS`) and is what the API must do; `claims.test.tsx` checks it. A step sends the `version` the page was read at.
- **Money:** `claimedMinor`, `approvedMinor` (at most claimed), `receivedMinor` (a payment at most the approved amount; one payment settles the claim, partial payments over time are not modelled), `writtenOffMinor`, and `outstandingMinor` = claimed less received and written off, worked out by the server (zero for a draft or a withdrawn claim). The write-off takes the whole outstanding amount and needs a reason and `claim:writeoff`.
- **Aging** (`/claims/aging`): outstanding money of sent claims by payer in four columns of days since the claim was sent (0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, over 90) and a total row. The server computes it; claims not yet sent are left out.
- **Not here yet:** a payer invoice with its own `payerId` (the invoice builder still bills the whole ledger), partial payments over time, resubmitting a rejected claim, files attached to the packet from stored documents, reminders and a due date per payer, and filtering the list by payer.

- Policy numbers are masked (`maskEnd`, last four) in lists and pickers (pre-authorization list, policy picker); the policy card shows the full value. The split panel needs `policy:read` and `charge:read`: a manifest can only require permissions it declares itself, so `charge:read` is checked inside `SplitPanel` (#785).

## Corporate credit billing and receivables (IN-05, #185)

Mock only; `GET /credit-accounts`, `GET` and `PUT /payers/{id}/credit-account`, `GET /payers/{id}/credit-bills`, `GET /payers/{id}/receipts`, `POST /payers/{id}/receipts`, `GET /payers/{id}/credit-statement` and `POST /encounters/{id}/credit-bills` are in the planned contract until the insurance API (#187). Permissions `receivable:read`, `receivable:manage` (open or change an account, bill a visit to credit) and `receivable:receipt` (record money received), behind the `insurance` switch. Routes `/credit` and `/credit/:payerId` belong to this manifest and, like the claims, have no nav entry of their own: they are reached from "Corporate credit and receivables" on the payer list, "Credit account" on a corporate's page and the link in the billing panel. Not desktop-only (billing must work on a tablet).

- **An account belongs to a corporate** (422 `not_corporate` for an insurer or TPA): a credit limit, the days to pay, and a `version` for the edit (409 `version_conflict`; the dialog keeps what was typed). A limit below what is owed is 422 `below_outstanding`.
- **A credit bill is a whole visit**: `CreditBillPanel`, a third contribution to `invoice.builder.panels` (`receivable:manage` and `receivable:read`), puts the visit's ledger on an account; the server totals it, due after the terms, and refuses beyond the credit left (422 `credit_limit_exceeded`), an empty ledger (`nothing_to_bill`) and a second bill for the visit (409 `credit_bill_exists`). The panel is hidden when no corporate has an account.
- **A receipt is set against the oldest unpaid bills first** and may not exceed what is owed (422 `exceeds_outstanding`, also checked on the form). The answer lists where each part went; the dialog shows it and prints it (`PrintSheet`).
- **Receivables reconcile with payments, and the screen checks it** (`credit.ts`): `accountReconciles` (owed = billed less received), `statementReconciles` (closing = opening + billed less received, and for a period that runs to today, closing = what the account owes) and `receiptReconciles` (allocations add up to the receipt). Each shows a badge in words, or a danger badge saying it does not add up. The screen computes nothing for display.
- **Statement:** a period (default the last 90 days, on the hospital's clock), lines with a running balance paged by cursor, totals from the first page. Printing reads every page first. The statement and the receipt carry the corporate and bill numbers, no patient.
- **Not here yet:** the visit's charges are not marked as billed to credit (the invoice builder can still invoice the same ledger; needs billing), credit notes or write-offs against a bill, interest and reminders, an aging view by corporate, a statement by employee, and the credit limit counting pre-authorized but unbilled amounts.
