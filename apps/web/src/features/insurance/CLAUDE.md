# Insurers, TPAs and corporates: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**Masters for the insurance milestone (M21), web only (IN-01, #181):** the organisations a hospital bills instead of the patient (`insurer`, `tpa`, `corporate`), their plans and their rate agreements, at `/insurance`, `/insurance/new` and `/insurance/:payerId`. Behind the `insurance` switch and the `payer:read` and `payer:manage` permissions. Desktop-only (master data). It runs on the mock: the endpoints (`/payers`, `/payers/{id}/plans`, `/payer-plans/{id}`, `/payers/{id}/rate-agreements`, `/rate-agreements/{id}`) are in `packages/contract/planned.openapi.json` until the insurance API (BE-28, #187) implements them; delete them there when it does.

Decided for now:

- **One kind of record, three kinds.** A TPA is a payer too (it administers claims for insurers); linking a TPA to the insurers it serves, and a patient's policy, are policy capture (IN-02, #182), not here. Contact details are the organisation's: the contact field says so, and nothing about a patient is ever stored or searched here, so the list search is a `GET` with `q` in the query.
- **A plan** holds the patient's share (`copayPercent`, whole percent), optional limits (`sumInsuredMinor` for a policy year, `roomRentLimitMinor` for a bed-day, minor units, absent means no limit), eligibility rules (`preAuthRequired`, `minAge`, `maxAge`, `waitingPeriodDays`) and covered services: `coverageMode` `all_except` (everything is covered but the listed services) or `only_listed`, with an optional limit per listed service. Services are chosen through `ServiceSearch` from `@/features/services`, searched on the server and kept by id; only the name is shown. A code is the hospital's own and cannot be changed. Terms are replaced as a whole by `PATCH` from the version the edit started from; a plan is archived, never deleted. Which rule applies to a given charge is worked out when a charge is made (IN-03), which keeps the cover it applied with the charge.
- **A rate agreement is effective-dated and never edited.** It has a first day, an optional last day, a discount on list prices in basis points and agreed rates for named services (an agreed rate replaces the list price of that service). Agreements of one payer never overlap (409 `agreement_overlap`, periods are closed ranges and no last day is open-ended) and cannot start in the past (422 `starts_in_past`). The only change is to end it: "End today" on a running agreement sets the last day to today on the hospital's clock. To change a price, end the agreement and add a new one from a later date, so a bill priced under old terms stays explainable.
- **Editing a payer** sends only the changed fields with the `version` the edit started from. A conflict keeps what was typed and takes what was saved in the other fields (`reset` with `keepDirtyValues`); unlike the patient and ward screens it does not show the other person's value under a field both changed. A field emptied is sent as `null`.
- Amounts are typed in the hospital's currency and read on their digits with `toMinor` (`@/features/services`); a discount is a percent with up to two decimals, read to basis points on its digits.
- **Not here yet:** the mock keeps no link between a payer and a price list (a rate agreement's discount and rates are data for billing to read later); payer-wise tariffs in the invoice builder, policy capture and pre-authorization (IN-02), split billing and co-pay on invoices (IN-03), claims (IN-04) and corporate credit (IN-05) are their own issues. Phone numbers and emails are only checked for shape.
