# Insurers, TPAs and corporates: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**Masters for the insurance milestone (M21), web only (IN-01, #181):** the organisations a hospital bills instead of the patient (`insurer`, `tpa`, `corporate`), their plans and their rate agreements, at `/insurance`, `/insurance/new` and `/insurance/:payerId`. Behind the `insurance` switch and the `payer:read` and `payer:manage` permissions. Desktop-only (master data). It runs on the mock: the endpoints (`/payers`, `/payers/{id}/plans`, `/payer-plans/{id}`, `/payers/{id}/rate-agreements`, `/rate-agreements/{id}`) are in `packages/contract/planned.openapi.json` until the insurance API (BE-28, #187) implements them; delete them there when it does.

Decided for now:

- **One kind of record, three kinds.** A TPA is a payer too (it administers claims for insurers); linking a TPA to the insurers it serves, and a patient's policy, are policy capture (IN-02, #182), not here. Contact details are the organisation's: the contact field says so, and nothing about a patient is ever stored or searched here, so the list search is a `GET` with `q` in the query.
- **A plan** holds the patient's share (`copayPercent`, whole percent), optional limits (`sumInsuredMinor` for a policy year, `roomRentLimitMinor` for a bed-day, minor units, absent means no limit), eligibility rules (`preAuthRequired`, `minAge`, `maxAge`, `waitingPeriodDays`) and covered services: `coverageMode` `all_except` (everything is covered but the listed services) or `only_listed`, with an optional limit per listed service. Services are chosen through `ServiceSearch` from `@/features/services`, searched on the server and kept by id; only the name is shown. A code is the hospital's own and cannot be changed. Terms are replaced as a whole by `PATCH` from the version the edit started from; a plan is archived, never deleted. Which rule applies to a given charge is worked out when a charge is made (IN-03), which keeps the cover it applied with the charge.
- **A rate agreement is effective-dated and never edited.** It has a first day, an optional last day, a discount on list prices in basis points and agreed rates for named services (an agreed rate replaces the list price of that service). Agreements of one payer never overlap (409 `agreement_overlap`, periods are closed ranges and no last day is open-ended) and cannot start in the past (422 `starts_in_past`). The only change is to end it: "End today" on a running agreement sets the last day to today on the hospital's clock. To change a price, end the agreement and add a new one from a later date, so a bill priced under old terms stays explainable.
- **Editing a payer** sends only the changed fields with the `version` the edit started from. A conflict keeps what was typed and takes what was saved in the other fields (`reset` with `keepDirtyValues`); unlike the patient and ward screens it does not show the other person's value under a field both changed. A field emptied is sent as `null`.
- Amounts are typed in the hospital's currency and read on their digits with `toMinor` (`@/features/services`); a discount is a percent with up to two decimals, read to basis points on its digits.
- **Not here yet:** the mock keeps no link between a payer and a price list (a rate agreement's discount and rates are data for billing to read later); payer-wise tariffs in the invoice builder, policy capture and pre-authorization (IN-02), split billing and co-pay on invoices (IN-03), claims (IN-04) and corporate credit (IN-05) are their own issues. Phone numbers and emails are only checked for shape.

## Policy capture and pre-authorization (IN-02, #182)

Mock only, like the masters; the endpoints (`/patients/{id}/policies`, `/patient-policies/{id}/end`, `/patients/{id}/pre-auths`, `/encounters/{id}/pre-auths`, `/pre-auths/{id}/decision` and `/cancel`) are in the planned contract until the insurance API (#187). Permissions `policy:read`, `policy:manage`, `preauth:read`, `preauth:manage`, behind the `insurance` switch.

- **The Insurance tab** (`patient.detail.tabs`, `InsuranceTab`): policies (organisation, plan, card number, holder and relation, dates; the server works out active, expired or ended) and pre-authorizations. Adding a policy also needs `payer:read` (it picks the organisation and plan; the first 25 organisations are offered, a server search is not built). A policy is ended, never edited or deleted; an ended or expired policy backs no new request. The lists are small arrays per patient, not paged.
- **A pre-authorization** is requested against an active policy: treatment text, amount asked for, and what is enclosed **by name**. The files themselves are uploaded under the patient's Documents tab; there is no link to a stored document yet. Answered once by `preauth:manage` (`approved` with an amount, or `rejected`), or withdrawn while waiting. Money is typed with `toMinor`.
- **Limits during admission billing:** `LimitPanel` is contributed to the new slot `invoice.builder.panels` (given `encounterId` and `patientId`, rendered by `billing/new`). It lists the visit's pre-authorizations from `GET /encounters/{id}/pre-auths`, where `billedMinor` is what the server worked out from the charge ledger (the mock holds a fixed figure). `LimitMeter` shows approved, billed and what is left, a bar and a word: ok below 80% (`NEAR_LIMIT_PERCENT`), near from 80%, reached at 100%; near and reached are announced (`status` and `alert`). It reads again when the window regains focus, not live as charges are added.
- **Not here yet:** policy fields on the patient registration form and on the admit form (the tab is where a policy is captured; both screens belong to other modules and would need a slot contribution), pre-authorization tied to an admission (it is tied to the visit, `encounterId`), enhancement requests when the limit is reached, eligibility checks against the plan's age and waiting period, and the TPA-to-insurer link.

## Split billing and co-pay (IN-03, #183)

Mock only; `GET` and `POST /encounters/{id}/coverage-split` are in the planned contract until the insurance API (#187). `SplitPanel` is a second contribution to `invoice.builder.panels` (for `policy:read`; saving needs `policy:manage`; no new permission). `mocks/handlers/coverage-split.ts` does what the API will.

- **The server divides, the screen shows.** Per charge of the visit's ledger (void ones left out): the payer's agreed rate (an agreed rate for the service, else the agreement's discount, rounded half up), then covered or not (`all_except` / `only_listed`), the limit per listed service, the plan's co-pay percent on the eligible part and the sum insured, in that order. Whole minor units, so `payer + patient = rated` on every line and `ledger + rate adjustment = payer + patient` in total. Each line says why (`covered`, `copay`, `not_covered`, `service_limit`, `sum_insured`) in words.
- **Reconciliation is checked, not assumed.** `splitReconciles` (`split.ts`) re-adds the server's figures and the panel shows "The split adds up to the charges" or, if not, a danger badge saying it does not. It computes nothing for display.
- **No active policy, no panel** (the API answers 404). The newest active policy of the patient is used. The sum insured is applied to the visit alone, not to the policy year, and the pre-authorization limit is not part of the split (it is `LimitPanel`'s).
- **"Save the split"** keeps a copy (ledger, payer and patient totals); the next read says `stale` with a message when the ledger moved since.
- **Not here yet:** issuing separate bills for the patient's and the payer's share (the invoice builder bills the whole ledger; the payer invoice needs the claims work, IN-04, and an invoice `payerId`), non-payable items by hospital rule beyond the plan's list, room-rent proportionate deduction, and the sum insured across visits.
