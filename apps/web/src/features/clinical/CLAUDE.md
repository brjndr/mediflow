# Clinical: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**The OPD consultation (web; the API is #86, notes in `apps/api/src/features/clinical/CLAUDE.md`):** the encounter, its notes and its diagnoses (#80). Behind the `clinical` switch and `encounter:read`, `encounter:create`, `encounter:update`, `clinicalNote:read`, `clinicalNote:write`, `diagnosis:read`, `diagnosis:write`, for prescriptions (#81) `prescription:read` and `prescription:create`, for printing (#89) `encounter:print`, for lab, imaging and procedure orders (#82, #87) `order:read`, `order:create` and `order:cancel`, and for referrals (#88) `referral:read` and `referral:create`. Works at every width.

**Starting (#80):** "Start consultation" on the token being served on the queue board (`queue.row.actions`) and on today's scheduled appointment, in the list and on its screen (`appointment.row.actions`, `appointment.detail.actions`), for `encounter:create` and `encounter:read`, and `patient:read`, which the component checks. One press opens the encounter (`POST /encounters` with the token or the appointment) and goes to it.

**The consultation (#80):** `encounters/:encounterId`, behind `encounter:read`. Who the visit is for (name and MRN, which `encounter:read` gives), with whom, its status, then `TriageSummary` from `@/features/triage` (allergies and the newest vitals; before the notes on a narrow screen, beside them from 1024 px), the notes for `clinicalNote:read`, the diagnoses for `diagnosis:read`, the prescriptions for `prescription:read` (#81), and closing or cancelling for `encounter:update`.

**Prescriptions (#81):** on the consultation, behind `prescription:read`: each prescription oldest first, with its lines in the order written (`lineNumber`), its advice, who wrote it and when, and a line's status in words when it is no longer just placed. "Write a prescription" for `prescription:create` while the encounter is open: 1 to 30 lines, each a medicine chosen with `DrugSearch` from `@/features/drugs`, with dose, route, frequency, days, quantity and instructions, and advice for the patient. "Revise" on a prescription not yet replaced.

**Lab orders (#82):** on the consultation, behind `order:read`: a `DataGrid` of the encounter's lab orders oldest first (`GET /encounters/:id/orders?type=lab`), each with the test or panel as ordered (`itemName`), its priority, its status in words, who ordered it and when, and its instructions. The visit's released results follow the order panels: `LabResultsPanel` from `@/features/lab` (#92), which checks `labResult:read` and the switch itself. "Order a test" for `order:create` while the encounter is open: a test or panel chosen with `LabTestSearch` from `@/features/lab-tests`, a priority (routine, urgent, stat) and instructions. "Cancel order" is a row action for `order:cancel`, with a reason.

**Imaging and procedure orders (#87):** two more panels after the lab orders, "Imaging orders" and "Procedure orders", working as the lab orders do (`GET /encounters/:id/orders?type=imaging|procedure`): a study or procedure is a service of that category chosen with `ServiceSearch` from `@/features/services`, with a priority and instructions, and cancelled with a reason. The imaging panel says reports arrive with the radiology module; the procedure panel says nothing of results.

**Printing (#89):** "Print summary" under the visit's details, and "Print" on each prescription not replaced with a line not cancelled, for `encounter:print` (and `prescription:read` for a prescription), on an encounter not cancelled. The page has the hospital's letterhead (`Letterhead` from `@/tenancy`: logo, name, address, phone and registration number from the tenant config, #397), who the visit is for, with whom and when, the doctor's signature line with their qualifications and registration (#397), and who printed it and when. A prescription prints its lines not cancelled, in order, and its advice; the summary prints the diagnoses not retracted, the signed notes not amended and the prescriptions not replaced, each only for its read permission.

**The clinical timeline (#83):** a "Clinical timeline" tab on the patient's screen (`patient.detail.tabs`, for `encounter:read`): a `DataGrid` of the patient's visits the caller may see, newest first (`GET /encounters?patientId=`), a page of 25 at a time with "Load more", each with when it started (a link to the consultation), its type, the doctor, the department and its status, filtered by type of visit and status on the server.

**Referrals (#88):** on the consultation, behind `referral:read`: a `DataGrid` of the encounter's referrals oldest first, each with where it goes (the department, and the doctor when one was named), its urgency, the reason, who referred and when, and whether it stands. "Refer the patient" for `referral:create` while the encounter is open: a department from `useDepartmentOptions` (`@/features/departments`), a doctor of it or any of them (`useDoctorOptions`), routine or urgent, and the reason. "Cancel referral" is a row action with a reason, also after the encounter closed.

**Follow-up (#88):** the consultation renders `FollowUpPanel` from `@/features/follow-ups`, which checks `followUp:read` itself; the due list is that feature's (`apps/web/src/features/follow-ups/CLAUDE.md`).

**Consultations (#80):** `encounters`, behind `encounter:read`, in the navigation. A `DataGrid` of the encounters the caller may see, open ones by default, opening on the signed-in doctor's own (found by `userId` in `useDoctorOptions`, as the queue board does); filtered by doctor and status. It brings a doctor back to a visit left open, since a served token leaves the board.

## Decided

- **Opening is a press, not a visit to a page.** The action sends the token or the appointment and the patient, with an idempotency key, and goes to the encounter the server answers with: the one opened now, or the one already open for that token or appointment (200). Pressing it again from a second desk finds the same encounter. Nothing in the address but the encounter's id.
- **Offered on the token being served only** (`called`): the doctor starts the consultation with the patient in the room; a waiting patient may still be skipped. On an appointment, only a scheduled one that starts today at the hospital, as check-in.
- **The appointment slots are given `patientId` (#80),** an additive change to the slot contexts (`docs/EXTENDING.md`), since opening needs the patient with the appointment and a contribution reads no data of its own.
- **Notes and diagnoses are each behind their own permission, and a role without one sees no trace of it:** no heading, no empty state, no request. A role with `encounter:read` alone (the nurse's default) sees the visit and what triage found, not what it was for (the field-level restriction of the issue).
- **The role matrix is one test (#84),** `permissions.test.tsx`: each role's defaults as the API seeds them (`apps/api/src/features/clinical/permissions.ts`, kept by hand in the test, as the web never imports the API), and a custom role, on the consultation (each panel and action, and that a hidden panel is never read), the list, the timeline and starting. Admin and doctor see everything; the nurse sees the visit, triage, prescriptions, orders and printing, and changes nothing; the receptionist, pharmacist, lab technician, radiologist and store keeper have no way in. A change to a role's defaults changes that table. The single permissions stay tested beside each panel.
- **In a real browser (#85),** `e2e/clinical.spec.ts` does each main task with the keyboard alone (starting from a token, a note signed, a prescription, printing, lab and imaging orders, the timeline), and two permission-absent cases against the mock's own roles: the clinic's doctor, who has no order permission, sees no order panel and sends no `/orders` request; the clinic's receptionist has no Consultations link, no Start consultation on the queue, no timeline tab and "no access" at a consultation's address, and sends no `/encounters` request. The full matrix stays in the unit test.
- **A draft is kept on the server as it is written, never in the browser** (`useDraftNote`). The first save creates it (`POST`, with an idempotency key per body), later ones save it whole from the version it was read at (`PATCH`). It is saved 1.5 seconds after typing stops, when focus leaves the editor (not when it moves to the toolbar), before signing, and when the editor goes away. What is typed waits in memory only, and nothing is sent once the session ended or moved hospital. An empty document is never sent. One save at a time; typing during a save is saved after it. `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` stay empty, which the unit test and the Playwright test check.
- **A first save whose answer never came is sent again as it was** (same body, same key) before anything newer, so the server answers with the draft it may have made instead of making a second one; a refusal (4xx) drops it. Each save after it is the whole text from the version held.
- **Signing and discarding stop the draft first.** Nothing more is sent once either starts (`stop`, which waits for a save in flight and gives the id the server has, so a draft created meanwhile is the one removed); the editor takes no typing while it runs (`inert`); a refusal resumes it. A sign refused with `409 conflict` asks the same question as a save that met one.
- **A draft whose author lost `clinicalNote:write`,** or of an encounter no longer open, is shown read-only as last saved.
- **A draft is not refreshed from the list while it is written.** Saving does not re-read the notes, so the editor never loses what is being typed. A draft started here keeps its place under a key of its own until the server gives it an id, and the list's copy of it is not drawn twice.
- **The save state is plain text under the editor, described by it, not a live region:** it changes at every pause in typing, and a screen reader would hear it all the time. A failed save and a conflict are alerts; signing, discarding and amending are said in the panel's status line, which takes focus when what had it went away.
- **A conflict pauses saving and asks.** A `409 conflict` on a draft means its author saved it from another window. The screen keeps what is typed and offers "Keep the text here" (saved over the newer version) or "Use the saved text" (the editor shows that one). Nothing is overwritten without the choice.
- **Discarding asks once**, in place: the question takes the button's place with focus on "Keep it", the safe answer.
- **Signed notes are shown oldest first, with their amendments.** An amended note says it was amended and is shown muted; the amendment says which note it amends and why. "Amend" is offered on a signed note not yet amended (a later correction amends the amendment), with the whole text to correct and a reason, for `clinicalNote:write`, also after the encounter closed but not once cancelled.
- **Diagnoses are a `DataGrid`:** the code and title as recorded and/or the words, provisional or final, who recorded it, and whether it stands. A retracted one stays, struck through and said in words ("Retracted … : reason"), never by the line alone. Recording takes `CodeSearch` from `@/features/codes` (ICD-10), words, or both (neither is refused on the form as on the server), while the encounter is open. "Retract" is a row action with a reason, also after the encounter closed.
- **Closing and cancelling are the server's to refuse.** Closing with a draft is `409 unsigned_notes` ("Sign or discard every draft first"); cancelling once anything is recorded is `409 has_records` ("close it instead"). Both send the version shown; a conflict re-reads the encounter. The buttons go once it is closed or cancelled, and focus goes to the status line.
- **Every POST carries an idempotency key,** one per body, cleared after an answer, as the API requires. An answer is written to the cache only for the session it was asked in (`useStillHere`, as triage does).
- **The consultations list is a `GET`** with a doctor's id and a status: nothing typed in it says anything about a patient. `total` comes only with a doctor chosen, as the API counts.
- **A prescription is never edited; a revision replaces it (#81).** "Revise" opens the form with the lines the revision would cancel (placed or accepted), to be corrected, and the advice, and asks why (`replacesId` and `replaceReason`). The lines already in progress or completed stay as they are, and the form says how many. The old prescription stays, muted and saying in words which replaced it; the revision says which it revises and why; a cancelled line is struck through and says "Cancelled: reason". The server refuses a second revision of the same one (`409 already_replaced`), said in words.
- **Dosage is written as the doctor writes it (#81):** dose and frequency are plain text up to 50 characters ("500 mg", "1-0-1"), the route one of the API's list, days 1 to 365 and quantity 1 to 10000 as whole numbers typed with digits (an empty box sends nothing). No dose arithmetic or maximum-dose check: the catalog has no dosing data.
- **Allergy and duplicate warnings warn, never refuse (#81).** A line whose medicine's generic name or brand matches a listed allergy, or is the same medicine or generic name as another line or as a line of this encounter still in effect (not cancelled, and not about to be cancelled by the revision), is marked on the line in words with an icon. Writing then asks once, in place ("Prescribe with these warnings?", focus on its heading): "Prescribe anyway" or "Review the lines". The checks are `prescription-checks.ts`, pure and unit-tested. A check that could not be made asks the same question and says so: the allergies not readable (no `allergy:read`, the switch off, a failed read, still loading, or never asked), or prescriptions of the encounter on a page not loaded. "Write a prescription" is not offered while the list failed to load. A generic name followed by more counts as the same medicine ("Insulin" and "Insulin glargine"), which can warn when it need not: it only warns.
- **Allergies are matched by words, as they are free text (#81):** a substance word of four letters or more that begins a word of the medicine's names ("amoxicillin", "sulfa" in "sulfamethoxazole"). It knows no drug classes: "penicillin" does not match amoxicillin; coded allergens and class checks are #380. The allergies come from `useAllergies` of `@/features/triage`, for `allergy:read` with the `triage` switch on; otherwise, or while never asked, the form says so and the check is not made.
- **`matchesAllergy` is exported** from `index.ts` (#100), so the pharmacy warns of an allergy by the same words as prescribing.
- **The medicine search is a `POST /drugs/search`** with the term in the body, medicines only and not archived, as the API takes only those (`unknown_drug` otherwise). A line keeps the medicine's generic name and brand for the checks only; what is shown afterwards is the name the server stored on the order (`itemName`).
- **Prescription templates and favourites are not here (#81):** nothing in the API keeps them; they are a later issue of their own (tables, routes, permissions and screens).
- **A print is recorded before anything prints (#89).** A press sends `POST /prescriptions/:id/print` or `POST /encounters/:id/print` (an audit entry `print`, nothing else, a new idempotency key per press); the print dialog opens only once it is answered, and a refusal is said under the button and prints nothing (`409 not_printable`, one replaced or cancelled meanwhile, in words of its own). A prescription is read afresh before it prints, so it prints as it is now. Leaving the screen aborts the reads; a summary list of more than 20 pages, or one whose cursor comes back, is refused rather than read for ever. The summary is read whole and afresh first (every page of notes, diagnoses and prescriptions the caller may read, in memory only), so the page never leaves out what was not loaded on the screen; it is recorded only once read. The page is drawn by `PrintSheet` and dropped when the dialog closes.
- **What a printed prescription says of who wrote it (#397).** Checked on 2026-10-20: the NMC's Registered Medical Practitioner (Professional Conduct) Regulations, 2023 are in abeyance since August 2023, so the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations, 2002 apply. Their 1.4.1 asks every prescription to show the registration number given by the state medical council or the NMC, and 1.4.2 allows only recognised degrees after the name. So the prescription prints the prescriber's name with the qualifications after it, the registration number with its council, and a signature line under the date written; the summary prints the attending doctor's the same way, and a prescription in it names its prescriber with qualifications and registration number. The letterhead gives the hospital's address and phone (the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules ask for the prescriber's address on a prescription; the hospital's stands for it). Read as understood, not legal advice: a pilot hospital confirms it. The patient's address, which the same rules mention for some medicines, is not printed (a proposed issue).
- **Missing details are said on the page, never refused (#397).** Under the signature: "Not on record, to be written in by hand: …", naming the hospital's address, the doctor's qualifications and the doctor's registration number when missing, as a list in the hospital's language. The council and the phone are not required. The summary of a visit with no doctor names only the address. The doctor's profile says the same (`features/doctors`).
- **The credentials are the profile's now, not when written** (the API's notes say why); the prescription is read afresh before it prints, the summary takes the encounter as the screen has it.
- **Printing is a permission of its own** (`encounter:print`, admin, doctor and nurse by default), so a hospital can take it from a role that still reads the visit. It shows nothing a role could not read on the screen.
- **Lab orders are one order per press (#82),** `POST /encounters/:id/orders` with `type: lab`, a `labTestId` and an idempotency key per body; the laboratory acts on `order.placed`, never on a call. A test or panel already in effect in this consultation (not cancelled or completed, among the rows loaded) is said in words beside the choice, with an icon: it warns, never refuses, as a second sample can be wanted. No confirmation step: unlike a medicine, a repeated test harms nobody.
- **The consultation never moves an order on (#82):** accepting it and starting it are the laboratory's (M8), so `order:update` gives nothing here. The nurse's default (`order:read` and `order:update`) reads the lab orders, and the doctor's also orders and cancels them, as the API grants. A role without `order:read` sees no trace of them.
- **Cancelling is from placed or accepted only, with a reason (#82),** from the version shown, also after the encounter closed but not once cancelled. A cancelled order stays, struck through and saying "Cancelled: reason". A refusal is said in words: `409 conflict` (it changed meanwhile) and `409 invalid_transition` (the laboratory started on it). The form works on the order as the list has it now, which a refusal reads again, so a second press sends the newer version; an order the laboratory started or someone cancelled meanwhile is said so, nothing more can be sent, and focus goes to "Close". An order being written stays (hidden) while a cancellation is asked, so it is not lost.
- **One panel per kind of order, one component (#87).** `OrdersPanel` takes the kind (`lab`, `imaging`, `procedure`, `ORDER_KINDS`); only the search, the words and the item's id field differ (`labTestId` for a test, `serviceId` for a service, `toOrderCreate`). Three short lists, each searching its own catalog, rather than one list with a type to pick: a doctor ordering an X-ray never sees a blood test offered. The strings shared by every kind are under `orders`, a kind's own under `labOrders`, `imagingOrders` and `procedureOrders`; a new kind adds its block and its entry in `ORDER_KINDS`.
- **An imaging or procedure order is a service of that category, not archived (#87),** as the API takes only those (`422 unknown_service`, said in the kind's words). The search needs `service:read` and the `services` switch; without them the box says so and nothing of that kind can be ordered, while the lab search, which needs neither, still works. Radiology (M9) and billing (BE-13) act on `order.placed`; neither exists yet, and the API already emits it. Diet orders have no screen: the issue did not ask for one.
- **Results are the laboratory's panel, not the orders panel's (#92):** the consultation renders `LabResultsPanel` after the order panels, with the encounter and its patient, and the lab orders panel no longer says where results come from. Acknowledging a critical result is there too; the laboratory's notes say how.
- **The test search is a `POST /lab-tests/search`** with the term in the body, tests and panels not archived, as the API orders only those (`422 unknown_lab_test`). It needs `labTest:read` and the `lab_tests` switch; without them the box says so and no test can be ordered.
- **The timeline lists visits, not every record (#83).** Encounter is the spine, so the tab lists the encounters and each opens its consultation, where every record of the visit is behind its own permission. "Filterable by type" is the type of visit (`opd`, `ipd`, `er`, `daycare`; only `opd` is opened yet), sent as `type` to `GET /encounters`, which #83 added to the API. A feed of the notes, diagnoses, prescriptions and orders of every visit in one list needs an endpoint of its own (one request per visit otherwise) and is a later issue. The scope is the server's: a visit outside it is left out, never shown greyed. A `GET`, as the consultations list: nothing typed in it says anything about a patient. The tab reads nothing until opened, and no tab is offered without `encounter:read` or with the switch off.
- **A referral goes to a department of the hospital, or one of its doctors (#88).** The department is required on the form (the API also takes a doctor alone and uses theirs); choosing another department lets go of a doctor of the one before. Without `department:read` or the departments switch the form says so and refers nobody; without `doctor:read` it refers to a department only. Its own record, not an `Order`: nothing is billed or fulfilled by a referral, and the consultation it leads to is an appointment. Never changed: cancelled with a reason, once, struck through and said in words. A referral to an outside hospital, and an inbox of the referrals a department or doctor received, are later issues.
- **The role matrix holds referrals and follow-ups (#88):** admin and doctor all four permissions; the nurse reads follow-ups; the receptionist reads follow-ups and books them, with no way into a consultation.
- **Triage's reading list takes two columns by its container's width (#80),** not the window's (`@container` in `features/triage/components/reading.tsx`), so the summary fits the side column, text at 200% included.

## Mock

`mocks/handlers/clinical.ts` keeps encounters, notes and diagnoses per hospital: at the hospital, an open consultation `enc_h_1` of `pth_00001` with `doc_h_01` (a signed note and a provisional J06.9) and a closed one `enc_h_2` of `pth_00002`; at the clinic, an open `enc_c_1` of `ptc_00001`. It follows the API's rules and codes: tokens of today (`mockQueueToken`), appointments (`mockAppointment`), one encounter per token or appointment, the doctor from the token, the appointment or the caller's own profile (`mockDoctorOfUser`), scopes `all` and `own`, drafts only their author sees, versions, idempotency keys per hospital and user, closing and cancelling refusals, amended once, retracted once, ICD-10 codes (`mockCode`). Prescriptions (#81): `rx_h_1` on `enc_h_1` (paracetamol and cetirizine, with advice), medicines of the drug mock (`mockDrug`: medicines only, not archived), the API's limits, replaced once, the replaced one's lines not started cancelled with the reason, and cancelling an encounter with orders refused. Printing (#89) records each print as the API's audit would, read with `mockPrints`. Credentials (#397) come from the doctors mock (`mockCredentials`): the hospital's doctors have all three but `doc_h_02` (qualifications only), the clinic's a registration number and no council; the hospital's letterhead has every detail, the clinic's no registration number, and tests change it with `setTenantLetterhead`. Tests read it with `mockEncounter`, `mockNotesOf` and `mockPrescriptionsOf`, save a draft behind the screen with `editMockNote`, and move a line on as the pharmacy would with `moveMockOrder`. Lab orders (#82): `ord_h_lab_1`, a routine complete blood count placed on `enc_h_1`, and `ord_h_lab_2`, a haemoglobin placed on the closed `enc_h_2`; the body checked before the encounter, as the API does; listing by type, placing lab, imaging and procedure orders (#87; a diet order is refused), tests of the lab tests mock (`mockOrderableLabTest`: the hospital's, not archived), versions, and cancelling from placed or accepted; read with `mockOrdersOf`, and moved on as the laboratory would with `moveMockOrder`. The encounters list takes `type` (#83; only `opd`), and tests add a closed past visit a day apart with `addPastMockEncounters`. Referrals and follow-ups (#88): none referred at first, the API's checks (a department of the hospital not archived, a practising doctor of it, cancelled once), read with `mockReferralsOf`; the follow-ups are described in the follow-ups notes. Cancelling an encounter with a referral or follow-up that stands is refused. Imaging (#87): `ord_h_img_1`, an urgent chest X-ray (`svc_h_24`) placed on `enc_h_1`; services of the services mock (`mockOrderableService`: the hospital's, of that category, not archived), `422 unknown_service` otherwise. The mock's admin and doctor hold every clinical permission, the clinic's doctor none of the order ones.

## Known gaps

- Walk-ins without a token are not started from the patient's screen: the API takes a walk-in, the web does not offer it yet.
- Allergy checks match names only, not drug classes (#380). No prescription templates or favourites yet (a later issue).
- The printed summary has no vitals or allergies (they are triage's, which exports no printable part). The hospital's address, phone and registration number have no screen yet: the seed sets them (`docs/STATUS.md`). The patient's address is not printed.
- The timeline lists visits only, not each record of them (see Decided); imaging reports come with radiology (M9), and diet orders have no screen; closing does not complete the appointment yet (an appointments subscriber of `encounter.closed`).
- Against the real API, `db:seed` opens no encounter: one is started from a token the doctor calls.
- The doctor filter offers up to 100 practising doctors, as `useDoctorOptions` does.
