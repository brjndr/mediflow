# Duty roster (web): module notes

What was decided for OP-01 (#195). The root `CLAUDE.md` wins where the two disagree. The API is `apps/api/src/features/operations/` (roster part); read its `CLAUDE.md` too.

- **One route, one nav entry** ("Duty roster", `roster:read`, `/roster`) behind the `roster` switch. It runs on the real operations API (`GET /roster/shifts`, `POST /roster/shifts`, `POST /roster/shifts/{id}/cancel`) and on `mocks/handlers/roster.ts`. Works at every width (`DataGrid`).
- **A week at a time** on the hospital's clock (Monday to Sunday, `dayBoundsUtc`), filtered by department when the caller may read departments. Cursor paged, 100 a page.
- **Shifts are added and cancelled, never edited** (API rule). Cancelling asks first and sends the version read. A new shift needs a person found with `StaffSearch` (staff feature, needs `staff:read`): a search on the server (`GET /staff?q=`, active staff, 20 matches), so a hospital of any size works (#594). The chosen name is shown with "Choose someone else"; the form holds only the id.
- **Shift templates** are a fixed table in `schemas.ts` (`SHIFT_TEMPLATES`): choosing the kind fills the usual start and end; an end at or before the start means the next day. They are not a hospital setting yet (needs a settings record in the API).
- **Conflicts are said in words.** The server refuses an overlap (409 `shift_overlap`, shown in the dialog with the choices kept). `rosterConflicts` (`conflicts.ts`) also flags, over the shifts shown, an overlap and less than `MIN_REST_HOURS` (8) of rest between two shifts of one person; both shifts get "Conflict: ..." in the status column and the status line counts them. A rest conflict is a warning, not a refusal.
- **On call in scheduling.** `useOnCallNow(departmentId?)` is exported from `index.ts`: the people whose scheduled `on_call` shift covers now (refreshed each minute; `available` is false without `roster:read` or with the switch off). The roster page shows it as a panel. The booking screen shows it too (#593, `OnCallNote` in appointments), hidden when nothing is on call or the roster is off or closed to the person.
- **Leave requests (#591).** `LeavePanel` under the shifts: upcoming leaves (cursor paged, ends in the future), `Ask for leave` (`roster:requestLeave`, for oneself only; whole days on the hospital's clock, sent as the start of the first day to the end of the last) and Approve, Reject (`roster:manage`, never one's own request: the server says 403 `own_request`) and Withdraw (the owner, or a manager). Approving waits until the person's scheduled shifts in those days are cancelled (409 `shifts_in_leave`, said in words); adding a shift into approved leave is refused (409 `on_leave`, said in the shift dialog). No reason is asked or kept (it can be health information). The leave list is not filtered by department.
- **Not built:** repeating rosters and shift swaps, a hospital's own templates, a day-by-day grid view of the week, coverage rules (minimum staff per department).

- **Approved leave shown where it matters (#668).** `useApprovedLeaves(window)` asks `GET /roster/leaves?status=approved` for a window (one page of 100; only approved leave counts, a request waiting does not). The roster page lists the week's approved leave under the shifts and marks a scheduled shift that overlaps one "On approved leave". `useOnCallNow` drops anyone on approved leave now. `useLeaveOnDay(userId, day)` is exported from `index.ts`: the booking and reschedule forms in appointments show `LeaveNote` when the chosen doctor (by `userId`) is on leave that day. It informs and does not block; hidden without `roster:read` or with the roster off. The mock's approved leave (person 4) now starts 10 hours ago.
