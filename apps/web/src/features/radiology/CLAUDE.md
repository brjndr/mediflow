# Radiology: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**The worklist and scheduling (#96, web and API):** `radiology`, in the navigation after the laboratory, behind the `radiology` switch and `imagingStudy:read`. A `DataGrid` of the hospital's imaging orders not cancelled or completed, at one stage (to schedule, which opens first, scheduled, performed and awaiting report), filtered by modality and priority on the server. To schedule is stat first, then urgent, then routine, the oldest first within each; scheduled is the soonest slot first; performed the longest waiting first. Each row names the patient (the MRN masked), the study as ordered, its modality, its priority (stat and urgent in bold), its slot in words (not scheduled, unscheduled, the slot, or when and by whom it was performed), who ordered it and when, and the doctor's instructions. Works at every width.

- **Schedule** (`imagingStudy:schedule`, to schedule only) and **Reschedule** (scheduled) open a panel in place: the patient, the modality, the priority, the current slot and the instructions, then a day (today to 90 days ahead, a date field) and a start time (a list of the day's 15-minute steps), on the hospital's clock (`zonedToUtc`). It opens on the study's own slot, or the next slot to come.
- **Unschedule** (`imagingStudy:schedule`) and **Mark as performed** (`imagingStudy:perform`) on a scheduled row are row actions with no panel; the status line says what was done or why it was refused.
- **Write report** on a performed row (**View report** without `imagingReport:write`) opens the report panel in place; **View report** on a reported row opens the signed report.

**Reports (#97, web and API):** the report of a performed study, in a panel in place. While it is a draft, `imagingReport:write` writes its technique, findings and impression (three rich text editors), fills the findings from an imaging report template, adds links to images held elsewhere (a label and an https address, at most 10, each with Remove), and saves it (**Save draft**); `imagingReport:sign` signs it (**Sign report**, then **Sign now** in a confirmation that says it can never be changed), which completes the order and moves it to the **Reported** stage, the newest signed first. A signed report shows who signed it and when, its parts with text, its links (opening in a new tab), its addenda oldest first (the reason, who and when, the text), and **Add addendum** for `imagingReport:sign`: the text and a reason from a list, signed as it is added. A role with `imagingStudy:read` alone reads the signed report and changes nothing; a draft is withheld from it (the API answers 404, so the panel says no report is signed yet), as an unverified lab result is. Works at every width.

**Modality in the services catalog:** an imaging service has a modality (X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, fluoroscopy, nuclear medicine, other), chosen on the service's form, which offers it for the category imaging only and sends `null` for none. The worklist filters on it; a service with none is "Other". Notes on that field are in `features/services`.

The endpoints are the radiology API's (`apps/api/src/features/radiology/CLAUDE.md`): `GET /radiology/worklist`, `POST /radiology/orders/{orderId}/schedule`, `/unschedule` and `/perform`, and for reports `GET` and `PUT /radiology/orders/{orderId}/report`, `POST /radiology/orders/{orderId}/report/sign` and `/report/addenda`.

## Decided

- **The switch is `radiology`,** the module flag the tenant config already listed.
- **Permissions follow the "Imaging (perform, report)" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`:** `imagingStudy:read`, `imagingStudy:schedule`, `imagingStudy:perform`, and for reports (#97) `imagingReport:write` (the draft) and `imagingReport:sign` (signing and addenda), all for the radiologist and the admin. Writing and signing are apart so a hospital can let a trainee draft what a consultant signs. Reading a report is `imagingStudy:read`. A doctor has none of these: the consultation shows an imaging order's status (`order:read`); the signed report in the consultation is a follow-up (the matrix's "view results"). The mock gives all five to its admin; the tests build the other roles with `setRolePermissions`.
- **The flow is ordered, scheduled, performed (#96), then reported (#97):** signing completes the order. Scheduling first accepts the order; performing moves it in progress; unscheduling takes it back to the stage to schedule and leaves the order accepted (an order never goes back). Performing needs a slot first: a walk-in X-ray is scheduled for the slot under way, then performed (the API's notes say what changes if a hospital asks otherwise). The doctor may still cancel until it is performed, and the order then leaves the worklist.
- **A slot is a start time, with no room, machine or length (#96).** Two studies may share a slot, and nothing stops one machine being booked twice. Rooms with a check against double booking are a follow-up, as are a day view of the schedule and a study's length.
- **The time is chosen from a list of 15-minute steps,** not typed, so every time offered is one the API takes (422 `invalid_slot` never comes from this form); whether it has passed is the server's to say (422 `outside_window`, "That time has passed"), as the clock decides. The day is checked on the form against today to 90 days ahead at the hospital (`todayIn`), as the date field's limits do not stop a typed day.
- **The day runs to the whole of the 90th day ahead at the hospital,** which is where the API's window ends too. The times are the day's wall-clock steps: in a zone with daylight saving, a skipped hour's time is moved by the conversion and one copy of a repeated hour cannot be chosen. No hospital today is in such a zone; a list built from the day's real instants is the change when one is.
- **The panel shows the date of birth** with the name, to tell the patient apart before the study, as the laboratory's collect panel does; the list does not show it.
- **The worklist is a `GET`** with a stage, a modality, a priority and a cursor: nothing typed in it says anything about a patient. Its answer names patients (name, MRN and date of birth), so the API audits every row read. `total` comes with the first page.
- **Every POST carries an idempotency key,** one per body, so a double press schedules or performs once.
- **Refusals are said in words:** scheduling `conflict` (someone moved it meanwhile), `invalid_transition` (cancelled or performed) and `order_not_found` in the panel, which then offers nothing more to send and moves focus to the alert; `outside_window` and `invalid_slot` in the panel with the form still open for another time. Unscheduling and performing `conflict`, `invalid_transition` and `order_not_found` in the status line.
- **Focus.** The panel's heading takes focus when it opens (`usePanelFocus`), and focus goes back to the button it came from when it closes, or to the status line when that row left the stage. A row action that moves its row on is said in the status line, which takes focus if the button that had it went away.
- **A draft is read only by whoever may write or sign reports (#97),** decided on the security review: unsigned diagnostic text is not shown to a role that only works the worklist. A role that signs and does not write sees the draft read-only and cannot sign it here (a gap, below).
- **The admin signs by default,** as the admin holds every permission of the module (`grantsWithin`); a hospital that wants only radiologists to sign takes it from the admin role.
- **A report is a draft, then signed, then never changed (#97).** One per study, made by its first save. Anyone with `imagingReport:write` edits the draft, not its author alone (a resident drafts, a consultant signs); the draft names who made it and who last saved it, the report who signed it.
- **A draft is saved when Save draft is pressed, not as it is typed,** unlike a clinical note: a report is written in one sitting at the reporting desk. What is typed waits in memory only (never in browser storage, which the unit test and Playwright check), and is lost if the panel is closed unsaved; Sign saves first what was not saved. Once the draft is on screen it stays until signed here: a read that finds it signed by someone else meanwhile says so in the alert, keeps what was typed and sends nothing more. Each save is the whole draft from the version held; a `409 conflict` (someone saved meanwhile) asks, as a note does: "Keep the text here" (saved over the newer version) or "Use the saved text" (the editors show it).
- **Signing asks once, in place,** and checks first what the server would refuse: findings and an impression (`422 report_incomplete`) and no placeholder of a template left in double braces (`422 unfilled_placeholders`). Focus goes to "Sign now", and Back returns it to Sign report.
- **Corrections are addenda, never edits.** A signed report's text and version never change (the API's trigger refuses it); an addendum is text and a reason from a list (correction, additional finding, clarification, comparison with an earlier study, other), never typed, signed by its author as it is added, never changed or removed.
- **Structured templates are the templates module's `imaging_report` kind (#97),** with `study.name` and `study.modality` beside the common placeholders. The picker shows for `template:read` with the `templates` switch on, and only when the hospital has one: **Fill findings** (**Replace findings** once they have text) replaces the findings with the chosen template's newest text, filled in for this study (the patient's name, MRN, age, sex, the study and modality, the hospital, the signed-in user as `doctor.name`, today). A value the screen does not have is left as a placeholder, to be seen and blocking the sign. The report records the template and version it was last filled from; an archived template is offered no more, and the one a draft already names stays accepted. The three parts (technique, findings, impression) are the report's fixed structure; a template fills the findings.
- **Images are links only in this release:** an https address with no user name or password and no parameter named as a secret (a token, a signature, a key, a session; checked on the form and by the API, `400 invalid_link`), since a signed report keeps it for ever, shown as a link that opens in a new tab with `noopener noreferrer`. No upload, no viewer: uploads through signed URLs with a malware scan are #99.
- **The reported stage** lists signed reports, newest first, with no count (the API leaves it out, as it only grows), with who signed and when in the slot column; a performed row with a draft says "report drafted". The worklist is read again after every save, sign and addendum.
- **Focus.** The report panel's heading takes focus when it opens (`usePanelFocus`); after signing, the status line says so; adding an addendum moves focus to its heading, and back to Add addendum on Back, or to the panel's status line once added.
- **Refusals are said in words:** `conflict`, `report_signed`, `invalid_transition` (no longer awaiting a report), `order_not_found`, `invalid_link`, `template_not_found`, `report_incomplete`, `unfilled_placeholders` and `report_not_signed`. After `report_signed`, `invalid_transition` or `order_not_found` nothing more can be sent from the draft (the editors are `inert`) and Close shows the worklist as it is now.

## Known gaps

- The doctor does not see the signed report in the consultation or on the timeline yet (a follow-up issue).
- A report's copy of a template's text is the report's own: a redaction of the template does not reach it (as for the clinical modules).
- No printed report yet, and no image uploads (#99).
- A role that signs but does not write cannot sign from this screen (it sees the draft read-only). No default role is so; a hospital that makes one is the trigger.

## Mock

`mocks/handlers/radiology.ts` works the imaging orders of the clinical mock (`mockImagingOrders`; at the hospital the urgent chest X-ray `ord_h_img_1` of pth_00001) and moves them on with `moveMockOrder`, as the API does through the clinical module. It keeps at most one study per order, takes the modality from the services mock (`mockServiceModality`; the sample imaging services have the dev seed's modalities), and follows the API's rules and codes: the stages and their order, the slot steps and window, versions, idempotency keys per hospital and user. Tests read it with `mockStudies`. The clinic has radiology switched off; a test switches it on. Reports (#97) follow the API's rules and codes (a draft per study, versions, signed never changed, addenda to a signed report only, links, the template check through `isMockReportTemplate` of the templates mock, which has one imaging report template, "Plain radiograph", `tpl_h_xray`); signing completes the order with `moveMockOrder`. Tests read them with `mockReports`.
