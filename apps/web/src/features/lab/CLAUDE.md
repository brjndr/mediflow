# Laboratory: module notes

What this module is and what was decided for it. The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules every module follows, and it wins where the two disagree. Keep this file current with the module.

## What exists

**The worklist and sample collection (web, #90; the API, #95):** `lab`, in the navigation, behind the `laboratory` switch and `labSample:read`. A `DataGrid` of the hospital's lab orders not cancelled, at one stage (to collect, which opens first, collected, received, completed), stat first, then urgent, then routine, the oldest first within each (completed: the newest completed first, #91), and filtered by priority on the server. Each row names the patient (the MRN masked), the test as ordered, its priority (stat and urgent in bold), where its sample is in words (not collected, its accession id and state, or the rejected one and why), who ordered it and when, and the doctor's instructions. Works at every width: a nurse collects at the bedside.

- **Collect sample** (`labSample:collect`, to collect only) opens a panel in place: the patient's name, MRN and date of birth to check against the patient, the sample and its container from the lab test catalog, the priority, the instructions, and why the last sample was rejected. "Mark as collected" sends one request; the answer gives the accession id, and the panel stays, after the row has left the stage, with "Print label".
- **Print label** (`labSample:collect`, on a sample not rejected, from the panel or a row): `POST /lab/samples/:id/print` records the print in the audit log first, then `PrintSheet` prints `SampleLabel` at 50 by 25 mm (its own `@page` size): the patient's name, MRN and date of birth, the accession id as a Code 128 barcode and in words, the test's code, the sample, the container and when it was collected.
- **Receive** (`labSample:receive`, collected only) is a row action with no panel; the status line says what was done or why it was refused.
- **Reject** (`labSample:reject`, collected or received) opens a panel: a reason from a list, and a note that "Other" requires. The order goes back to be collected.

**Results (#91):** `lab/orders/:orderId`, behind `labResult:read`, opened from "Results" on a received or completed row. The page is headed by the patient (the MRN masked), the sample and the order's priority and state. A `DataGrid` has one line per test (the test, or each test of a panel in the catalog's order): the current result with its unit, the API's flag, the patient's range, who entered or verified it and when, and its comment.

- **Enter results** (`labResult:enter`, a received sample of an open order): one field per test not yet resulted, its unit in the label and the range as its hint, with the decimal keyboard for a number; a comment behind "Add a comment". Any of them may be filled in now, and the rest later. One save; the status line says how many were saved, and names a critical one with "Tell the doctor now".
- **Verify** (`labResult:verify`): a box for each current result still entered, ticked by default, its label giving the value, unit and flag; "Verify ticked results" sends their ids. The last one verified completes the order, and the status line says so.
- **Correct** (`labResult:enter`, a row action on a current result, entered or verified): a panel for the whole corrected value, a comment and a required reason. The correction is entered, to verify again; "Corrections" lists every replaced result with who entered and verified it and the reason it was replaced.
- **The doctor** (`labResult:read` alone) sees "Awaiting verification" with no value or flag until it is verified, as the API gives nothing more (#91); no entry, verifying or correcting.

**Reports and result viewing (#92):**

- **Print report** (`labReport:print`: admin, doctor, lab technician) on the results page once a result is verified: the order's results read afresh, `POST /lab/orders/:id/print` records the print in the audit log, and only then `PrintSheet` prints the hospital's letterhead, the patient (the whole MRN, as on paper for the patient), the accession id, the sample with when it was collected and received, each test with its newest verified result, unit, flag in words and range, a corrected one marked so, a comment, a test still to verify as "Pending" (never its value), who verified, and who printed it and when. A refusal (`409 not_printable`) is said under the button and prints nothing.
- **In the consultation:** `LabResultsPanel`, exported, rendered by the clinical module after the order panels: the encounter's released results, the newest collected first, a page of 25 at a time. Each row is the test (a link to its order's results), the value as entered with its unit, the flag, the range, when it was collected, and who verified it (and "Corrected result." for a correction). For `labResult:read` with the switch on; no trace and no request otherwise. The list loads with the consultation, never with the app (`lazy`), as the registry imports the index.
- **On the patient's screen:** a "Lab results" tab (`patient.detail.tabs`, for `labResult:read`): every released result of the patient, as in the consultation, and "Show trend" on a numeric one, which opens the test's trend in place: its newest hundred results, oldest to newest by collection, as `TimeSeriesChart` (a dot per result, `dots`) with the same numbers as a table, and the newest result's range in words. A trend needs two results and says so. Reads nothing until the tab is opened.
- **Acknowledging a critical result** (`labResult:acknowledge`: admin, doctor), from the consultation, the tab or the results page: a verified critical result not yet acknowledged says "Critical, not acknowledged" in words with an icon, the list above it counts them, and "Acknowledge" records it (`POST /lab/results/:id/acknowledge`). Then it says who acknowledged it and when. Without the permission the words stay and the button is not offered.

**Outsourced tests (#94):** "Send to partner lab" (`labSample:outsource`: admin, lab technician) on a received row with no results opens a panel: the partner laboratory by name (required) and its reference (optional). The sample stays received, at the stage "With partner lab", where "Report received" (a row action with no panel, as Receive is) records that the partner's report is back; the row then says so on the received stage, and results are entered from the report and verified as any other. While it is out, the results page says the sample is with the partner and offers no entry. The results page and the printed report say "Tested at" the partner, with its reference.

**Tests (#93):** `result-entry.test.tsx` holds what the entry form takes (`DECIMAL`, text results, an empty entry, a comment with no result), the range in words and the flag as a word and an icon; `e2e/lab.spec.ts` has one test per stage with the keyboard alone, and one journey with the pointer at every width: the doctor orders in the consultation, the sample is collected and received, the result entered and verified, and the doctor sees it, flagged, in the consultation.

The endpoints are the laboratory API's (#95, `apps/api/src/features/lab/CLAUDE.md`): `GET /lab/worklist`, `POST /lab/orders/{orderId}/samples`, `POST /lab/samples/{sampleId}/receive`, `/reject` and `/print`. It also has the results API that result entry (#91) and reports (#92) build against: `GET /lab/orders/{orderId}/results`, `POST /lab/samples/{sampleId}/results` and `/verify`, and `POST /lab/results/{resultId}/amend`, with `labResult:read` (doctor, lab technician, admin), `labResult:enter` and `labResult:verify` (lab technician, admin).

## Decided

- **The switch is `laboratory`,** the module flag the tenant config already listed, not one of its own; `lab_tests` stays the catalog's.
- **Permissions follow the "Lab (collect, result, verify)" row of `docs/PRODUCT.md`:** `labSample:read` and `labSample:collect` for the lab technician, the nurse ("collect") and the admin; `labSample:receive` and `labSample:reject` for the lab technician and the admin. The admin holds all four, as in the clinical module, since nobody invites into a role that holds more than their own. A doctor has none of these: the consultation shows a lab order's status (`order:read`). Results (#91): `labResult:read` for the doctor, the lab technician and the admin, `labResult:enter` and `labResult:verify` for the lab technician and the admin. The API seeds these defaults (#95); the mock gives the sample permissions and `labResult:*` to its admin and `labResult:read` to its doctor, and the tests build the other roles with `setRolePermissions`.
- **One sample per order, and a recollection is a new sample (#90).** A sample is never changed back or removed: a rejected one stays, with its reason, and the order is collected again as a new sample with a new accession id (the API's `already_collected` refuses a second sample while one stands). Several orders in one tube (a blood count and a sugar from one draw) is not done; it would need a sample naming several orders.
- **The accession id is the server's,** unique in the hospital and numbered within the hospital day: `YYMMDD-NNNN` (`261023-0001`). The browser never makes one. It holds no patient detail, so it can be printed, scanned and said aloud.
- **What a sample does to its order.** Collecting accepts an order that was only placed; receiving and rejecting leave it as it is. The order goes in progress when its result is first entered (#91), so a sample rejected at reception never leaves an order in progress with nothing to work on, and the doctor can cancel until a result is started (cancelling is from placed or accepted, `apps/api/src/features/clinical/CLAUDE.md`). A cancelled or completed order leaves the worklist, and collecting, receiving or rejecting a sample of one is refused (`409 invalid_transition`); once results exist, rejecting is refused too (409 `has_results`, #95). The laboratory API moves the order through the clinical module's export, never by writing its table.
- **What the database holds to (#95),** as the mock's checks do: one sample not rejected per order (a partial unique index on `(tenant_id, order_id) where status <> 'rejected'`, the loser of two collections at once gets `409 already_collected`), accession ids unique per hospital (`(tenant_id, accession_id)`), and their numbers from a row-locked counter per hospital and day, never `max() + 1`. A sample copies the order's encounter and patient, so results and the audit reach them without the clinical module's tables. Collecting emits `lab.sample_collected` and rejecting `lab.sample_rejected` (in `docs/EXTENDING.md` and `core/outbox/events.ts`).
- **The worklist is a `GET`** with a stage, a priority and a cursor: nothing typed in it says anything about a patient. Its answer names patients, so the API audits every page read, as for appointments. `total` comes with the first page, as a hospital's open lab orders are few.
- **The date of birth is in the row** (`patientDob`), as a calendar date, for the check before the sample is taken and for the label; the list does not show it.
- **The sample and container are the catalog's when collected,** stored on the sample, so a later change to the test does not change a label already printed. A panel has its own sample and container in the catalog.
- **The barcode is drawn by the module (`code128.ts`), not a library:** Code 128 code set B, about fifty lines with its tests (every symbol eleven modules, the check symbol weighted by position). JsBarcode was the alternative; it brings symbologies the label does not need, and its last release was in 2023.
- **Refusals are said in words:** collecting `already_collected` (someone else collected it meanwhile), `invalid_transition` (cancelled or completed) and `order_not_found`; receiving and rejecting `conflict` (it changed meanwhile, read again), `invalid_transition` and `sample_not_found`; a label `not_printable` (rejected meanwhile) and `sample_not_found`, each map of its own. After a refusal the collect and reject panels offer nothing more to send (what they hold would be refused again) and focus goes to the alert; closing shows the worklist as read again. A print refusal stays until the next print, the panel closing or the stage or priority changing.
- **Each stage offers only its own actions:** to collect, "Collect sample"; collected, "Receive", "Reject" and "Print label"; received, "Results", "Send to partner lab" (#94), "Reject" and "Print label"; with partner lab, "Report received", "Reject" and "Print label"; completed, "Results" (#91). No card carries a button that cannot apply. A received or completed row says how far its results are ("2 entered, 1 to verify", "3 verified").
- **The barcode's module is 0.25 mm,** two dots of a 203 dpi label printer, drawn with crisp edges at a fixed width (an accession id is 176 modules, 44 mm), so every bar prints the same width. Code set C for the digit runs would halve that; not needed at this length.
- `rejectedSamples` (how many samples of the order were rejected) is in the row for result entry and reports (#91, #92); the worklist shows the newest rejection only.
- **Every POST carries an idempotency key,** one per body (collecting: one per order), so a double press collects one sample; a print has a new key per press, as in the clinical module.
- **The flags are the API's, never the browser's (#91).** The page shows the flag the API read against the range the result kept, as a word with an icon, so colour is never the only sign, and the form checks only the number's format (`DECIMAL`, the API's). So the page cannot disagree with the API whatever #310 decides.
- **A result's value is shown as entered** (`5.10` stays `5.10`), not through the number formatter, so what is verified is what was typed; range limits use the hospital's number formatter.
- **Unverified results are never shown to the doctor as final (#91):** the API withholds them (`awaitingVerification`), and the page says "Awaiting verification". A role that enters or verifies sees entered results marked "Not verified".
- **Refusals reread the page.** Entering (`already_resulted`, `unknown_test`, `invalid_transition`, `invalid_number`, `sample_not_found`) and verifying (`conflict`, `unknown_result`, `invalid_transition`, `sample_not_found`) are said in the page's status line (which takes focus when what had it went away), since the page is read again. The entry form stays with the sample: its rows follow the tests still to result and keep what was typed in the others, so nothing given is asked again (WCAG 3.3.7). A message is a new node each time, so the same words said twice are announced twice. Correcting (`already_amended`, `invalid_transition`, `result_not_found`) stays in its panel with no resend, as rejecting does. Any other error stays in its form with what was typed.
- **Results is a page, not a panel,** because a panel can have many tests to fill in, which a panel on a phone could not hold. It needs no `patient:read`: the API's answer names the patient.
- **Focus.** A panel's heading takes focus when it opens (`usePanelFocus`), and focus goes back to the button it came from when it closes, or to the status line when that row left the list. Receiving and rejecting are said in the status line, which takes focus if the button that had it went away.

- **What is released is the API's (#92):** `POST /lab/results/search` with the patient and, for the consultation, the encounter, or for a trend, the test, in the body (together they say what was tested). Of each test of each sample it gives the newest verified result, so a correction still to verify leaves the one it replaces, as the results page shows the doctor. A text result has no trend.
- **A critical value needs acknowledging, once (#92).** Only the verified critical result released can be acknowledged, not one a verified correction replaced (`409 not_acknowledgeable` otherwise), by one person, once (`409 already_acknowledged`, said "Someone acknowledged … meanwhile", and the list read again). A correction is a new result, so a critical correction is acknowledged again. Acknowledging does not stop anything else: the "Tell the doctor now" call stays the laboratory's, and a list of every unacknowledged critical value in the hospital, and escalation when nobody acknowledges, are later issues.
- **The report prints verified results only (#92),** each test's newest verified one, and a test still to verify as pending, so paper never carries a value the laboratory has not released. It is printed from the browser (print CSS), as the prescription is; a PDF made on the server is a later issue if a hospital needs one to send.
- **Printing a report is a permission of its own** (`labReport:print`), as `encounter:print` is, so a hospital can take it from a role that still reads results.
- **"Back to the worklist" is offered with `labSample:read` only:** a doctor reaches the results page from the consultation or the patient's tab and has no worklist.
- **Acknowledging keeps focus where it was:** the button stays, now `aria-disabled`, and the list's status line says what was done.

- **Outsourcing is on the sample, not a record of its own (#94).** A sample goes out at most once: one the partner cannot test is rejected and collected again, as a new sample, as at the hospital's own laboratory. So the partner, its reference, who sent it and when, and who recorded the report back and when, are columns of the sample, set once each. Only a received sample with no results is sent out (409 `has_results`), and no result is entered while it is out (409 `with_partner`, and the database's trigger), so a value is never typed before the partner's report is in hand. Rejecting stays open while it is out (the partner may find it unusable).
- **The partner is typed, not chosen (#94).** A list of partner laboratories (for consistent names, and the partner's cost when billing needs it) and attaching the partner's report as a file (once object storage exists) are later issues; until then the partner's reference is recorded and its values are entered by hand, then verified.
- **Stage "With partner lab" is the API's** (`with_partner`): a received sample sent out and its report not yet back. Its refusals are said in words: sending `conflict`, `invalid_transition`, `has_results` and `sample_not_found` in the panel, with no resend; "Report received" `conflict`, `invalid_transition` and `sample_not_found` in the status line.

## Mock

`mocks/handlers/lab.ts` works the lab orders of the clinical mock (`mockLabOrders`; at the hospital the routine `ord_h_lab_1`, a blood count of pth_00001, and `ord_h_lab_2`, a haemoglobin of pth_00002 ordered earlier) and moves them on with `moveMockOrder`, as the API will through the clinical module. It keeps samples per hospital, numbers accession ids per hospital day at the hospital's timezone, takes the sample and container from the lab tests mock (`mockLabTestSample`) and the date of birth from the patients mock, follows the planned contract's rules and codes (stages, priorities, versions, idempotency keys per hospital and user), and records label prints. It also enters, verifies and corrects results as the API does (#91): the tests from the lab tests mock (`mockLabAnalytes`, with the band for the patient's sex and age on the collection day), the API's decimal format and flags, all or nothing per entry, a correction once per result, completing the order once every test is verified, and the doctor given verified results only. The admin holds `labResult:*` and the doctor `labResult:read`. Reports (#92): the released results search, acknowledging (stored on the result, once, verified critical only) and report prints, read with `mockReportPrints`. Tests release a whole order's results as the laboratory would with `releaseMockResults` (every test verified, the order completed), and give a patient a test more than once with `addMockLabOrder` from the clinical mock. The admin and the doctor hold `labResult:acknowledge` and `labReport:print`. Tests read it with `mockSamplesOf`, `mockResultsOf` and `mockLabelPrints`. Outsourcing (#94): sending out and the report back, with the API's rules and codes; the admin holds `labSample:outsource`. The clinic has the laboratory switched off; a test switches it on.
