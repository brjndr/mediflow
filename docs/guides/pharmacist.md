# Pharmacist quick guide

## 1. Prescription queue

Open **Pharmacy**. Signed prescriptions wait here. Open one.

## 2. Dispense

1. Read the **allergy warning** if shown. Stop and call the doctor if unsure.
2. The app picks batches nearest expiry first. If the drug is out of stock you may offer a generic substitute.
3. Dispense in full or in part (the rest stays open). Press **Dispense**, print the dispense slip.
4. Stock moves once, through the stock ledger. Never adjust stock to hide a mistake.

## 3. Counter sale

Open **Sales**, then **New sale**. Scheduled drugs (H1, X, narcotic, controlled) need an outside prescription and a registered patient. Press **Record sale** and print the receipt.

## 4. Returns

Open the dispense or sale and press **Record return**: back to stock if sealed and in date, or wastage with a reason.

## 5. The drug register

Open **Register** for every movement of scheduled drugs. It is read from the ledger and cannot be edited.

## 6. Stock and expiry

Open **Inventory**: low stock and expiring batches are flagged. Ask for an adjustment with a reason; a second person approves it. Write off expired stock from the stock list.

## 7. Ordering

Purchase orders are raised by the store keeper and approved by the admin.

## Never

Sell a scheduled drug without the prescription, or dispense expired stock.
