# Nurse quick guide

## 1. Triage in OPD

1. Open **Queue** and the patient's token, then triage.
2. Record vital signs (the app marks values outside the range for the patient's age), chief complaint and **allergies**. Allergies you record are checked when the doctor prescribes.

## 2. Ward worklist

Open **Nursing**. It lists the admitted patients of your ward and what is due: vitals, medicines, rounds.

## 3. Chart vital signs

Open the patient's **Vitals**, enter the readings, save. Out-of-range readings are flagged with a word and an icon, not colour alone.

## 4. Give medicines (medication chart)

1. Open the patient's medication chart.
2. For each due dose, record given, held or refused, with a reason when not given. Entries are never deleted: a mistake is corrected by a new entry.

## 5. Fluids, notes and care plan

Record intake and output (totals are shown for each shift), nursing notes and care plan tasks. Diet orders and the kitchen list are on the diet screen.

## 6. Ask for ward stock

Raise an **indent** for stock from the store. The store keeper issues it after approval.

## 7. Beds

The bed board shows free, occupied and cleaning beds. Transfers and discharges are done by the admit desk or the doctor; you give your nursing clearance.

## Never

Chart a reading you did not take, or for a patient you did not see.
