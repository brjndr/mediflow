# Receptionist quick guide

Sign in with your email and password. If your hospital uses the authenticator, type the six-digit code too. Sign out when you leave the desk.

## 1. Register a new patient

1. Open **Patients**. Search first by phone or name so you do not make a second record.
2. Choose the new patient button, fill the form (name, date of birth, gender, phone) and press **Register patient**.
3. If the app shows possible duplicates, open each one before you go on. Two records for one person are hard to undo.
4. Tick the consent box only if the patient agreed to be contacted. Nothing is sent to patients today.

## 2. Book an appointment

1. Open **Appointments**, then **New appointment**.
2. Find the patient, pick the doctor and a free time on the slot picker, then press **Book**.
3. To change it later, open the appointment: move it, cancel it, or mark it no-show. Say why when asked.

## 3. Walk-in: issue a token

1. Open **Queue**, then **New token**. Find the patient, pick the department or doctor and press **Issue token**.
2. Give the patient the number. The waiting-room board shows numbers and the doctor, never names.

## 4. Take a payment

1. Open **Billing** and find the patient's open invoice, or start **New invoice** from the visit's charges.
2. Open the invoice and press **Record payment**: amount, mode (cash, card, UPI), reference.
3. Check the balance reads zero. Print the invoice for the patient.
4. Cash: open your register at the start of the shift and close it at the end (see the billing clerk guide).

## 5. Fix a mistake

- Wrong phone or spelling: open the patient, **Edit**, **Save changes**.
- Wrong payment: never edit it. Ask your admin or billing clerk for a refund or credit note.
- Two records for one person: tell the admin. Only they merge.

## Never

Share your password. Read out a patient's details to someone who is not the patient or their carer. Leave a patient screen open and walk away.
