# Billing clerk quick guide

The billing clerk is a custom role the admin makes (see the admin guide). It needs the billing and cash register permissions.

## 1. Open your register

Open **Billing**, then **Register**, then **Open register**. Count the cash you start with and enter it. Cash payments are tied to your drawer.

## 2. Build and issue an invoice

1. **New invoice**: pick the patient and visit. Pending charges (consultation, lab, pharmacy, procedures) are listed. Tick those to bill and add typed lines if needed.
2. Tax comes from the service's tax class. A discount above your hospital's limit needs approval by a second person.
3. Save as a draft, check it, then issue. **An issued invoice cannot be edited.** It has a number by fiscal year.

## 3. Take payment

On the invoice press **Record payment**: amount, mode, reference. Part payments are fine.

## 4. Corrections

- Billed too much or wrong item: **Issue credit note** with a reason.
- Money back: refund a payment from the invoice.
- Both leave the original untouched and are recorded in the audit log.

## 5. Packages

Apply a package to a visit to replace the charges it includes with a single charge.

## 6. Insurance and corporate patients

Look at the approved limit on the new-invoice screen and warn the family when the bill nears it. Corporate bills go on the corporate account.

## 7. Close the day

Press **Close and settle** on your register: count the cash, enter it, note any difference and print the handover. Another person can see every session.

## Never

Change a payment after the fact, or close the register without counting.
