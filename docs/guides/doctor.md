# Doctor quick guide

## 1. Start the day

Open **Queue**. Your waiting tokens are first. Call the next token: the board shows the number and your name. **Appointments** lists booked patients.

## 2. See the patient

1. Start the consultation from the token or appointment. This opens an encounter for the patient.
2. Read the triage summary at the top: vital signs, chief complaint and **allergies**. Check allergies before you prescribe.
3. Write the note. It saves itself as a draft on the server. Sign it when done. A signed note cannot be changed: you add an amendment with a reason.
4. Add diagnoses, searching by name or code.

## 3. Prescribe and order

- **Prescription:** search the drug, set dose, route, frequency and days. Use **Load a set** for your common courses and **Save as a set** to keep one. The allergy check warns by drug class.
- **Lab, imaging, procedure:** place the order from the encounter. The result returns into the same screen. A critical result needs your acknowledgement.
- Results appear only once the lab has verified them.

## 4. Refer or follow up

Add a referral or a follow-up with a date. The follow-up list shows who is due.

## 5. Print

Print the prescription from the encounter. The patient takes it to the pharmacy.

## 6. Look back

Open the patient and the **Timeline** tab for earlier visits, results and notes.

## Never

Sign a note under a colleague's login or lend your own. Every read and write is recorded against you.
