# One-hour training session, by role

Same shape for every role, in the practice hospital ([practice-hospital.md](practice-hospital.md)). Groups of up to six, one trainer, one screen each. Keep the guide on the desk.

| Minutes  | What                                                                                         |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0 to 10  | Sign in, the authenticator, the home page, the menu, sign out. Why every action is recorded. |
| 10 to 40 | The role's tasks below, each done by every person, not just shown.                           |
| 40 to 50 | A short practice day: the trainer plays a patient and the group handles them end to end.     |
| 50 to 60 | Mistakes and who to ask. Fill in the "Rough edges" line. Questions.                          |

## Tasks for the middle half hour

- **Receptionist:** register (with the duplicate check), book, move and cancel an appointment, issue a token, take a cash payment, ask for a refund of a wrong payment.
- **Doctor:** call a token, read triage and allergies, write and sign a note, prescribe with a set, order a lab test, read the verified result, add a follow-up, print.
- **Nurse:** triage a token, chart vitals and see a flagged value, give a dose and record a refused one, record fluids, raise an indent.
- **Lab technician:** collect and label a sample, reject one, enter results including a critical one, verify, correct a result, print the report.
- **Pharmacist:** open a prescription, meet the allergy warning, dispense in part, substitute a generic, make a counter sale, return to stock, read the drug register.
- **Billing clerk:** open the register, build an invoice from pending charges, apply a discount that needs approval, part-pay, issue a credit note, close and settle.
- **Hospital admin:** the day-one checklist: invite a staff member, edit a role, import a small file and fix a rejected row, approve a stock adjustment.

## After the session

Each person signs in once more on their own and does their first task from the guide alone. If they cannot, the guide is wrong: fix the guide.
