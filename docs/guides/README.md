| [Lab technician](lab-technician.md)           | Samples, results, verification                       |

## Rules for these guides

- They describe screens as they are in the app. If a screen differs, the app is right: tell the admin, who files it.
- Screenshots are not committed yet. When added, take them only in the seeded sample hospital (see the practice hospital page) and never from a real hospital. Every sample patient is invented.
- What you can see depends on your role. A button or menu entry you do not have is not an error: your hospital admin decides who may do what.
- "Billing clerk" is not a built-in role. The admin creates it from the Roles screen (see the admin guide) or gives the work to the receptionist role.
- Where a step is awkward in the screen, write it on the "Rough edges" line at the bottom of your guide and give it to the admin. Each becomes its own issue.
